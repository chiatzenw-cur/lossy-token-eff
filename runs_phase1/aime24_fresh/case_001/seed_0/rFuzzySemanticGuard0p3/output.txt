* \(s\) = Aya’s walking pace in km h\(^{-1}\) (first scenario)  
* \(t\) = minutes spent in the coffee shop  

For a 9‑km walk the walking time is \( \frac{9}{s}\) hours.  
The total time is the walking time plus the coffee time, so

\[
\frac{9}{s}+\frac{t}{60}=4\ \text{hours}\qquad\text{(1)}
\]

When she walks \(s+2\) km h\(^{-1}\),

\[
\frac{9}{s+2}\!+\frac{t}{60}=2{\rm h}\,24{\rm min}=2.4\ \text{hours}\qquad\text{(2)}
\]

---

### Solve for \(s\) and \(t\)

Subtract:

\[
\frac{9}{s}-\frac{9}{s+2}=4-\!2.4=2.4-2.4=1.6
\]

\[
\frac{18}{s(s+2)}=\;1.6
\]

\[
s(s+2)=\frac{18}{1.6}=11.25
\]

Hence  

\[
s^2+2s-11.25=0\qquad\Longrightarrow\qquad s=\frac{-2+\sqrt{49}}{2}=2.5\ \text{km/h}
\]

Plug into (1):

\[
\frac{9}{2.5}+\frac{t}{60}=4
\;\Longrightarrow\;3.6+\frac{t}{60}=4
\;\Longrightarrow\;\frac{t}{60}=0.4\;\Longrightarrow\;t=24\ \text{minutes}
\]

---

### Walking at \(s+ \tfrac12\) km h\(^{-1}\)

Now \(s+ \tfrac12 = 2.5+0.5=3.0\) km h\(^{-1}\).

Walking distance \(=9\) km, in \( \frac{9}{3}=3\) hours.  
Add the coffee time:

\[
3\text{ h} + 24\text{ min} = 3\text{ h }24\text{ min}
\]

Converting minutes:

\[
3\text{ h} = 180\text{ min}
\quad\Rightarrow\quad 180+24=204\text{ min}
