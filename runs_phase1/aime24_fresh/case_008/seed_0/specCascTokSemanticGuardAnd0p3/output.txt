### 1.  Rewrite the logarithmic equations

\[
\begin{aligned}
\log_x(y^x)=10 &\Longrightarrow y^x = x^{10}\quad\text{(1)}\\ 
\log_y(x^{4y})=10 &\Longrightarrow x^{4y}=y^{10}\quad\text{(2)}
\end{aligned}
\]

Take natural logarithms of both sides of each equation:

\[
\begin{aligned}
x\,\ln y &= 10\,\ln x \quad\text{(1$'$)}\\
4y\,\ln x &= 10\,\ln y \quad\text{(2$'$)}
\end{aligned}
\]

Let  

\[
A=\ln x,\qquad B=\ln y .
\]

Then \(x=e^{A}\) and \(y=e^{B}\).  
Equations (1$'$) and (2$'$) become

\[
\begin{cases}
e^{A}B = 10A \quad &(3)\\[4pt]
4\,e^{B}A = 10B \quad &(4)
\end{cases}
\]

---

### 2.  Express \(B\) in terms of \(A\)

From (3):

\[
B =\frac{10A}{e^{A}} .
\tag{5}
\]

---

### 3.  Substitute \(B\) into (4)

Insert (5) into (4):

\[
4\,e^{\, \frac{10A}{e^{A}}}\,A = 10\,\frac{10A}{e^{A}}
   \;\Longrightarrow\;
4 e^{\, \frac{10A}{e^{A}}} = \frac{100}{e^{A}} .
\]

Multiply by \(e^{A}\):

\[
4 e^{\, \frac{10A}{e^{A}} + A}=100
   \;\Longrightarrow\;
e^{\, \frac{10A}{e^{A}} + A}=25 .
\]

Taking natural logs gives

\[
\frac{10A}{e^{A}} + A = \ln 25 .
\tag{6}
\]

But by (5) we have \(B=\dfrac{10A}{e^{A}}\).  
Hence (6) is

\[
A + B = \ln 25 .
\]

### 4.  Compute \(xy\)

Since \(x=e^{A}\) and \(y=e^{B}\),

\[
xy = e^{A}\,e^{B}=e^{\,A+B}=e^{\ln 25}=25 .
