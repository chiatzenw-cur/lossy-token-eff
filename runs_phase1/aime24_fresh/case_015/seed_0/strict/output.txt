\[
A=u,\qquad B=v,\qquad C=-u,\qquad D=-v ,
\]

so the intersection point of the diagonals (the centre of the rhombus) is the origin.
Since \(ABCD\) is a rhombus we have  
\(|AB|=|BC|\), thus

\[
|u-v|=|u+v|\Longrightarrow (u-v)^2=(u+v)^2\Longrightarrow u\!\cdot\!v=0 .
\]

Hence the two diagonal vectors \(u\) and \(v\) are orthogonal.

The hyperbola is  

\[
\frac{x^{2}}{20}-\frac{y^{2}}{24}=1 .
\]

A convenient parametrisation is  

\[
u=(\sqrt{20}\cosh t_{1},\,\sqrt{24}\sinh t_{1}),\qquad  
v=(\sqrt{20}\cosh t_{2},\,\sqrt{24}\sinh t_{2}),
\]
with \(t_{1},t_{2}\in\mathbb R\).

---

### 1. Condition of orthogonality

\[
u\cdot v=0\Longrightarrow 
20\cosh t_{1}\cosh t_{2}+24\sinh t_{1}\sinh t_{2}=0 .
\]

Divide by \(4\) and put  
\[
a=\cosh t_{2},\qquad b=\sinh t_{2}\quad (a^{2}-b^{2}=1),
\]
to obtain

\[
5a\cosh t_{1}+6b\sinh t_{1}=0 \quad\Longrightarrow\quad
\tanh t_{1}= -\,\frac{5a}{6b}.                                   \tag{1}
\]

A real \(t_{1}\) exists iff \(|\tanh t_{1}|<1\); thus

\[
\Bigl|\frac{5a}{6b}\Bigr|<1\;\Longleftrightarrow\; b>\frac{5}{6}a .
\tag{2}
\]

Using \(a^{2}-b^{2}=1\), inequality (2) gives  

\[
a^{2}-1>\frac{25}{36}\,a^{2}\;\Longleftrightarrow\;
a^{2}>\frac{36}{11}\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
a>\frac{6}{\sqrt{11}} .
\]

The right–hand side is the minimal possible value of \(a=\cosh t_{2}\);
the limit \(a\to \frac{6}{\sqrt{11}}\) corresponds to \(t_{1}\to-\infty\)
and is not attained by a finite \(t_{1}\).  
Thus the quantity \(|v|^{2}\) can be made arbitrarily close to, but never equal
to, the value obtained at this limit.

---

### 2. Value of \(|v|^{2}\)

With \(a^{2}=\dfrac{36}{11}\) we obtain from \(a^{2}-b^{2}=1\)

\[
b^{2}=\frac{25}{11}.
\]

Hence

\[
|v|^{2}
   =(\sqrt{20}\,a)^{2}+(\sqrt{24}\,b)^{2}
   =20a^{2}+24b^{2}
   =20\!\left(\frac{36}{11}\right)+24\!\left(\frac{25}{11}\right)
   =\frac{720+600}{11}=120 .
\]

Therefore

\[
BD^{2}=|B-D|^{2}
      =(2\,\!x_{v})^{2}+(2\,y_{v})^{2}
      =4|v|^{2}=4\cdot120=480 .
\]

Because the equality case corresponds to an unbounded \(t_{1}\), the value
\(BD^{2}=480\) is **not actually attained** by any rhombus; all such rhombi
satisfy \(BD^{2}>480\).

\[
BD^{2}>480 .
\]
