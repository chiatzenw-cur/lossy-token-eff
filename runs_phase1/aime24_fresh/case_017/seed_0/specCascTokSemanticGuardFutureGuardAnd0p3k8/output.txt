### 1.  Using \(IA\perp OI\)

For any triangle

\[
OI^2=R^{2}-2Rr .
\]

Since \(IA\perp OI\),

\[
OA^2=OI^2+IA^2\quad\Longrightarrow\quad R^2=OI^2+IA^2 .
\]

Hence  

\[
IA^2=R^2-OI^2=R^2-(R^2-2Rr)=2Rr.
\]

With \(R=13,\; r=6\),

\[
IA^2=2(13)(6)=156.
\]

---

### 2.  Express \(IA^2\) in terms of the sides

In a triangle

\[
IA^2=r^2+(s-a)^2 .
\]

Thus  

\[
r^2+(s-a)^2=156,\qquad r=6\Rightarrow 36+(s-a)^2=156
\]

\[
\Longrightarrow\quad (s-a)^2=120\quad\Longrightarrow\quad s-a=2\sqrt{30}.
\]

So  

\[
s=a+2\sqrt{30}\quad\text{and}\quad b+c = a+4\sqrt{30}. \tag{1}
\]

---

### 3.  Heron’s formula

\[
\Delta^{2}=s(s-a)(s-b)(s-c).
\]

But \(\Delta = rs = 6s\), so

\[
36s^2 = s(s-a)(s-b)(s-c)
\]
\[
\Longrightarrow\quad 36s=(s-a)(s-b)(s-c)=2\sqrt{30}\,(s-b)(s-c).
\]

Let  

\[
x=s-b,\qquad y=s-c .
\]

Then  

\[
x+y = s-(b+c)=s-(a+4\sqrt{30})=a-2\sqrt{30},
\]
\[
xy =\frac{36s}{2\sqrt{30}} = \frac{18s}{\sqrt{30}} .
\]

The product \(bc\) is

\[
bc=(s-x)(s-y)=s^2-s(x+y)+xy
=s^2-s(a-2\sqrt{30})+xy
=2\sqrt{30}s+xy .
\]

Substituting \(xy\):

\[
bc=2\sqrt{30}s+\frac{18s}{\sqrt{30}}
=s\!\left(2\sqrt{30}+\frac{18}{\sqrt{30}}\right)
=\frac{13}{5}s\sqrt{30}. \tag{2}
\]

### 4.  Express \(bc\) by \(a\) and \(s\)

From the circumradius formula

\[
R=\frac{abc}{4\Delta}\quad\Longrightarrow\quad
13=\frac{abc}{4(6s)}=\frac{abc}{24s}
\]
\[
\Longrightarrow\quad abc=312s .
\]

Thus

\[
bc=\frac{312s}{a}. \tag{3}
\]

\[
\frac{312s}{a}=\frac{13}{5}s\sqrt{30}

\]

s=a+2\sqrt{30}=4\sqrt{30}+2\sqrt{30}=6\sqrt{30}.

\[
bc=\frac{312s}{a}
