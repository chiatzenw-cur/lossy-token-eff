### 1.  Distance \(OI\)

The well‑known relation  

\[
OI^{2}=R(R-2r)
\]

gives  

\[
OI^{2}=13(13-12)=13\quad\Longrightarrow\quad OI=\sqrt{13}.
\]

--------------------------------------------------------------------
### 2.  Length \(AI\)

The condition \(IA\perp OI\) means that \(\triangle AIO\) is right at \(I\).  
Hence  

\[
AO^{2}=AI^{2}+OI^{2}\quad\Longrightarrow\quad 13^{2}=AI^{2}+13
\]

and  

\[
AI^{2}=169-13=156,\qquad AI=\sqrt{156}.
\]

--------------------------------------------------------------------
### 3.  Angle \(A\)

In a triangle, the distance from the incenter to a vertex is  

\[
AI=\frac{r}{\sin\frac{A}{2}}
\]

so

\[
\frac{6}{\sin\frac{A}{2}}=\sqrt{156}\quad\Longrightarrow\quad 
\sin^{2}\frac{A}{2}=\frac{36}{156}=\frac{3}{13}.
\]

Thus  

\[
\sin\frac{A}{2}=\sqrt{\frac{3}{13}},\qquad 
\cos\frac{A}{2}=\sqrt{\frac{10}{13}}.
\]

--------------------------------------------------------------------
### 4.  Use of the inradius formula

\[
r=4R\sin\frac{A}{2}\sin\frac{B}{2}\sin\frac{C}{2}
\]

\[
6=4\cdot13\left(\sqrt{\frac{3}{13}}\right)\sin\frac{B}{2}\sin\frac{C}{2}
\]

\[
\sin\frac{B}{2}\sin\frac{C}{2}
=\frac{6}{52\sqrt{\frac{3}{13}}}
=\frac{\sqrt{39}}{26}.
\]

### 5.  Compute \(\sin B\sin C\)

Let  

\[
u=\frac{B}{2},\; v=\frac{C}{2},\; w=\frac{A}{2}\;(u+v+w=\tfrac{\pi}{2}).
\]

Denote  

\[
M=\sin u\sin v=\frac{\sqrt{39}}{26}, \qquad
\sin w=\sqrt{\frac{3}{13}}=\frac{\sqrt{39}}{13}=2M.
\]

Using

\[
\sin u\sin v=\frac{\cos(u-v)-\cos(u+v)}{2},
\qquad
\cos u\cos v=\frac{\cos(u-v)+\cos(u+v)}{2},
\]

and \(\cos(u+v)=\sin w\), we obtain  

\[
\cos u\cos v=M+\sin w.
\]

Therefore  

\[
\sin B\sin C=4\sin u\sin v\cos u\cos v
=4M(M+\sin w)=4M(3M)=12M^{2}
=3\sin^{2}w.
\]

Since \(\sin w=\sin\frac{A}{2}\),

\[
\sin B\sin C=3\sin^{2}\frac{A}{2}
=3\cdot\frac{3}{13}=\frac{9}{13}.
\]

### 6.  Find \(AB\cdot AC\)

With \(b=AC=2R\sin B,\; c=AB=2R\sin C\),

\[
AB\cdot AC = bc = (2R)^{2}\sin B\sin C
=4R^{2}\sin B\sin C
