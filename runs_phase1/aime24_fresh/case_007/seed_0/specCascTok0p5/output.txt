\[
\begin{cases}
ab+bc+ca=27\\[2mm]
abc=23
\end{cases}
\]

The smallest sphere that can contain a rectangular box has its centre at the centre of the box, and its radius is

\[
r=\sqrt{\left(\frac{a}{2}\right)^2+\left(\frac{b}{2}\right)^2+\left(\frac{c}{2}\right)^2}
= \frac{\sqrt{a^2+b^2+c^2}}{2}.
\]

To find the value of \(r\) that works for all boxes in \(\mathcal{B}\), we must maximise
\(a^2+b^2+c^2\) subject to the given constraints.

---

### 1.  Express \(a^2+b^2+c^2\) in terms of \(a+b+c\)

Let \(T=a+b+c\). Then

\[
a^2+b^2+c^2 = T^2-2(ab+bc+ca)=T^2-54.
\]

Hence we need to maximise \(T\).

---

### 2.  Use Lagrange multipliers

We maximise \(T=a+b+c\) with the constraints

\[
g_1=ab+bc+ca-27=0,\qquad 
g_2=abc-23=0.
\]

Setting \(\nabla T=\lambda\nabla g_1+\mu\nabla g_2\) gives

\[
1=\lambda(b+c)+\mu bc,\qquad
1=\lambda(c+a)+\mu ca,\qquad
1=\lambda(a+b)+\mu ab.
\]

Subtracting the first two equations yields

\[
(b-a)\bigl(\lambda+\mu c\bigr)=0,
\]

and similarly for the other pairs.  
Thus either all of \(a,b,c\) are equal (which is impossible, because \(abc=23\not=27\)), or exactly two of them are equal.  
Take \(a=b=x\) and \(c=y\).

---

### 3.  Solve the system for \(x\) and \(y\)

From \(x^2y=23\Rightarrow y=\dfrac{23}{x^2}\).  
Insert into \(x^2+2xy=27\):

\[
x^2+2x\left(\frac{23}{x^2}\right)=27
\Longrightarrow x^3-27x+46=0.
\]

This cubic factors as

\[
(x-2)(x^2+2x-23)=0,
\]

so the positive solutions are

\[
x=2,\qquad x=-1+2\sqrt6\approx3.898.
\]

Corresponding \(y\) values are

\[
y=\frac{23}{x^2}\quad\Longrightarrow\quad
y=5.75\quad\text{or}\quad y\approx1.514.
\]

---

### 4.  Find the maximum \(T\)

\[
T=2x+y.
\]

For \(x=2\): \(T=4+5.75=9.75\).  
For \(x\approx3.898\): \(T\approx9.31\).

Thus the maximum occurs when \((a,b,c)=(2,2,5.75)\).

---

### 5.  Compute \(r^2\)

\[
a^2+b^2+c^2=2^2+2^2+5.75^2=4+4+33.0625=41.0625.
\]

\[
r^2=\frac{a^2+b^2+c^2}{4}=\frac{41.0625}{4}=10.265625.
\]

Express as a fraction:

\[
10.265625=\frac{657}{64}\qquad(\text{since }10\times64+17=657).
\]

Thus \(r^2=\dfrac{657}{64}\) and \(p=657,\;q=64\).

\[
p+q=657+64=721.
