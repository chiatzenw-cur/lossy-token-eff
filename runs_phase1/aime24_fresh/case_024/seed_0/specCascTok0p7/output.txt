### 2.  Express the 3‑digit sum in terms of column sums

Let  

\[
T_1=a+d,\qquad T_2=b+e,\qquad T_3=c+f
\]

Then the 3‑digit sum equation becomes  

\[
100T_1+10T_2+T_3=999\tag{1}
\]

Since \(T_1,T_2,T_3\le 18\) (sums of two digits), the only way to obtain the right‑hand side of

\[
T_1=9,\; T_2=9,\; T_3=9 .
\]

Thus

\[
a+d=9,\quad b+e=9,\quad c+f=9\tag{2}
\]

### 3.  Use the column‑sum condition

\[
