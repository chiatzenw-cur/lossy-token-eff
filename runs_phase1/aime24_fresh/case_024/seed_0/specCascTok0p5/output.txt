### 1.  Conditions on the row sums  

Reading left‑to‑right gives the two 3‑digit numbers  

\[
X=100a+10b+c,\qquad Y=100d+10e+f .

The problem states  

\[
X+Y=999 \quad\Longrightarrow\quad 100(a+d)+10(b+e)+(c+f)=999 .
\tag{1}
\]

Write  

\[
T_1=a+d,\qquad 
T_2=b+e,\qquad 
T_3=c+f .
\]

Equation (1) becomes  

\[
100T_1+10T_2+T_3=999 .
\tag{2}
\]

Because \(0\le T_i\le 18\), the only way to represent \(999\) in this way is
\[
T_1=9,\;T_2=9,\;T_3=9 .
\]

Thus  

\[
a+d=9,\qquad b+e=9,\qquad c+f=9 .
\tag{3}
\]

### 2.  Conditions on the column sums  

Reading top‑to‑bottom gives the three 2‑digit numbers  

\[
N_1=10a+d,\qquad N_2=10b+e,\qquad N_3=10c+f .

N_1+N_2+N_3=99 .

\[
10a+d+10b+e+10c+f
