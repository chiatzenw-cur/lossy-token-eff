Let

### 2.  Determine possible values of \(X,Y\) and \(r+s+t\)

From (3) and \(X+Y=r+s+t\) we have  

\[
X+Y = r+s+t,\qquad 10X+Y = 99 .
\]

Eliminate \(Y\):

\[
10X + (r+s+t-X)=99\implies 9X = 99-(r+s+t).
\]

\]

\[

### 3.  Solve for \(r,s,t\)

**Case \(r+s+t=18\).**

\[
100r+10s+t=999,\qquad t=18-r-s .
\]

\]

\[
99r+9s=972\;\Longrightarrow\; 11r+s=108 .
