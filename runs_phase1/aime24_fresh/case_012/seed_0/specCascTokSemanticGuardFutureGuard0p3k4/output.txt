If the path changes direction exactly \(4\) times, the sequence of moves is divided into  

\[
4+1 = 5
\]

consecutive blocks of equal direction.  
The blocks must alternate (right ↔ up ↔ right ↔ up ↔ right or the opposite).

---

### 1.  \(R\!-\!U\!-\!R\!-\!U\!-\!R\) pattern  

Let  
\[
r_1+r_3+r_5 = 8 \qquad (\text{right moves}),\qquad  
u_2+u_4 = 8 \qquad (\text{up moves}),
\]
with all \(r_i,u_j \ge 1\).

Number of positive integer solutions:  

\[
\#(r_1,r_3,r_5)=\binom{8-1}{3-1}=\binom{7}{2}=21,

\[
\#(u_2,u_4)=\binom{8-1}{2-1}=\binom{7}{1}=7.
\]

Hence  
\[
N_{RURUR}=21\times 7=147.
