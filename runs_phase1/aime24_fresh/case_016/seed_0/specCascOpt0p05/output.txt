* \(v\): number of residents who have \(G\) and \(S\) only  

* \(t\): number of residents who have \(D\) and \(G\) and \(S\) (what we want)

The problem gives

\[
\begin{aligned}
x+y+z & = 437 &&\text{(exactly one of the four items)}\\[2mm]
w+u+v & = 234 &&\text{(exactly two of the four items)}\\[2mm]
t &=  \text{(exactly three of the four items)}\\[2mm]
\end{aligned}
\]

The total number of residents who possess each set is

\[
\begin{aligned}
d &= x+w+u+t = 195,\\
g &= y+w+v+t = 367,\\
s &= z+u+v+t = 562.
\end{aligned}
\]

Add the three mutually exclusive equations for \(d,g,s\):

\[
(x+w+u)+(y+w+v)+(z+u+v)=195+367+562-3t.\tag{1}
\]

Simplify the left–hand side:

\[
(x+y+z)+(w+u+v)+(w+u+v)=437+468=905 .
\]

\[
905 = 1124-3t \quad\Longrightarrow\quad 219 = 3t
