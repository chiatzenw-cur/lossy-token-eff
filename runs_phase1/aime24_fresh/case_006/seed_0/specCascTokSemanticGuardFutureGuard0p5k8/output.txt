V^2=\frac{1}{288}\bigl(1\,167\,680-671\,744-200\,000-91\,136+204\,800\bigr)
      =\frac{12\,800}{9},
\]
so

\[
V=\frac{80\sqrt{2}}{3}.
\]

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

### 2.  Areas of the faces

All faces are congruent; for the triangle with side–squares \(p,q,r\) the area is

\[
A^2=\frac{1}{16}\Bigl(2(pq+qr+rp) -(p^2+q^2+r^2)\Bigr).
\]

For \(p=41,\;q=80,\;r=89\):

\[
A^2=\frac{1}{16}\Bigl(2(41\cdot80+80\cdot89+89\cdot41)-(41^2+80^2+89^2)\Bigr)
      =\frac{12\,096}{16}=756.
\]

Thus

\[
A=\sqrt{756}=6\sqrt{21}.
\]

Each of the four faces has this area, so

\[
S=4A=24\sqrt{21}.
\]

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

### 3.  Inradius

For a tetrahedron, the inradius \(r\) satisfies  

\[
V=\frac{1}{3}rS \quad\Longrightarrow\quad
r=\frac{3V}{S}.
\]

Hence

\[
r=\frac{3\!\left(\dfrac{80\sqrt{2}}{3}\right)}{24\sqrt{21}}
   =\frac{80\sqrt{2}}{24\sqrt{21}}
   =\frac{10\sqrt{2}}{3\sqrt{21}}
   =\frac{10\sqrt{42}}{63}.
\]

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

The radius is expressed in the required form  

\[
\boxed{\dfrac{10\sqrt{42}}{63}},
\]

where \(m=10,\;n=42,\;p=63\).  
All conditions are satisfied: \(m\) and \(p\) are coprime, and \(n\) is square‑free.

Thus

\[
m+n+p=10+42+63=115.
\]
