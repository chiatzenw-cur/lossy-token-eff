### Real part of \(F(\theta)\)

For a complex number \(a+bi\),

\[
(a+bi)e^{i\theta} = (a\cos\theta - b\sin\theta)+ i\,(b\cos\theta + a\sin\theta).
\]

Hence  

\[
\begin{aligned}
\text{Re}\bigl[(300+468i)e^{i\theta}\bigr] &= 300\cos\theta - 468\sin\theta,\\[4pt]
\text{Re}\bigl[(24+36i)e^{-i\theta}\bigr] &= 24\cos\theta + 36\sin\theta .
\end{aligned}
\]

Adding them gives  

\[
\operatorname{Re}F(\theta)= (300+24)\cos\theta + (-468+36)\sin\theta
   = 324\cos\theta - 432\sin\theta .
\]

---

### Maximizing \(\operatorname{Re}F(\theta)\)

The expression \(324\cos\theta - 432\sin\theta\) is of the form \(R\cos\theta + S\sin\theta\) with  
\(R=324,\; S=-432\).

Its maximum value is  

\[
\sqrt{R^{2}+S^{2}}
    = \sqrt{324^{2}+(-432)^{2}}
    = \sqrt{104\,976+186\,624}
    = \sqrt{291\,600}
    = 540 .
\]

(Indeed, this occurs when \(\cos\theta = \frac{324}{540}=0.6\) and \(\sin\theta = -\frac{432}{540}=-0.8\).)
