\[

&\Longrightarrow& 6+2b+3c+d &\equiv 0\pmod 7\\
&\Longrightarrow& 2b+3c+d &\equiv 1\pmod 7\tag{A}\\[2mm]

Divide \(N\) by \(1000\):

\[
\frac{5694}{1000}=5\text{ remainder }694 .
\]

Hence \(Q=5,\;R=694\) and

\[
Q+R=5+694=699 .
\]
