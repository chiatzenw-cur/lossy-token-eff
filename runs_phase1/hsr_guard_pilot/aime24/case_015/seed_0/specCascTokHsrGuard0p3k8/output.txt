### 1.  Condition for a rhombus
For a rhombus all sides are equal.  
Using the coordinates above,

\[
\begin{aligned}
AB^{2}&=(x_1-x_2)^2+(y_1-y_2)^2,\\
BC^{2}&=(x_1+x_2)^2+(y_1+y_2)^2,
\end{aligned}
\]

and \(AB=BC\) gives  

\[
(x_1-x_2)^2+(y_1-y_2)^2=(x_1+x_2)^2+(y_1+y_2)^2

\]

Thus the vectors \(\overrightarrow{OA}\) and \(\overrightarrow{OB}\) are

--------------------------------------------------------------------
### 2.  Using the perpendicular line
Equation (2) means that the vector \(B=(x_2,y_2)\) is orthogonal to all
points \(u\) on the line

\[
u=(-\lambda y_2,\;\lambda x_2),\qquad \lambda\in\mathbb R
\]

through the origin perpendicular to \(B\).
We need such an \(u\) to satisfy (1). Substituting,

\[
\frac{(-\lambda y_2)^2}{20}-\frac{(\lambda x_2)^2}{24}=1
\quad\Longrightarrow\quad
\lambda^2\Bigl(\frac{y_2^2}{20}-\frac{x_2^2}{24}\Bigr)=1. \tag{3}
\]

\[
