### 1.  Condition for a rhombus with diagonals passing through the origin  

The side vectors are  

\[
\overrightarrow{AB}= (x_2-x_1,\; y_2-y_1),\qquad
\overrightarrow{BC}=(-x_1-x_2,\; -y_1-y_2).
\]

For a rhombus we need \(|\,\overrightarrow{AB}\,|=|\,\overrightarrow{BC}\,|\).  
Squaring and simplifying gives  

\[
(x_2-x_1)^2+(y_2-y_1)^2=(-x_1-x_2)^2+(-y_1-y_2)^2,
\]

which reduces to  

\[
x_1x_2+y_1y_2=0. \tag{1}
\]

Thus the vectors \((x_1,y_1)\) and \((x_2,y_2)\) are orthogonal.

### 2.  Express \(y_i\) in terms of \(x_i\)

From the hyperbola equation  

\[
