\[
a=\frac{5}{2}\frac{\,10a/e^a\,}{e^b}
   =\frac{25a}{e^a e^b}.
\]

\[
1=\frac{25}{e^a e^b}\quad\Longrightarrow\quad e^a e^b=25.
\]

But \(e^a=x\) and \(e^b=y\), so

\[
xy=25.
\]
