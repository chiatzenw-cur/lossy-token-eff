\]

Thus the position vectors \(\mathbf{u}=(x_1,y_1)\) and \(\mathbf{v}=(x_2,y_2)\) are orthogonal.

Each vertex lies on the hyperbola  

\[
\frac{x^2}{20}-\frac{y^2}{24}=1 .
\]

Introduce the standard parametrisation

\[
(x,y)=\bigl(\sqrt{20}\sec\theta,\;\sqrt{24}\tan\theta\bigr),
\qquad \theta\in\mathbb R .
\]

Hence

\[
\mathbf{u}=\bigl(\sqrt{20}\sec\theta,\;\sqrt{24}\tan\theta\bigr),\qquad
\mathbf{v}=\bigl(\sqrt{20}\sec\phi,\;\sqrt{24}\tan\phi\bigr).
\]

Orthogonality \((1)\) yields

\[
20\sec\theta\sec\phi+24\tan\theta\tan\phi=0
\;\Longrightarrow\;
20+24\sin\theta\sin\phi=0
\;\Longrightarrow\;
\sin\theta\sin\phi=-\frac{5}{6}. \tag{2}
\]

\[
BD^2=4(x_2^2+y_2^2)
=4\bigl(20\sec^2\phi+24\tan^2\phi\bigr)
=4\bigl(44\sec^2\phi-24\bigr)

\]

\[
BD^2=176\frac{1}{1-s}-96

\]

\[
s\ge \frac{25}{36}.
\]

\[
s_{\min}=\frac{25}{36}\quad\Longrightarrow\quad
