The number of possible draws is

\[

---

### 2.  Probability of the grand prize  

The grand prize occurs only when the draw equals Jen’s set of four numbers.  
Hence

\[
P(\text{grand prize})=\frac{1}{210}.
\]

---

### 3.  Probability of winning a prize  

Jen wins a prize when at least two of her numbers are among the drawn numbers.  
Count the draws that have **0 or 1** of her numbers and subtract from the total.

* Draws with **0** of her numbers: choose all 4 from the 6 numbers not in her set  

  \[
  \binom{6}{4}=15 .
  \]

* Draws with **exactly 1** of her numbers:  
  \[
  \binom{4}{1}\binom{6}{3}=4\cdot20=80 .
  \]

So the number of draws with at least two of her numbers is

\[
210-15-80=115 .
\]

Hence

\[
P(\text{prize})=\frac{115}{210}=\frac{23}{42}.
\]

---

### 4.  Conditional probability

\[
P(\text{grand prize}\mid\text{prize})=
\frac{P(\text{grand prize})}{P(\text{prize})}
