### Length \(CE\)

The coordinate of \(C\) is \(x_C=107\).  
The distance between \(C\) and \(E\) is

\[
CE = |\,x_C-x\,| = |\,107-x\,|.
\]

The two possible values are

\[
x=3  \;\Rightarrow\; CE=107-3=104,
\]
\[
x=-187 \;\Rightarrow\; CE=107-(-187)=294.
\]

The configuration where the two rectangles are disjoint has \(x=-187\);  
hence \(CE=294\).
