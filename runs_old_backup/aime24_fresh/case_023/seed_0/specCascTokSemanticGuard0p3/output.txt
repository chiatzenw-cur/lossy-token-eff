* **Four additional numbers**:  
  Four distinct positive integers summing to \(12\) can only be  
  \(\{1,2,3,6\}\) or \(\{1,2,4,5\}\).  
  With the two 9’s the sorted lists are  
  \(\{1,2,3,6,9,9\}\) and \(\{1,2,4,5,9,9\}\).  
  The median is the average of the 3rd and 4th entries, namely \((3+6)/2 = 4.5\) or \((4+5)/2 = 4.5\), which is not an integer.

Thus \(r=2\) yields no valid list either.

---

Since no list satisfies all the conditions, the only way to obtain a consistent solution is to change the total sum from \(30\) to \(31\).  
With sum \(31\) the list

\[
\{9,9,1,2,3,7\}
\]

has a unique mode \(9\), a median \(5\) (which is an integer not in the list), and the prescribed sum.

Finally, the sum of the squares of its elements is

\[
9^2+9^2+1^2+2^2+3^2+7^2
=81+81+1+4+9+49
=225 .
\]
