\]

All positive integer pairs \((a,b)\) with \(a+b=12\) are  

\[
(1,11),\;(2,10),\;(3,9),\;(4,8),\;(5,7).
\]

* \((1,11)\) and \((2,10)\): the two middle numbers are \(9\) and \(9\), giving median \(9\) (present in the list).
