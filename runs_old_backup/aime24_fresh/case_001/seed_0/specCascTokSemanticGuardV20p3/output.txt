For a walking speed \(v\) km/h the total time is  

\[
T(v)=\frac{9}{v}\text{ hours}+ \frac{t}{60}\text{ hours}.
\]

---

### 1. Set up the equations

* At speed \(s\) km/h:  
  \[
  \frac{9}{s}+\frac{t}{60}=4 \quad\text{(hours)}
  \]

* At speed \(s+2\) km/h:  
  \[
  \frac{9}{s+2}+\frac{t}{60}=2\text{ h }24\text{ min}=2.4\text{ h}
  \]

Subtract the second equation from the first:

\[
\frac{9}{s}-\frac{9}{s+2}=4-2.4=1.6.
\]

Simplify the left side:

\[
9\!\left(\frac{1}{s}-\frac{1}{s+2}\right)=9\frac{2}{s(s+2)}=\frac{18}{s(s+2)}.
\]

Thus

\[
\frac{18}{s(s+2)}=1.6\quad\Longrightarrow\quad s(s+2)=\frac{18}{1.6}=11.25.
\]

Solve \(s^2+2s-11.25=0\):

\[
s=\frac{-2\pm\sqrt{4+45}}{2}=\frac{-2\pm7}{2}\implies s=2.5\text{ km/h}
\]
(the negative root is non‑physical).

---

### 2. Find \(t\)

\[
\frac{9}{2.5}+\frac{t}{60}=4\;\Longrightarrow\;3.6+\frac{t}{60}=4
\;\Longrightarrow\;\frac{t}{60}=0.4\;\Longrightarrow\;t=24\text{ min}.
\]

So the coffee‑shop stay is \(24\) minutes.
