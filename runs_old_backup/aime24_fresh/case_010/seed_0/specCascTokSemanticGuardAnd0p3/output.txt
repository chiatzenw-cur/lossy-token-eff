### 2.  Probability of the grand prize

The grand prize occurs when the drawn set equals Jen’s chosen set.  
Only one 4‑subset matches her choice.

\[
P(\text{grand prize})=\frac{1}{210}
\]

### 3.  Probability of winning a prize (at least two hits)

We count the number of 4‑subsets that contain at least two of her four numbers.

* Subsets that contain **0** of her numbers: choose all 4 from the other 6 numbers  
  \(\displaystyle \binom{6}{4}=15\).

* Subsets that contain **exactly 1** of her numbers: choose 1 from her 4 and 3 from the other 6  
  \(\displaystyle 4\binom{6}{3}=4\cdot20=80\).

Hence subsets with at least two matches:

\[
210-15-80=115
\]

\[
P(\text{at least two matches})=\frac{115}{210}= \frac{23}{42}
\]
