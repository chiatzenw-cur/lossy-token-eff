### 2.  Probability of winning a prize  

Winning a prize means that at least two of the numbers in \(D\) belong to \(J\).  
Let \(k\) be the number of matches \(|D\cap J|=k\).  
For a fixed \(k\) the number of draws with exactly \(k\) matches is  

\[
\binom{4}{k}\binom{6}{4-k}\qquad (k=0,1,2,3,4).
\]

Thus for \(k=2,3,4\):

\[
\begin{aligned}
k=2 &: \binom{4}{2}\binom{6}{2}=6\cdot15=90,\\
k=3 &: \binom{4}{3}\binom{6}{1}=4\cdot6=24,\\
k=4 &: \binom{4}{4}\binom{6}{0}=1\cdot1=1.
\end{aligned}
\]

\[
90+24+1=115 .

P(\text{prize})=\frac{115}{210}=\frac{23}{42}.
