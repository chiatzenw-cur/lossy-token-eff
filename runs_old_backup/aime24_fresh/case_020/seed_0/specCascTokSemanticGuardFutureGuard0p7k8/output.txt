### Resultant Approach

For monic polynomials \(f,g\) of degrees \(n,m\)

\[
\operatorname{Res}(f,g)=\prod_{i=1}^{n} g(\alpha_i),
\]

where \(\alpha_i\) are the roots of \(f\).  
By symmetry (and \((-1)^{nm}=1\) for \(n=2,m=13\)) we also have

\[
\operatorname{Res}(g,f)=\prod_{j=1}^{m} f(\beta_j),
\]

where \(\beta_j\) are the roots of \(g\).  
Hence

\[
P=\prod_{\beta_j^ {\,13}=1} f(\beta_j)=\operatorname{Res}(x^{13}-1,\;x^2-2x+2).
\]

---

### Computing the Resulant

The roots of \(f(x)=x^2-2x+2\) are

\[
\alpha_1=1+i,\qquad \alpha_2=1-i .
\]

Thus

\[
\operatorname{Res}(x^{13}-1,\;x^2-2x+2)=\bigl((1+i)^{13}-1\bigr)\bigl((1-i)^{13}-1\bigr).
\]

Compute \( (1+i)^{13}\) and \( (1-i)^{13}\):

\[
(1+i)^2 = 2i,\qquad (1+i)^4 = (2i)^2 = -4,
\]
\[
(1+i)^8 = (-4)^2 = 16,\qquad (1+i)^{12} = 16(-4) = -64,
\]
\[
(1+i)^{13}= -64(1+i).
\]

Similarly,

\[
(1-i)^{13}= -64(1-i).
\]

Hence

\[
\begin{aligned}
(1+i)^{13}-1 &= -64(1+i)-1 = -65-64i,\\
(1-i)^{13}-1 &= -64(1-i)-1 = -65+64i.
\end{aligned}
\]

Multiplying gives

\[
P=(-65-64i)(-65+64i)=(-65)^2+(64)^2=65^2+64^2
=4225+4096=8321.
\]
