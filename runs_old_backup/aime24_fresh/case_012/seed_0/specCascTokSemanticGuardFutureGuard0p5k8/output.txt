* 8 moves to the right (R) and  
* 8 moves upward (U).

Hence the path has length \(16\) (there are \(16\) steps).

---

### 1.  Exactly 4 changes of direction  

A change of direction occurs whenever the path switches from a run of R’s to a run of U’s or vice‑versa.  
To have **exactly four changes** the path must be made of

\[
\underbrace{R\,\text{run}}\; \underbrace{U\,\text{run}}\; \underbrace{R\,\text{run}}\; \underbrace{U\,\text{run}}\; \underbrace{R\,\text{run}}
\]

or the symmetric version starting with U.  
Thus there are **5 runs** (alternating between R and U).

---

### 2.  Counting the runs  

Let the lengths of the three R‑runs be \(r_1,r_2,r_3\) and the lengths of the two U‑runs be \(u_1,u_2\).

They must satisfy  

\[
r_1+r_2+r_3 = 8,\qquad u_1+u_2 = 8,
\]

with each \(r_i,u_j\ge 1\) (each run must contain at least one step).

*Compositions of 8 into 3 positive parts*  
\[
\#\text{(ways to choose }r_1,r_2,r_3)=\binom{8-1}{3-1}=\binom{7}{2}=21.
\]

*Compositions of 8 into 2 positive parts*  
\[
\#\text{(ways to choose }u_1,u_2)=\binom{8-1}{2-1}=\binom{7}{1}=7.
\]

Therefore, for a path that starts with an R:

\[
\text{ways} = 21 \times 7 = 147.
\]

The same count holds for paths that start with a U (just interchange R and U).  
Thus

\[
\text{total ways} = 147 + 147 = 294.
\]
