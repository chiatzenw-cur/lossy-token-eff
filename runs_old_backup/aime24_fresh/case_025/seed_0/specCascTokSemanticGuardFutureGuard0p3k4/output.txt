\end{cases}

Adding all three gives  

\[
-(a+b+c)=\frac12+\frac13+\frac14=\frac{13}{12}
\quad\Longrightarrow\quad a+b+c=-\frac{13}{12}.
\]

Now solve pairwise:

\[
\begin{aligned}
(1)+(2)&: a-b-c+(-a+b-c)= -2c= \frac12+\frac13=\frac56 
      \Longrightarrow c=-\frac{5}{12},\\[4pt]
(1)+(3)&: a-b-c+(-a-b+c)= -2b= \frac12+\frac14=\frac34 
      \Longrightarrow b=-\frac38,\\[4pt]
(2)+(3)&: -a+b-c+(-a-b+c)= -2a= \frac13+\frac14=\frac7{12}
      \Longrightarrow a=-\frac7{24}.
\end{aligned}
\]

Thus  

\[
\log_2 x=-\frac{7}{24},\quad
\log_2 y=-\frac{3}{8},\quad
\log_2 z=-\frac{5}{12}.
\]

Now evaluate the desired logarithm:

\[
\begin{aligned}
\log_2 (x^4y^3z^2)
&=4\log_2 x+3\log_2 y+2\log_2 z\\
&=4\!\left(-\frac{7}{24}\right)+3\!\left(-\frac{3}{8}\right)+2\!\left(-\frac{5}{12}\right)\\
&=-\frac{7}{6}-\frac{9}{8}-\frac{5}{6}\\
&=-\frac{75}{24}=-\frac{25}{8}.
\end{aligned}
\]

\left|\log_2(x^4y^3z^2)\right|=\frac{25}{8}.
