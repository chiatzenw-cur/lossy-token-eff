### 4.  Solving the system

The system is

\[
y=F(x),\qquad x=G(y).
\]

Since \(F,G:[0,1]\to[0,1]\) are continuous, intersection points are the
fixed points of the composite map

\[
H(x)=G(F(x)),\qquad x\in[0,1].
\]

One can solve the equations directly.
