So a key theme: the objective intent, not subjective. That matches option A, maybe.

The court considered the parties' intent to be bound, whether the contract was formed orally or later in writing. The court looked at the objective expression of intent, such as signings, press releases, etc. It also considered the parties' intent to be bound prior to signing a formal agreement.
