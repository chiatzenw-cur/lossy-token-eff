\[
\text{surface area}=2(ab+bc+ca)=54   \quad\Longrightarrow\quad ab+bc+ca=27
\]
\[
\text{volume}=abc=23
\]

The smallest sphere that can contain the box is the circumscribed sphere.  
With the centre at the centre of the box, the distance from the centre to a
corner is

\[
r_{\text{box}}=\frac12\sqrt{a^{2}+b^{2}+c^{2}}
\]

Hence the radius \(r\) that works for **all** boxes in \(\mathcal B\) is

\[
r=\max_{(a,b,c)}\frac12\sqrt{a^{2}+b^{2}+c^{2}}
\]

so we must maximise \(a^{2}+b^{2}+c^{2}\) subject to  
\(ab+bc+ca=27,\; abc=23\).

--------------------------------------------------------------------
### 1.  Express \(a^{2}+b^{2}+c^{2}\) in terms of \(s_1=a+b+c\)

\[
a^{2}+b^{2}+c^{2}=s_{1}^{2}-2s_{2} = s_{1}^{2}-54
\]

Thus the problem reduces to maximising \(s_{1}\) while keeping
\(s_{2}=27,\;s_{3}=23\).

--------------------------------------------------------------------
### 2.  Use Lagrange multipliers

Let \(f(a,b,c)=a+b+c\) with constraints  

\[
g_{1}(a,b,c)=ab+bc+ca-27=0,\qquad
g_{2}(a,b,c)=abc-23=0 .
\]

The conditions \(\nabla f=\lambda\nabla g_{1}+\mu\nabla g_{2}\) give  

\[
1=\lambda(b+c)+\mu bc,\qquad
1=\lambda(c+a)+\mu ca,\qquad
1=\lambda(a+b)+\mu ab.
\]

Subtracting any two equations yields \((a-b)(\lambda+\mu c)=0\), etc.
Hence at an interior extremum at least two of the variables are equal.
Let \(a=b=x,\;c=y\).

--------------------------------------------------------------------
### 3.  Solve the two‑variable system

\[
\begin{cases}
x^{2}+2xy=27,\\[2mm]
x^{2}y=23.
\end{cases}
\]

From the second equation \(y=\dfrac{23}{x^{2}}\).  
Insert into the first:

\[
x^{2}+ \frac{46}{x}=27
\quad\Longrightarrow\quad
x^{3}-27x+46=0.
\]

Factoring gives \((x-2)(x^{2}+2x-23)=0\).

The positive roots are  
\(x=2\) and \(x=-1+2\sqrt6\approx3.898\).

For \(x=2\): \(y=\dfrac{23}{4}=5.75\).

For \(x=-1+2\sqrt6\): \(y=\dfrac{23}{x^{2}}\approx1.513\).

The sum of sides is

\[
s_{1}=2x+y.
\]

* For \(x=2\): \(s_{1}=2(2)+5.75=9.75\).
* For \(x=-1+2\sqrt6\): \(s_{1}\approx9.31\).

Hence the **maximum** possible \(s_{1}\) is \(9.75\).

--------------------------------------------------------------------
### 4.  Compute the maximal radius

\[
a^{2}+b^{2}+c^{2}=4+4+\left(\frac{23}{4}\right)^{2}
      =8+\frac{529}{16}
      =\frac{656}{16}+\frac{529}{16}
      =\frac{1185}{16}=41.0625.
\]

Therefore

\[
r^2=\frac14 (a^{2}+b^{2}+c^{2})
     =\frac14\cdot41.0625
     =\frac{657}{64}.
\]

This fraction is already in lowest terms: \(\gcd(657,64)=1\).

Hence \(p=657,\;q=64\) and

\[
p+q=657+64=721.
