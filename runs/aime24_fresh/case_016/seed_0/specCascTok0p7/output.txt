### 2. Verification (inclusion–exclusion)

Let  

\[
P = \sum_{\text{pairs}}|X\cap Y|,\qquad
T = \sum_{\text{triples}}|X\cap Y\cap Z| .

A resident with exactly \(k\) items contributes \(\binom{k}{2}\) pairs and \(\binom{k}{3}\) triples.

\[
P = 1\cdot 437 + 3\cdot 234 + 6\cdot x = 1139 + 6x,

\[
|A\cup B\cup C\cup D| = 2024 - P + T - x.

\[
900 = 2024 - (1139+6x) + (234+4x) - x.
