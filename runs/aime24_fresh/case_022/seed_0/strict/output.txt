\end{array}
\]

The total number of chords is \(6\times5+6\times6=30+36=66=C(12,2)\), as it
should be.

--------------------------------------------------------------------
### 3.  Naming the perpendicular pairs of directions

Two sides of a rectangle are perpendicular, so we must choose an
unordered pair of perpendicular directions.  
With the sets above the perpendicular pairs are

\[
\begin{aligned}
&(0^{\circ},90^{\circ}),\;(30^{\circ},120^{\circ}),\;(60^{\circ},150^{\circ}),\\
&(15^{\circ},105^{\circ}),\;(45^{\circ},135^{\circ}),\;(75^{\circ},165^{\circ}).
\end{aligned}
\]

--------------------------------------------------------------------
### 4.  Counting rectangles for each pair

For a fixed pair \((d_1,d_2)\),

* choose any two distinct chords of direction \(d_1\)  – this can be
  done in \(\binom{n_{d_1}}{2}\) ways, where \(n_{d_1}\) is the number of
  chords having that direction;
* choose any two distinct chords of direction \(d_2\) – in
  \(\binom{n_{d_2}}{2}\) ways.

The four intersection points of these two pairs of chords form a
rectangle.  Thus the number of rectangles associated with a pair is

\[
\binom{n_{d_1}}{2}\binom{n_{d_2}}{2}.
\]

Using the numbers from the previous table:

\[
\begin{array}{l|c}
\text{Pair} & \binom{n_{d_1}}{2}\binom{n_{d_2}}{2} \\ \hline
(0^{\circ},90^{\circ}) & \binom{5}{2}^{2}=10^{2}=100\\
(30^{\circ},120^{\circ}) & 100\\
(60^{\circ},150^{\circ}) & 100\\
(15^{\circ},105^{\circ}) & \binom{6}{2}^{2}=15^{2}=225\\
(45^{\circ},135^{\circ}) & 225\\
(75^{\circ},165^{\circ}) & 225
\end{array}
\]

--------------------------------------------------------------------
### 5.  Total number of rectangles

Adding the six values gives

\[
100+100+100+225+225+225=975 .
\]
