A path that stays on the gridlines and has length \(16\) can only move **right** or **up**; the Manhattan distance between the two corners is \(8+8=16\).  
Hence every admissible path is a monotonic path consisting of 8 rights and 8 ups.

---

### 1.  What does “change direction exactly four times” mean?

A change of direction occurs whenever a right step is followed by an up step or vice‑versa.  
If the path changes direction exactly four times, then the sequence of moves is divided into

\[
5\text{ segments of consecutive identical moves}
\]

and the move direction alternates between the segments.

---

### 2.  Two possible patterns

Because the path can start with either a right or an up move, there are two patterns:

| Start | Segments |
|-------|----------|
| Right | \(R\,U\,R\,U\,R\) |
| Up    | \(U\,R\,U\,R\,U\) |

Thus, in the first pattern the rights are split into **three** positive integers  
\(r_1,r_2,r_3\) and the ups into **two** positive integers \(u_1,u_2\).

In the second pattern the ups are split into **three** positive integers  
\(u_1,u_2,u_3\) and the rights into **two** positive integers \(r_1,r_2\).

---

### 3.  Counting the assignments

The number of positive integer solutions to \(x_1+x_2+\dots+x_k=n\) is
\(\displaystyle \binom{n-1}{k-1}\).

*Pattern 1 – start with Right*  

\[
\begin{aligned}
r_1+r_2+r_3 &= 8 &\Longrightarrow&\ \binom{8-1}{3-1}= \binom{7}{2}=21,\\[2mm]
u_1+u_2 &= 8 &\Longrightarrow&\ \binom{8-1}{2-1}= \binom{7}{1}=7.
\end{aligned}
\]

So this pattern gives \(21\times 7 = 147\) distinct paths.

*Pattern 2 – start with Up*  

\[
\begin{aligned}
u_1+u_2+u_3 &= 8 &\Longrightarrow&\ \binom{7}{2}=21,\\[2mm]
r_1+r_2 &= 8 &\Longrightarrow&\ \binom{7}{1}=7.
\end{aligned}
\]

Again we obtain \(21\times 7 = 147\) distinct paths.
