\prod_{k=1}^{2} F(\beta_k)
\]

where \(\beta_k\) are the roots of \(G(z)=0\).  
Solving \(G(z)=0\) gives

\[
\beta_{1}=1+i,\qquad \beta_{2}=1-i .
\]

Thus

\[
\prod_{k=0}^{12} (2-2\omega^{k}+\omega^{2k})
  =\prod_{k=1}^{2} \bigl(\beta_k^{\,13}-1\bigr).
\]

Compute the powers:

\[
\begin{aligned}
(1+i)^2 &= 2i,\qquad (1+i)^4=-4,\\
(1+i)^8 &= 16,\qquad (1+i)^12 &=16(-4)=-64,\\
(1+i)^13 &= (-64)(1+i)=-64-64i .
\end{aligned}
\]

Similarly

\[
(1-i)^2=-2i,\qquad (1-i)^8 =(-4)^2=16,\qquad
(1-i)^12 =-64,\qquad
(1-i)^13 = (-64)(1-i)=-64+64i .
\]

Consequently

\[
(1+i)^13-1 = -65-64i,\qquad
(1-i)^13-1 = -65+64i .
\]

Their product is

\[
(-65-64i)(-65+64i)= (-65)^2 -(64i)^2
         = 4225-4096(-1)=4225+4096=8321 .
\]

Therefore

\[
\prod_{k=0}^{12} (2-2\omega^{k}+\omega^{2k})=8321 .
\]

Finally, the remainder when \(8321\) is divided by \(1000\) is

\[
8321-1000\cdot\lfloor 8321/1000\rfloor  = 8321-8000=321 .
\]
