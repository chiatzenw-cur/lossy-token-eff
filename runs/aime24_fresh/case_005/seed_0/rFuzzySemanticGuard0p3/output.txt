\[
g^{(p-1)/2}= (g^{s})^{4}\qquad\Longrightarrow 
\frac{p-1}{2}\equiv 0\gmod 4 ,\quad\Rightarrow\quad p-1\equiv0\equiv8 \pmod{8}.
\]

Hence the necessary condition is

\[
p\equiv 1\pmod 8 .
\]

For the smallest prime with this property, \(p\) is \(17\); all smaller primes
\(2,3,5,7,11 \) fail to obtain \(n^4+1\equiv 0\pmod{p^2\)}.
