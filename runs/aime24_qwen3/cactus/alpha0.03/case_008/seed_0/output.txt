### Step 1: Use Logarithmic Properties

Recall the power rule of logarithms:
$$
\log_b(a^c) = c \log_ba
$$
Applying this to both given equations:

$$
\log_x(y^x) = x \log_x y = 10 \quad \text{(Equation 1)}
$$

$$
\log_y(x^{4y}) = 4y \log_y x = 10 \quad \text{(Equation 2)}
$$

$$
\log_x y = \frac{1}{\log_y x}
