Aya walks **9 kilometers** each morning. The total time of the walk includes both the **walking time** and the **t minutes** she spends at the coffee shop. When she walks at a **speed of $ s $ km/h**, the total time of the walk is **4 hours**, and when she walks at a speed of **$ s+2 $ km/h**, the total time is **2 hours and 24 minutes**, which converts to **2.4 hours**.

---

### Step 1: Express Walking Time in Terms of Speed

We'll proceed by using the formula:
$$
\text{Distance} = \text{Speed} \times \text{Time}
$$
Let’s denote:

- $ s $ = her constant walking speed (km/h),
- $ t $ = time spent in coffee shop (in minutes),
- Her walking speed increases by 2 km/h and by 0.5 km/h respectively in the two given cases.

#### First Scenario:
Speed: $ s $  
Total time: 4 hours  
Coffee shop time: $ t $ minutes = $ \frac{t}{60} $ hours  
Walking time: $ 4 - \frac{t}{60} $ hours  
So, using the distance formula:
$$
9 = s \left(4 - \frac{t}{60} \right)
$$

#### Second Scenario:
Speed: $ s + 2 $  
Total time: 2.4 hours  
Walking time: $ 2.4 - \frac{t}{60} $ hours  
So:
$$
9 = (s + 2) \left(2.4 - \frac{t}{60} \right)
$$

### Step 2: Solve the System of Equations

From the first equation:
$$
9 = s \left(4 - \frac{t}{60} \right) \Rightarrow 4 - \frac{t}{60} = \frac{9}{s}
$$
Multiply both sides by 60:
$$
240 - t = \frac{540}{s} \Rightarrow t = 240 - \frac{540}{s}
$$

From the second equation:
$$
9 = (s + 2) \left(2.4 - \frac{t}{60} \right) \Rightarrow 2.4 - \frac{t}{60} = \frac{9}{s + 2}
$$
Multiply both sides by 60:
$$
144 - t = \frac{540}{s + 2} \Rightarrow t = 144 - \frac{540}{s + 2}
$$

Now equating the two expressions for $ t $:
$$
240 - \frac{540}{s} = 144 - \frac{540}{s + 2}
$$

Solve:

$$
240 - 144 = \frac{540}{s} - \frac{540}{s + 2}
\Rightarrow 96 = 540 \left( \frac{1}{s} - \frac{1}{s + 2} \right)
$$

$$
96 = 540 \left( \frac{s + 2 - s}{s(s + 2)} \right) = 540 \cdot \frac{2}{s(s + 2)}
\Rightarrow 96 = \frac{1080}{s(s + 2)}
\Rightarrow s(s + 2) = \frac{1080}{96} = 11.25
$$

Multiplying through:
$$
s(s + 2) = 11.25 \Rightarrow s^2 + 2s - 11.25 = 0

Multiply by 4 to eliminate decimals:

4s^2 + 8s - 45 = 0

s = \frac{-8 \pm \sqrt{8^2 + 4(4)(45)}}{2(4)} = \frac{-8 \pm \sqrt{64 + 720}}{8} = \frac{-8 \pm \sqrt{784}}{8} = \frac{-8 \pm 28}{8}
