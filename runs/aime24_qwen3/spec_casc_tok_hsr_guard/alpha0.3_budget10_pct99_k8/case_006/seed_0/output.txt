- $BC = AD = \sqrt{89}$

This configuration has special properties: it is a **disphenoid**, a type of tetrahedron where **opposite edges are equal**. In such tetrahedrons, **all four faces are congruent**, which is a crucial observation for simplifying our solution.

---

### Step 1: Understanding the Incenter and Distance to Faces

In a tetrahedron, the **incenter** is the point where the distances to all four triangular faces are equal. This point is equidistant to each face, and the distance $d$ from this point to a face can be calculated using the formula:

$$
d = \frac{3V}{A_1 + A_2 + A_3 + A_4} = \frac{3V}{4A}
$$

Here, $V$ is the volume of the tetrahedron, and $A$ is the area of **one of the faces**, since all areas are equal in a disphenoid.

---

### Step 2: Compute the Volume $V$

We use coordinates and the **scalar triple product** to compute the volume. Let us define the vertices of the tetrahedron as follows (by exploiting the symmetry of the disphenoid):

- $A = (2, \frac{5}{2}, 4)$
- $B = (-2, -\frac{5}{2}, 4)$
- $C = (-2, \frac{5}{2}, -4)$
- $D = (2, -\frac{5}{2}, -4)$

Vectors from point $A$ are:
- $\vec{AB} = B - A = (-4, -5, 0)$
- $\vec{AC} = C - A = (-4, 0, -8)$
- $\vec{AD} = D - A = (0, -5, -8)$

Compute the **cross product** $\vec{AC} \times \vec{AD}$:
$$
\vec{AC} \times \vec{AD} = (-40, -32, 20)
$$

Now calculate the **dot product** $ \vec{AB} \cdot (\vec{AC} \times \vec{AD}) $:
$$
\vec{AB} \cdot (-40, -32, 20) = (-4)(-40) + (-5)(-32) + (0)(20) = 160 + 160 = 320
$$

The **volume** of the tetrahedron is:
$$
V = \frac{1}{6} \cdot |\vec{AB} \cdot (\vec{AC} \times \vec{AD})| = \frac{1}{6} \cdot 320 = \frac{160}{3}
$$

### Step 3: Compute the Area of One Face

Choose any triangular face, say triangle $ABC$. Using vectors $\vec{AB}$ and $\vec{AC}$, we compute the **magnitude of their cross product**:

$$
\vec{AB} \times \vec{AC} = (40, -32, -20) \quad \Rightarrow \quad \|\vec{AB} \times \vec{AC}\| = \sqrt{40^2 + (-32)^2 + (-20)^2} = \sqrt{1600 + 1024 + 400} = \sqrt{3024}
$$
