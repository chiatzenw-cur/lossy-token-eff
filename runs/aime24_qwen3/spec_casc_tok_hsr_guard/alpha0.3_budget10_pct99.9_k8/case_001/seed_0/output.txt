To determine how long Aya’s walk takes when she walks at $ s + \frac{1}{2} $ kilometers per hour, we are given two scenarios involving Aya’s walking speed and the total time taken, including $ t $ minutes spent at the coffee shop. Let’s carefully unpack and solve this step by step.

---

### **Understanding the Problem**

We know:

- Aya walks **9 kilometers** every morning.
- When walking at **speed $ s $** (in km/h), her **total time** (including $ t $ minutes at the coffee shop) is **4 hours**.
- When walking at **speed $ s + 2 $** (in km/h), her **total time** (including $ t $ minutes at the coffee shop) is **2 hours and 24 minutes = 2.4 hours**.
- We are given that she now walks at **speed $ s + 0.5 $** and need to find the **total time in minutes**, including the $ t $ minutes spent at the coffee shop.

First, we convert all times to **hours** to avoid unit confusion. Let’s define:

- Let $ t $ be the time (in minutes) spent at the coffee shop.
- Then, the **walking time** in each case is:

  $$
  \text{Walking time} = \text{Total time} - \frac{t}{60}
  $$

  Because the total time includes the walking time and the coffee shop time.

---

### **Setting Up Equations**

Using this, we get two equations based on the distance walked (9 km):

---

**First scenario (speed = $ s $):**

$$
9 = s \left( 4 - \frac{t}{60} \right)
\tag{1}
$$

**Second scenario (speed = $ s + 2 $):**

$$
9 = (s + 2) \left( 2.4 - \frac{t}{60} \right)
\tag{2}
$$

Now we solve this system of equations.

---

### **Solving the System**

Let’s let $ m = \frac{t}{60} $, converting coffee shop time to hours. The equations become:

$$
9 = s(4 - m) \tag{1'}
$$
$$
9 = (s + 2)(2.4 - m) \tag{2'}
$$

From equation (1’):

$$
4s - sm = 9 \tag{A}
$$

From equation (2’):

$$
(s + 2)(2.4 - m) = 2.4s - sm + 4.8 - 2m = 9 \tag{B}
$$

Now substitute $ 4s - sm = 9 $ from (A) into (B):

$$
(9 - 1.6s) + 4.8 - 2m = 9
\Rightarrow 13.8 - 1.6s - 2m = 9
$$

$$
\Rightarrow -1.6s - 2m = -4.8
\Rightarrow 1.6s + 2m = 4.8 \tag{C}
$$

Dividing (C) by 2:

$$
0.8s + m = 2.4 \tag{D}
$$

Now solve (A) and (D) together:

From (A): $ 4s - sm = 9 $

From (D): $ m = 2.4 - 0.8s $

Substitute into (A):

$$
4s - s(2.4 - 0.8s) = 9
\Rightarrow 4s - 2.4s + 0.8s^2 = 9
\Rightarrow 1.6s + 0.8s^2 = 9
\Rightarrow 0.8s^2 + 1.6s - 9 = 0
$$

Multiply through by 10 to eliminate decimals:

$$
8s^2 + 16s - 90 = 0 \Rightarrow 4s^2 + 8s - 45 = 0
$$

Solve using the quadratic formula:

$$
s = \frac{-8 \pm \sqrt{64 + 720}}{8} = \frac{-8 \pm \sqrt{784}}{8} = \frac{-8 \pm 28}{8}
$$

$$
s = \frac{20}{8} = 2.5 \quad \text{or} \quad s = \frac{-36}{8} = -4.5
$$

Since speed cannot be negative, we have:

$$
s = 2.5 \text{ km/h}
$$

Now find $ t $ using $ m = 2.4 - 0.8s $:

$$
m = 2.4 - 0.8(2.5) = 2.4 - 2.0 = 0.4 \text{ hours}
