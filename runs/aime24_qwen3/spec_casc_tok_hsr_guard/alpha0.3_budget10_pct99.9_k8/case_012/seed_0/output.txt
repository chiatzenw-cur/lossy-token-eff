---

### Understanding the Path

An $8 \times 8$ grid requires moving **8 steps to the right (R)** and **8 steps up (U)** to go from $(0,0)$ to $(8,8)$. So, every valid path consists of **16 moves**, where exactly **8** are R and **8** are U.

A **change of direction** occurs when the path switches from R to U or from U to R. Therefore, a path that changes direction **exactly four times** must switch direction **four times**, implying it has **five segments** of consecutive moves in the same direction (i.e., runs).

Each segment must be at least length 1 (since two consecutive segments must alternate direction), and the total number of R's and U's remains fixed.

---

### Structure of the Path

Because the path changes direction **exactly four times**, it has **five segments**. These segments alternate directions. Thus, the beginning and end of the path determine the structure:

- **If it starts with R**, the sequence of directions will be:  
  **R, U, R, U, R**  
  So, it has **3** R segments and **2** U segments.

- **If it starts with U**, the sequence of directions will be:  
  **U, R, U, R, U**  
  So, it has **3** U segments and **2** R segments.

There are **two distinct cases** to consider: paths that start with **R** and those that start with **U**. Due to symmetry in the grid, the number of paths in each case is equal, so we can compute the number for one and double the result.

---

### Counting the Number of Paths

To count the number of such paths in each case:

#### Case: Starting with R
- The **3 R segments** must partition the 8 R moves into 3 positive integers (since each segment must have at least 1 move). This is equivalent to finding the number of **compositions** of 8 into 3 parts, which is:
  
  $$
  \binom{7}{2} = 21
  $$

- The **2 U segments** must partition the 8 U moves into 2 parts:
  
  $$
  \binom{7}{1} = 7
  $$

So, the number of such paths starting with R is:
$$
21 \times 7 = 147
$$

#### Case: Starting with U
- The **3 U segments** partition 8 U moves: $ \binom{7}{2} = 21 $
- The **2 R segments** partition 8 R moves: $ \binom{7}{1} = 7 $

So, the number of such paths starting with U is also:
$$
21 \times 7 = 147
$$

---

### Final Computation

Adding both cases together:
$$
147 + 147 = 294
$$
