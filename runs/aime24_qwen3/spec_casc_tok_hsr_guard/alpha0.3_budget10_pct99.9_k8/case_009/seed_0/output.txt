In combinatorial game theory, we can classify each number $ n $ as either **winning** or **losing**.

- A **winning position** is one where the current player can pick a move that leaves the opponent in a **losing** position.
- A **losing position** is one where **every possible move** leaves the opponent in a **winning** position.
