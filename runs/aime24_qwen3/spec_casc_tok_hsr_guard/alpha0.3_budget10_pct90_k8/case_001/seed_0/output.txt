To determine how many minutes Aya’s walk takes when she walks at a speed of $ s + \frac{1}{2} $ kilometers per hour, we first need to understand the relationship between her speed, the distance of her walk, and the total time (including t minutes at the coffee shop).

---

### **Step 1: Setting up the equations**

Let:
- $ s $: Aya's walking speed in kilometers per hour.
- $ t $: Time (in minutes) she spends at the coffee shop.

From the problem:
1. At speed $ s $, the walk takes **4 hours** including $ t $ minutes at the shop.
   - Walking time = $ 4 - \frac{t}{60} $ hours
   - So, the walking time equation is:
     $$
     9 = s \left(4 - \frac{t}{60}\right)
     $$

2. At speed $ s + 2 $, the walk takes **2 hours and 24 minutes** (which is 2.4 hours), again including $ t $ minutes at the shop.
   - Walking time = $ 2.4 - \frac{t}{60} $ hours
   - So, the second equation becomes:
     $$
     9 = (s + 2)\left(2.4 - \frac{t}{60}\right)
     $$

---

### **Step 2: Solve the system of equations**

We have two equations:

$$
(1)\quad 9 = s\left(4 - \frac{t}{60}\right)
$$
$$
(2)\quad 9 = (s + 2)\left(2.4 - \frac{t}{60}\right)
$$

From equation (1), solve for $ \frac{t}{60} $:

$$
\frac{t}{60} = 4s - 9 \Rightarrow t = 60(4s - 9)
$$

Substitute $ t = 60(4s - 9) $ into equation (2):

$$
9 = (s + 2)\left(2.4 - \frac{60(4s - 9)}{60}\right) = (s + 2)\left(2.4 - (4s - 9)\right)
$$

Simplify the right-hand side:

$$
2.4 - (4s - 9) = 2.4 - 4s + 9 = 11.4 - 4s
$$

So the equation becomes:

$$
9 = (s + 2)(11.4 - 4s)
$$

Expand the product:

$$
9 = s(11.4 - 4s) + 2(11.4 - 4s) = 11.4s - 4s^2 + 22.8 - 8s
$$

Combine like terms:

$$
9 = (11.4s - 8s) + 22.8 - 4s^2 = 3.4s + 22.8 - 4s^2
$$

Bring all terms to one side:

$$
0 = -4s^2 + 3.4s + 22.8 - 9 \Rightarrow -4s^2 + 3.4s + 13.8 = 0
$$

Multiply through by -1:

$$
4s^2 - 3.4s - 13.8 = 0
$$

Now solve this quadratic using the quadratic formula:

$$
s = \frac{-(-3.4) \pm \sqrt{(-3.4)^2 - 4(4)(-13.8)}}{2(4)} = \frac{3.4 \pm \sqrt{11.56 + 220.8}}{8} = \frac{3.4 \pm \sqrt{232.36}}{8}
$$

$$
\sqrt{232.36} = 15.24 \Rightarrow s = \frac{3.4 \pm 15.24}{8}
$$

$$
