### Step 1: Define the Variables

- Distance walked: $ 9 $ kilometers
- Let $ s $ (in km/h) be Aya's original walking speed
- Let $ t $ (in minutes) be the time spent at the coffee shop
- The total time includes both the walking time and the coffee shop time

When Aya walks at speed $ s $:
- Total time: 4 hours = 240 minutes
- Walking time: $ 240 - t $ minutes
- So walking time (in hours) is $ \frac{240 - t}{60} $

Using the relation $ \text{Distance} = \text{Speed} \times \text{Time} $, we have:
$$
9 = s \cdot \left( \frac{240 - t}{60} \right)
$$

Simplify:
$$
9 = \frac{s(240 - t)}{60} \quad \Rightarrow \quad 540 = s(240 - t) \tag{1}
$$

---

### Step 2: Second Scenario

When Aya walks at speed $ s + 2 $:
- Total time: 2 hours and 24 minutes = 144 minutes
- Walking time: $ 144 - t $ minutes
- Walking time (in hours): $ \frac{144 - t}{60} $

$$
9 = (s + 2) \cdot \left( \frac{144 - t}{60} \right)

$$

### Step 3: Use the Two Equations

$$
540 = s(240 - t)
$$

From (2):

96s - 288 + 2t = 0
$$

Rearranging:
