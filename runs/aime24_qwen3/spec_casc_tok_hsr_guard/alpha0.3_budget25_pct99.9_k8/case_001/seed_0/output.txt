To solve this problem, we start by identifying the key variables and relationships given:

- Aya walks **9 kilometers** every morning.
- At a constant speed of **$ s $** kilometers per hour, her total time (including **$ t $** minutes in the coffee shop) is **4 hours**.
- At a speed of **$ s + 2 $** km/h, her total time (including the same **$ t $** minutes in the coffee shop) is **2 hours and 24 minutes**.
- We are asked to find the total time, **including the coffee shop**, when she walks at **$ s + \frac{1}{2} $** km/h.

---

### Step 1: Convert All Time Units Consistently

Since the coffee shop time $ t $ is in **minutes**, but the walking time in the problem is given in **hours**, it's helpful to convert all times to **hours** for consistency.

Let’s define:
- $ t $: time spent in the coffee shop, in **minutes**.
- $ m = \frac{t}{60} $: time spent in the coffee shop, in **hours**.

---

### Step 2: Set Up the Equations

At speed $ s $:

- Total time = 4 hours = $ 4 - m $ (walking time) + $ m $ (coffee shop time)
- Walking time = $ 4 - m $
- So, the distance covered is:  
  $$
  \text{Distance} = \text{Speed} \times \text{Walking Time} \Rightarrow 9 = s(4 - m) \quad \text{(1)}
  $$

At speed $ s + 2 $:

- Total time = 2 hours and 24 minutes = 2.4 hours = $ 2.4 - m $ (walking time) + $ m $ (coffee shop time)
- So, the distance covered is also 9 km:  
  $$
  9 = (s + 2)(2.4 - m) \quad \text{(2)}
  $$

---

### Step 3: Solve the System of Equations

From equation (1):
$$
s = \frac{9}{4 - m}
$$

Plug this into equation (2):
$$
\left(\frac{9}{4 - m} + 2\right)(2.4 - m) = 9
$$

Simplify the first factor:
$$
\frac{9 + 2(4 - m)}{4 - m} = \frac{9 + 8 - 2m}{4 - m} = \frac{17 - 2m}{4 - m}
$$

So the equation becomes:
$$
\frac{17 - 2m}{4 - m} \cdot (2.4 - m) = 9
$$

Multiply both sides by $ 4 - m $:
$$
(17 - 2m)(2.4 - m) = 9(4 - m)
$$

Expand both sides:

**Left Side**:
$$
(17)(2.4) - 17m - 2m(2.4) + 2m^2 = 40.8 - 17m - 4.8m + 2m^2 = 40.8 - 21.8m + 2m^2
$$

**Right Side**:
$$
36 - 9m
$$

Equating both sides:
$$
40.8 - 21.8m + 2m^2 = 36 - 9m
$$

Bring all terms to one side:
$$
2m^2 - 12.8m + 4.8 = 0
$$

Divide the entire equation by 2:
$$
m^2 - 6.4m + 2.4 = 0
$$

Multiply by 5 to eliminate decimals (making calculation cleaner):
$$
5m^2 - 32m + 12 = 0
$$

Solve using the quadratic formula:
$$
m = \frac{32 \pm \sqrt{(-32)^2 - 4 \cdot 5 \cdot 12}}{2 \cdot 5}
$$
$$
m = \frac{32 \pm \sqrt{1024 - 240}}{10} = \frac{32 \pm \sqrt{784}}{10} = \frac{32 \pm 28}{10}
$$

Solutions:
- $ m = \frac{60}{10} = 6 $
- $ m = \frac{4}{10} = 0.4 $

Since $ m = \frac{t}{60} $, $ m = 6 $ would imply:
$$
t = 6 \times 60 = 360 \text{ minutes} \Rightarrow \text{Walking time} = 4 - \frac{360}{60} = 4 - 6 = -2 \text{ hours} \quad \text{(invalid)}
$$

Therefore, the valid solution is:
$$
m = 0.4 \Rightarrow t = 24 \text{ minutes}
$$

Now find $ s $ using equation (1):
$$
s = \frac{9}{4 - 0.4} = \frac{9}{3.6} = 2.5 \text{ km/h}
$$
