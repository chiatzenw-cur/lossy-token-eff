$$
\log_x(y) = \frac{1}{\log_y(x)}
$$

Let’s denote $ \log_y(x) = k $, then $ \log_x(y) = \frac{1}{k} $. Substituting into the equations:

- Equationpdf: $ x \cdot \frac{1}{k} = 10 \Rightarrow x = \frac{10}{k} $
- Equation 2: $ 4y \cdot k = 10 \Rightarrow y = \frac{10}{4k} = \frac{5}{2k} $
