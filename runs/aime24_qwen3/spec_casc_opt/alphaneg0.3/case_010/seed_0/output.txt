To solve the given problem, we are asked to find the **conditional probability** that Jen **wins the grand prize** given that she **won a prize** in a lottery where she picks 4 distinct numbers from the set $ S = \{1, 2, 3, \, \, \, \, 9, 10\} $, and the lottery independently chooses 4 numbers from $ S $.

---

### Step 1: Total Number of Lottery Combinations

The total number of ways to choose 4 distinct numbers from a set of 10 is:

$$
\binom{10}{4} = 210
$$

So the total possible outcomes are **210** different combinations.

---

### Step 2: Define Events

Let:

- $ A $ = the event that **Jen wins the grand prize**, i.e., **all four** of her numbers were chosen in the lottery.
- $ B $ = the event that **Jen wins a prize**, i.e., **at least two** of her numbers matched the number chosen in the lottery.

We are asked to find:

$$
P(A \mid B) = \frac{P(A \cap B)}{P(B)} = \frac{P(A)}{P(B)} \quad \text{(since } A \subseteq B\text{)}
$$

---

### Step 3: Calculate $ P(A) $

There is exactly **one** combination that matches all four of Jen's numbers. Since there are 210 total combinations, we have:

$$
P(A) = \frac{1}{210}
$$

---

### Step 4: Calculate $ P(B) $

Now, we need to compute the number of combinations where **at least two** of Jen's chosen numbers match theGClosely.

This is the **complement** of combinations that have fewer than two matches:

$$
\text{Combinations with at least 2 matches} = \text{Total combinations} - \text{Combinations with 0 matches} - \text{Combinations with 1 match}
$$

We calculate each:

- **Combinations with 0 matches**: Jen has 4 numbers, so there are $ 10 - 4 = 6 $ other numbers in $ S $. We choose all 4 from these 6:

  $$
  \binom{6}{4} = 15
  $$

- **Combinations with 1 match**: Choose 1 number from Jen's set, and the other 3 from the remaining 6:

  $$
  \binom{4}{1} \cdot \binom{6}{3} = 4 \cdot 20 = 80
  $$

- So the number of combinations with **at least 2 matches** is:

  $$
  210 - 15 - 80 = 115
  $$

Therefore:

$$
P(B) = \frac{115}{210}
$$

---

### Step 5: Compute the Conditional Probability

$$
P(A \mid B) = \frac{P(A)}{P(B)} = \frac{1/210}{115/210} = \frac{1}{115}
$$
