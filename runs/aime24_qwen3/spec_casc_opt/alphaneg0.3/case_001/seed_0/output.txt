### **Step 1: Understand the setup**

Let $ s $ be Aya’s walking speed in km/h. Two different scenarios are given:

- **Scenario 1:**
  - Speed: $ s $ km/h
  - Total time: 4 hours including $ t $ minutes of coffee shop time
  - Therefore, **walking time** = $ 4 - \frac{t}{60} $ hours
  - Walking distance in this case is 9 km/h, so:
    $$
    9 = s \left(4 - \frac{t}{60} \right)
    \tag{1}
    $$

- **Scenario 2:**
  - Speed: $ s + 2 $ km/h
  - Total time: 2 hours and 24 minutes = 2.4 hours
  - Again, **walking time** = $ 2.4 - \frac{t}{60} $ hours
  - Walking distance is still 9 km, so:
    $$
    9 = (s + 2) \left(2.4 - \frac{t}{60} \right)
    \tag{2}
    $$

$$
s = \frac{9}{4 - \frac{t}{60} }
\tag{1a}
$$

From (2):
$$
s + 2 = \frac{9}{2.4 - \frac{t}{60} }
\tag{2a}
$$

Substitute (1a) into (2a):
$$
\frac{9}{4 - \frac{t}{60} } + 2 = \frac{9}{2.4 - \frac{t}{60} }
$$

$$

9.6 - 6.4x + x^2 = 7.2
$$

$$
x^2 - 6.4x + 9.6 = 7.2
$$

$$
x^2 - 6.4x + 2.4 = 0
$$

Use the quadratic formula:

$$
x = \frac{6.4 \pm \sqrt{6.4^2 - 4 \cdot 1 \cdot 2.4}}{2}
$$

$$
x = \frac{6.4 \pm \sqrt{40.96 - 9.6}}{2} = \frac{6.4 \pm \sqrt{31.36}}{2}
$$

$$
x = \frac{6.4 \pm 5.6}{2}
$$

So:

- $ x = \frac{6.4 + 5.6}{2} = 6 $ (not valid since walk time becomes negative)
- $ x = \frac{6.4 - 5.6}{2} = 0.4 $

Thus, $ x = 0.4 $, and since $ x = \frac{t}{60} $, this gives:

$$
t = 60 \cdot 0.4 = 24 \text{ minutes}
$$

---

### **Step 5: Find walking time at $ s + \frac{1}{2} $ km/h**

From earlier, in the first case:

$$
s = \frac{9}{4 - x} = \frac{9}{4 - 0.4} = \frac{9}{3.6} = 2.5 \text{ km/h}
$$

So:

$$
s + \frac{1}{2} = 2.5 + 0.5 = 3.0 \text{ km/h}
$$

Walking time:

$$
\frac{9}{3.0} = 3 \text{ hours}
$$

Add the constant coffee shop time $ t = 24 $ minutes:

$$
3 \text{ hours} + 24 \text{ minutes} = 180 + 24 = 204 \text{ minutes}
$$

\boxed{204}
