$$

$$
ac + bd = 0

### **Step 2: Expression for $ BD^2 $**

Diagonal $ BD $ = distance between $B = (c, d)$ and $D = (-c, -d)$:

$$
BD^2 = (c - (-c))^2 + (d - (-d))^2 = (2c)^2 + (2d)^2 = 4(c^2 + d^2)
$$
