This is a key formula. Let’s denote it as:
$$
\frac{1}{\sin(\theta/2)} = 1 + \frac{2r(n - 1)}{r_{\text{tri}} - r} \tag{1}
$$

---

### Step 3: Apply to Both Configurations

We apply equation (1) to both cases, with:

- First case: $ n = 8 $, $ r = 34 $
- Second case: $ n = 2024 $, $ r = 1 $

For each case, plug into equation (1):

#### Case 1 (n = 8, r = 34):
$$
\frac{1}{\sin(\theta/2)} = 1 + \frac{2(34)(7)}{r_{\text{tri}} - 34} = 1 + \frac{476}{r_{\text{tri}} - 34} \tag{2}
$$

#### Case 2 (n = 2024, r = 1):
$$
\frac{1}{\sin(\theta/2)} = 1 + \frac{2(1)(2023)}{r_{\text{tri}} - 1} = 1 + \frac{4046}{r_{\text{tri}} - 1} \tag{3}
$$

Since both expressions equal $ \frac{1}{\sin(\theta/2)} $, we equate (2) and (3):

$$
1 + \frac{476}{r_{\text{tri}} - 34} = 1 + \frac{4046}{r_{\text{tri}} - 1}
$$

Subtract 1 from both sides:

$$
\frac{476}{r_{\text{tri}} - 34} = \frac{4046}{r_{\text{tri}} - 1}
$$

Cross-multiplying:

$$
476(r_{\text{tri}} - 1) = 4046(r_{\text{tri}} - 34)
$$

Expand both sides:

$$
476r_{\text{tri}} - 476 = 4046r_{\text{tri}} - 137,564
$$

Rearrange and simplify:

$$
476r_{\text{tri}} - 4046r_{\text{tri}} = -137,564 + 476
$$

$$
-3570r_{\text{tri}} = -137,088
$$

$$
r_{\text{tri}} = \frac{137,088}{3570}
$$

---

### Step 4: Simplify the Fraction

To simplify $ \frac{137,088}{3570} $, compute the GCD of numerator and denominator:

- $ \frac{137,088}{3570} = \frac{192}{5} $ after simplification.

So:
$$
r_{\text{tri}} = \frac{192}{5}
$$
And since 192 and 5 are coprime:
$$
m = 192, \quad n = 5 \Rightarrow m + n = 197
$$

---

### Final Answer:

$$
\boxed{197}
