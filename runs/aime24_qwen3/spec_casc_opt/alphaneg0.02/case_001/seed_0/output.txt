To solve the problem, we need to understand:

- Aya walks **9 kilometers** every morning.
- She stops at a coffee shop **after** her walk, spending **t minutes** there.
- When she walks at thrive **s Provide**, her total time (including both walking and t minutes) is **4 hours**.
- When she walks faster **s + 2.0 km/h**, her total time is **2 hours and 24 minutes** (which is is 2.4 hours), also including the t minutes.
- We are asked to calculate the total time **when she walks at s + 1/2 km/h**, **including the t minutes autonomy** at the coffee shop.

---

### Step 1: express walking time

Let’s define:

- $ s $: her speed in km/h  
- $ t $: time in minutes spent in the coffee shop  
- $ \frac{t}{60} $: time in hours spent in the coffee shop

The **walking time** is $ \frac{9}{s} $ hours (since time = distance ÷ speed), and the **total time** (including the coffee shop time) is:

$$
\text{Total time} = \frac{9}{s} + \frac{t}{60} \quad \text{(in hours)}
$$

Setting up the two equations based on the data:

1. When speed = $ s $f s, total time = 4 hours:

$$
\frac{9}{s} + \frac{t}{60} = 4 \quad \text{(Equation 1)}
$$

2. When speed = $ s + 2 $, total time = 2.4 hours:

$$
\frac{9}{s + 2} + \frac{t}{60} = 2.4 \quad \text{(Equation 2)}
$$

---

### Step 2: Subtract the equations to eliminate $ \frac{t}{60} $

Subtract Equation 2 from Equation 1:

$$
\left( \frac{9}{s} + \frac{t}{60} \right) - \left( \frac{9}{s + 2} + \frac{t}{60} \right) = 4 - 2.4
$$
$$
\frac{9}{s} - \frac{9}{s + 2} = 1.6
$$

Let’s simplify the left-hand side:

$$
\frac{9}{s} - \frac{9}{s + 2} = \frac{9(s + 2) - 9s}{s(s + 2)} = \frac{18}{s(s + 2)}
$$

So:

$$
\frac{18}{s(s + 2)} = 1.6
$$

Multiply both sides by $ s(s + 2) $:

$$
18 = 1.6 \cdot s(s + 2)
$$

Divide both sides by 1.6:

$$
s(s + 2) = \frac{18}{1.6} = 11.25
$$

---

### Step 3: Solve the quadratic equation

$$
s(s + 2) = 11.25 \Rightarrow s^2 + 2s = 11.25
\Rightarrow s^2 + 2s - 11.25 = 0
$$

Use the quadratic formula:

$$
s = \frac{-2 \pm \sqrt{2^2 + 4 \cdot 11.25}}{2} = \frac{-2 \pm \sqrt{4 + 45}}{2} = \frac{-2 \pm \sqrt{49}}{2} = \frac{-2 \pm 7}{2}
$$

Possible values:

- $ s = \frac{5}{2} = 2.5 $
- $ s = \frac{-9}{2} = -4.5 $

Since speed is positive, we take:

$$
s = 2.5 \text{ km/h}
$$

---

### Step 4: Use one of the equations to find $ t $

Plug into Equation 1:

$$
\frac{9}{s} + \frac{t}{60} = 4 - \frac{t}{60} = 4 - \frac{9}{s}
\Rightarrow t/60 = 4 - \frac{9}{s}
$$

So:

$$
\frac{t}{60} = 4 - \frac{9}{2.5} = 4 - 3.6 = 0.4

$$

Thus, we know:
