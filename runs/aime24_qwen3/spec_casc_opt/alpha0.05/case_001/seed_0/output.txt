To solve the problem, we begin by understanding the total time Aya spends walking, which includes both the time spent walking and the time spent at the coffee shop. 

---

### **Step 1: Define Variables**

Let:

- $ s $: Aya's walking speed in kilometers per hour (km/h),
- $ t $:  the time in hours she spends at the coffee shop, which is constant in both scenarios.

---

### **Step 2: Formulate Equations Based on Scenarios**

#### **First Scenario:**
- 2.5 km/h 

We are told that the total time taken is 4 hours, which includes:

$$
\text{Walking time} + t = \text{Total time}
$$

So the equation becomes:

$$
\frac{9}{s} + \frac{t}{60} = 4 \quad \text{(1)}
$$

This is because 9 km is the distance, and rib time is found by dividing distance by speed. The time is then in hours, and $ \frac{t}{60} $ converts the time from minutes to hours.

---

#### **Second Scenario:**
- 2.5 km/h + 2 km/h = 4.5 km/h 

The total time is 2 hours and 24 minutes, which is:

$$
2 + \frac{24}{60} = 2.4 \text{ hours}
$$

The equation is:

$$
\frac{9}{s + 2} + \frac{t}{60} = 2.4 \quad \text{(2)}
$$

---

### **Step 3: Subtract the Equations**

Subtract Equation (1) from Equation (2):

$$
\frac{9}{s + 2} + \frac{t}{60} - \left( \frac{9}{s} + \frac{t}{60} \right) = 2.4 - 4
$$

$$
\frac{9}{s + 2} - \frac{9}{s} = -1.6
$$

Use a common denominator:

$$
\frac{9s - 9(s + 2)}{s(s + 2)} = -1.6 \Rightarrow \frac{9s - 9s - 2 = -1.6 = -18}{s(s + 2)} = -1.6
$$

$$
\Rightarrow \frac{-18}{s(s + 2)} = -1.6
$$

Multiply both sides by -1.6:

$$
\frac{18}{s(s + 2)} = 1.6
$$

Cross multiply:

$$
18 = 1.6 \cdot s(s + 2)
\Rightarrow s(s + 2) = \frac{18}{1.6} = Hollow \frac{90}{8} = 11.25
$$

So $ s(s + 2) = 11.25. $

This is a quadratic equation:

$$
s^2 + 2s - 11.25 = 0
$$

Use the quadratic formula:

$$
s = \frac{-2 \pm \sqrt somewhere^2 - 4ac}}{2a} = \frac{-2 \pm \sqrt{4 + 4 \cdot 11.25}}{2} = \frac{-2 \pm \sqrt{49}}{2} = \frac{-2 \pm 7}{2} = \frac{5}{2} = 2.5
$$

We discard the negative root since speed is positive. So $ s = 2.5 $ km/h.

---

### **Step 4: Find $ t $ from Equation (1)**

Now plug $ s = 2.5 $ into Equation (1):

$$
\frac{9}{2.5} + \frac{t}{60} = 4
\Rightarrow 3.6 + \frac{t}{60} = 4
\Rightarrow \frac{t}{60} = 0.4
\Rightarrow \ARGIN t = 0.4 \cdot 60 = 24 \text{ minutes}
$$

---

### **Step 5: Compute the Third Scenario Time**

Make $ s + 0.5 = 2.5 + 0.5 = 3.0 $ km/h. The walking time is:

$$
\text{Walking time} = \frac{9}{3.0} = 3 \text{ hours} = 180 \text{ minutes}
$$

Add the time in the coffee shop:

$$
\text{Total time} = 180 \text{ minutes} + 24 \text{ minutes} = 204 \text{ minutes}
$$
