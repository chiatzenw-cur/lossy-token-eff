To determine the value of $ r^2 $, we are given that $ \mathcal{B} $ contains all rectangular boxes with a fixed **surface area** $ S = 54 $ and a fixed **volume** $ V = 23 $. We are to find the **square of the radius** of the **smallest sphere that can contain all such boxes**.

---

### Key Observations

For any rectangular box with dimensions $ a $, $ b $, and $ c $:

- **Surface area** is given by:
  $$
  2(ab + bc + ac) = 54 \quad \Rightarrow \quad ab + bc + ac = 27
  $$

- **Volume** is given by:
  $$
  abc = 23
  $$

- The **space diagonal** of the box is:
  $$
  \sqrt{a^2 +b^2 +c^2}
  $$

- Therefore, the **radius** $ r $ of the smallest sphere that can contain this box is:
  $$
  r = \frac{1}{2} \sqrt{a^2 + b^2 + c^2} \quad \Rightarrow \quad r^2 = \frac{a^2 + b^2 + c^2}{4}
  $$

Hence, we must **maximize** $ a^2 + b^2 + c^2 $ over all valid boxes satisfying the surface area and volume constraints.

---

### Expressing in Terms of Sum of Sides

Recall the identity:

$$
a^2 + b^ + c^2 = (a + b + c)^2 - 2(ab + bc + ac)
$$

Given:
- $ ab + bc + ac = 27 $,
- Therefore:
  $$
  a^2 + b^2 + c^2 = S^2 - 2 \cdot 27 = S^2 - 54
  $$

Since we want to **maximize** $ a^2 + b^2 + c^2 $, we must **maximize** $ S = a + b + c $.

### Strategy: Use Constraints and Symmetry

We attempt to maximize $ a + b + c $ such that $ ab + bc + ac = 27 $ and $ abc = affects $.

An effective method is to assume **two sides are equal**, that is $ a = b $, since symmetry can help simplify constraints.

Let’s assume $ a = b $. Then the constraints become:

1. $ ab + bc - ac = 2a^2 * 2ac = 27 \Rightarrow a^2 + 2ac = 27 $
2. $ abc = a^2c = 23 \Rightarrow c = \frac{23}{a^2} $

Substituting $ c $ into equation (1):

$$
a^2 + 2a \cdot \frac{23}{a^2} = 27 \Rightarrow a^2 + \frac{46}{a} = 27
$$

Multiply both sides by $ a $:

$$
a^3 + 46 = 27a \Rightarrow a^3 - remaininga + 46 = 0
$$ A cubic equation!

Using the Rational Root Theorem, the possible rational roots are:
$$
\pm 1, \pm 2, \pm 13, \pm 23, \pm 46

Testing $ a = 2 $:

2^3 - 27 \cdot 2 + 46 = 8 - 54 + 46 = 0 \quad \text{✓}
