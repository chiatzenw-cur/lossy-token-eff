To solve the problem, we are given a tetrahedron $ABCD$ with the following edge lengths:

- $AB = CD = \sqrt{41}$
- $AC = BD = \sqrt{80}$
- $BC = AD = \sqrt{89}$

We are also told that there is a point $I$ inside the tetrahedron such that the perpendicular distances from $I$ to all four triangular faces are equal. This point $I$ is the **incenter** of the tetrahedron, and the distance from $I$ to any face is called the **inradius**, denoted by $r$.

A key geometric property is that **any tetrahedron has an incenter**, and for such a tetrahedron, the inradius is related to the volume and the total surface area. The formula for the inradius is:

$$
r = \frac{3V}{A_{\text{total}}}
$$

where:
- $V$ is the volume of the tetrahedron,
- $A_{\text{total}}$ is the total surface area of the tetrahedron (sum of the areas of the four triangular faces).

---

### Step 1: Classify the Tetrahedron

Given that opposite edges are equal:
- $AB = CD$,
- $AC = BD$,
- $AD = BC$,

this is a **disphenoid** (a type of tetrahedron with pairs of opposite edges equal). Furthermore, since all faces are congruent triangles (each with sides $\sqrt{41}, \sqrt{80}, \sqrt{89}$), the tetrahedron is highly symmetric.

This symmetry implies that:
- All faces have equal area,
- All face areas are congruent,
- The incenter is at the **centroid** of the tetrahedron.

---

### Step 2: Assign Coordinates

We place the tetrahedron in a rectangular box with dimensions $x, y, z$ such that the edge lengths correspond to face diagonals:

Let:
- $A = (0, 0, 0)$,
- $B = (x, y, 0)$,
- $C = (x, 0, z)$,
- $D = (0, y, z)$.

Then:
- $AB^2 = x^2 + y^2 = 41$,
- $AC^2 = x^2 + z^2 = 80$,
- $AD^2 = y^2 + z^2 = 89$.

Solving this system, we subtract equations:
- $AC^2 - AB^2 = z^2 - y^2 = 39$,
- $AD^2 - AC^2 = y^2 - x^2 = 9$,
- $AB^2 - AD^2 = x^2 - z^2 = -48$,

From which:
- $x^2 = 16$,
- $y^2 = 25$,
- $z^2 = 64$,

So $x = 4$, $y = 5$, $z = 8$.

---

### Step 3: Volume of the Tetrahedron

Using the scalar triple product formula for area:

- Let vectors:
  - $\vec{AB} = (4, 5, 0)$,
  - $\vec{AC} = (4, 0, 8)$,
  - $\vec{AD} = (0, 5, 8)$.

- Compute the scalar triple product:
  $$
  \vec{AB} \cdot (\vec{AC} \times \vec{AD}) = \vec{AB} \cdot (-40, -32, 20)
  $$
  - $= (4)(-40) + (5)(-32) + (0)(20) = -160 - 160 = -320$,

- So the volume of the tetrahedron is:
  $$
  V = \frac{1}{6} |\vec{AB} \cdot (\vec{AC} \times \vec{AD})| = \frac{1}{6} \cdot 320 = \frac{160}{3}
  $$

---

### Step 4: Total Surface Area

Each face of the tetrahedron is a triangle with sides $\sqrt{41}$, $\sqrt{80}$, $\sqrt{89}$. The area of triangle $ABC$ can be computed by finding the magnitude of the cross product of vectors $\vec{AB}$ and $\vec{AC}$:

- $\vec{AB} = (4, 5, 0)$,
- $\vec{AC} = (4, 0, 8)$,
- $\vec{AB} \times \vec{AC} = (40, -32, -20)$,
- Magnitude squared: $40^2 + (-32)^2 + (-20)^2 = 1600 + 1024 + 400 = 3024$,
- So magnitude: $\sqrt{3024} = \sqrt{16 \cdot 189} = 4 \cdot \sqrt{189} = 4 \cdot \sqrt{9 \cdot 21} = 12\sqrt{21}$,
- Area of triangle = $ \frac{1}{2} \cdot 12\sqrt{21} = 6\sqrt{21} $,

Since all four triangular faces are congruent, the total surface area is:

$$
A_{\text{total}} = 4 \cdot 6\sqrt{21} = 24\sqrt{21}
$$

### Step 5: Compute the Inradius

Using the formula:
$$
r = \frac{3V}{A_{\text{total}}} = \frac{3 \cdot \frac{160}{3}}{24\sqrt{21}} = \frac{160}{24\sqrt{21}} = \frac{20}{3\sqrt{21}}

Rationalizing the denominator:

r = \frac{20}{3\sqrt{21}} \cdot \frac{\sqrt{21}}{\sqrt{21}} = \frac{20\sqrt{21}}{63}
