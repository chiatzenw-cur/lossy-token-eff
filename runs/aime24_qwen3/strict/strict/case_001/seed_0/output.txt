To determine the total time Aya takes for her walk when walking at a speed of $ s + \frac{1}{2} $ km/h, we begin by analyzing the information provided.

### Step 1: Define Variables and Equations
We are given:

- Aya walks 9 km each morning.
- Her total time (including coffee shop time) is 4 hours at a speed of $ s $ km/h.
- Her total time is 2 hours 24 minutes (or 2.4 hours) at a speed of $ s + 2 $ km/h.
- We are to find the total time when her speed is $ s + \frac{1}{2} $ km/h.

Let $ t $ be the number of **minutes** spent at the coffee shop. Convert all times to **hours** for consistency with the speed (km/h) units.

Then, the total time (in hours) for each case is:
- First case: $ \frac{9}{s} + \frac{t}{60} = 4 $
- Second case: $ \frac{9}{s + 2} + \frac{t}{60} = 2.4 $

---

### Step 2: Solve the System of Equations

Subtract the second equation from the first:

$$
\left( \frac{9}{s} + \frac{t}{60} \right) - \left( \frac{9}{s + 2} + \frac{t}{60} \right) = 4 - 2.4
$$

Simplifying:

$$
\frac{9}{s} - \frac{9}{s + 2} = 1.6
$$

Use the common denominator:

$$
\frac{9(s + 2) - 9s}{s(s + 2)} = 1.6
\Rightarrow \frac{18}{s(s + 2)} = 1.6
$$

Convert 1.6 to a fraction: $ 1.6 = \frac{8}{5} $

Cross-multiplying:

$$
18 \cdot 5 = 8 \cdot s(s + 2)
\Rightarrow 90 = 8s^2 + 16s
$$

Simplify:

$$
8s^2 + 16s - 90 = 0
\Rightarrow 4s^2 + 8s - 45 = 0
$$

Use the quadratic formula:

$$
s = \frac{-b \pm \sqrt{b^2 - 4ac}}{2a} = \frac{-8 \pm \sqrt{64 + 720}}{8} = \frac{-8 \pm 28}{8}
$$

$$
s = \frac{20}{8} = 2.5 \ \text{(reject the negative root)}
$$

Thus, $ s = 2.5 $ km/h.

---

### Step 3: Solve for Coffee Shop Time $ t $

Use the first equation:

$$
\frac{9}{s} + \frac{t}{60} = 4
\Rightarrow \frac{9}{2.5} + \frac{t}{60} = 4
\Rightarrow 3.6 + \frac{t}{60} = 4
\Rightarrow \frac{t}{60} = 0.4
\Rightarrow t = 24 \ \text{minutes}
$$

So, Aya spends **24 minutes** at the coffee shop.

Now, compute the new speed $ s + \frac{1}{2} = 2.5 + 0.5 = 3 $ km/h.

$$
