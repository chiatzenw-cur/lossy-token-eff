To determine how long Aya’s walk takes when she walks at $ s + \frac{1}{2} $ kilometers per hour—**including** $ t $ minutes spent at the coffee shop—we begin by analyzing the information provided in two scenarios.

---

### Step 1: Understand the Given Information

- **Distance:** 9 km  
- **Total time in first case:** 4 hours = 240 minutes  
- **Total time in second case:** 2 hours and 24 minutes = 144 minutes  
- The coffee shop time is **common and constant** (denoted $ t $ minutes) in both scenarios  
- In each case, the total time includes both walking time and the $ t $ minutes spent at the coffee shop

Let’s define:
- $ s $: Aya’s constant walking speed (in km/h)
- $ t $: Time (in minutes) spent at the coffee shop

Let’s also define $ x = \frac{t}{60} $, to express everything in hours, where $ x $ is the **fractional part of hours spent at the coffee shop**.

---

### Step 2: Set Up the Equations

#### First Scenario (speed $ s $)
- Walking time = $ 4 - x $ hours  
- Total distance: $ 9 = s(4 - x) $

#### Second Scenario (speed $ s + 2 $)
- Walking time = $ 2.4 - x $ hours  
- Total distance: $ 9 = (s + 2)(2.4 - x) $

Now we have the system of equations:
$$
\begin{cases}
s(4 - x) = 9 \\
(s + 2)(2.4 - x) = 9
\end{cases}
$$

---

### Step 3: Solve the System

**From the first equation:**
$$
s = \frac{9}{4 - x}
$$

**Substitute into the second equation:**
$$
\left(\frac{9}{4 - x} + 2 \right)(2.4 - x) = 9
$$

Simplify the left side:
$$
\left(\frac{9 + 2(4 - x)}{4 - x}\right)(2.4 - x) = \left(\frac{9 + 8 - 2x}{4 - x}\right)(2.4 - x) = \frac{17 - 2x}{4 - x}(2.4 - x)
$$

Now multiply both sides by $ 4 - x $:
$$
(17 - 2x)(2.4 - x) = 9(4 - x)
$$

Expand both sides:
- Left side:
  $$
  (17)(2.4) - 17x - 4.8x + 2x^2 = 40.8 - 21.8x + 2x^2
  $$
- Right side:
  $$
  36 - 9x
  $$

$$
40.8 - 21.8x + 2x^2 = 36 - 9x
$$

Bring all terms to one side:
$$
2x^2 - 12.8x + 4.8 = 0
$$

$$

$$

$$
