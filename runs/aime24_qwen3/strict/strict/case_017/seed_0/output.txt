\Rightarrow 480 = b^2 + c^2 - \frac{14}{13}bc
$$

We also know that:

$$
b + c = 2s - a = 2 \cdot 6\sqrt{30} - 4\sqrt{30} = 8\sqrt{30}
$$

Now use the identity:

$$
(b + c)^2 = b^2 + c^2 + 2bc
\Rightarrow 64 \cdot 30 = b^2 + c^2 + 2bc
\Rightarrow 1920 = b^2 + c^2 + 2bc
$$

Substitute $ b^2 + c^2 $ from the Law of Cosines:

$$
480 + \frac{14}{13}bc = 1920 - 2bc
\Rightarrow \frac{14}{13}bc + 2bc = 1920 - 480
\Rightarrow \left(\frac{14}{13} + \frac{26}{13}\right) bc = 1440
\Rightarrow \frac{40}{13} bc = 1440
\Rightarrow bc = \frac{1440 \cdot 13}{40} = 468
$$
