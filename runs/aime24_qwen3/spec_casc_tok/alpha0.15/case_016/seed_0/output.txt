### Step 1: Understand the Structure

There are **900 residents** in Aimeville. The key observation is that **every resident owns a bag of candy hearts**. This means that **candy hearts (C)** are **common to all residents**.

Thus, the set of items to be considered are:

- D: diamond ring
- G: golf clubs
- S: garden spade
- C: candy hearts (everyone has it)

But since **everyone has C**, the statement regarding "owning exactly two of these things" refers to owning **C plus one of D, G, or S**.

**Similarly**, "owning exactly three" implies **C plus two of D, G, or S**. And "owning all four" means **C plus D, G, and S**.

---

### Step 2: Define Variables

Let’s define the following:

- $ a $: residents owning only D
- $ b $: residents owning only G
- $ c $: residents owning only S
- $ d $: residents owning D and G but not S
- $ e $: residents owning D and S but not G
- $ f $: residents owning G and S but not D
- $ g $: residents owning all three D, G, and S (which also equates to owning all four items, since everyone has C)
- $ h $: residents owning just C and no other items

Since everyone has a candy heart (C), all residents fall into one of the following categories:
- Only C (h)
- C and one of D, G, S (a + b + c)
- C and two of D, G, S (d + e + f)
- C and all three D, G, S (g)

So total residents:
$$
h + (a + b + c) + (d + e + f) + g = 900
$$

We are told:

- $ a + b + c = 437 $ (exactly two of the four items = C + one of D, G, S)
- $ d + e + f = 234 $ (exactly three of the four items = C + two of D, G, S)

So:
$$
900 = h + 437 + 234 + g \\
900 = h + g + 671 \\
\Rightarrow h + g = 229 \quad \text{(Equation 1)}
$$

### Step 3: Use Inclusion-Exclusion for D, G, S

- Number of D owners = $ a + d + e + g = 195 $
- Number of G owners = $ b + d + f + g = 367 $
- Number of S owners = $ c + e + f + g = 562 $

$$
