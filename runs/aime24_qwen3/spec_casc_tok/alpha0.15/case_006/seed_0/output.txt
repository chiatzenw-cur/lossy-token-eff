### **Step 1: Volume of the Tetrahedron**

We use a **symmetric coordinate system** to represent the tetrahedron and compute its volume. Assign the vertices as:

- $A = (2, \frac{5}{2}, 4)$
- $B = (-2, -\frac{5}{2}, 4)$
- $C = (-2, \frac{5}{2}, -4)$
- $D = (2, -\frac{5}{2}, -4)$

This setup ensures that the edge lengths and symmetries match the problem’s conditions.

Compute vectors:
- $\vec{AB} = (-4, -5, 0)$
- $\vec{AC} = (-4, 0, -8)$
- $\vec{AD} = (0, -5, -8)$

Compute the **cross product** $\vec{AC} \times \vec{AD}$:

$$
\vec{AC} \times \vec{AD} = \begin{vmatrix}
\mathbf{i} & \mathbf{j} & \mathbf{k} \\
-4 & 0 & -8 \\
0 & -5 & -8
\end{vmatrix}
= \left(0(-8) - (-8)(-5)\right) \mathbf{i}
- \left((-4)(-8) - (-8)(0)\right) \mathbf{j}
+ \left((-4)(-5) - 0(0)\right) \mathbf{k}
$$

$$
= (-40) \mathbf{i} - (32) \mathbf{j} + (20) \mathbf{k} = (-40, -32, 20)
$$

Now compute the **dot product** $\vec{AB} \cdot (\vec{AC} \times \vec{AD})$:

$$
(-4, -5, 0) \cdot (-40, -32, 20) = 160 + 160 + 0 = 320
$$

Volume of the tetrahedron is:

$$
V = \frac{1}{6} \|\vec{AB} \cdot (\vec{AC} \times \vec{AD})\| = \frac{1}{6} \cdot 320 = \frac{160}{3}
$$

So, the volume is:

$$
V = \frac{160}{3}
$$
