---

### **Step 1: Understanding the Problem**

Aya walks **9 kilometers** each morning and **stops at a coffee shop** for **t minutes**. Her walking speed changes from:

- **First Scenario**: $ s $ km/h, with total time = **4 hours** (including t minutes)
- **Second Scenario**: $ s + 2 $ km/h, with total time = **2 hours and 24 minutes** (also including t minutes)

We are to find how **many minutes** the walk takes when she walks at speed $ s + \frac{1}{2} $ km/h, **including** the t minutes at the coffee shop.

---

### **Step 2: Set Up Equations**

Let’s express the total time in consistent units (hours):

- **First case**: 4 hours = 4 hr
- **Second case**: 2 hours and 24 minutes = $ 2 + \frac{24}{60} = 2.4 $ hours

Let $ t $ be the constant time (in **hours**) Aya spends in the coffee shop.

Then the actual **walking time** is:

- **First case**: $ 4 - \frac{t}{60} $ hr
- **Second case**: $ 2.4 - \frac{t}{60} $ hr

The walking time can also be written as:

- **First case**: $ \frac{9}{s} $ hr
- **Second case**: $ \frac{9}{s + 2} $ hr

Thus, we have two equations:

$$
\frac{9}{s} = 4 - \frac{t}{60} \tag{1}
$$
$$
\frac{9}{s + 2} = 2.4 - \frac{t}{60} \tag{2}
$$

Subtracting (2) from (1):

$$
\frac{9}{s} - \frac{9}{s + 2} = (4 - \frac{t}{60}) - (2.4 - \frac{t}{60}) = 1.6
$$

So,

$$
\frac{9}{s} - \frac{9}{s + 2} = 1.6 \tag{3}
$$

---

### **Step 3: Solve for $ s $**

$$
\frac{9}{s} - \frac{9}{s + 2} = 1.6
$$

Find a common denominator:

$$
\frac{9(s + 2) - 9s}{s(s + 2)} = 1.6
$$

$$
\frac{18}{s(s + 2)} = 1.6
$$

Convert 1.6 to a fraction: $ 1.6 = \frac{8}{5} $

$$
\frac{18}{s(s + 2)} = \frac{8}{5}
$$

Cross-multiplying:

$$
18 \cdot 5 = 8 \cdot s(s + 2)
\Rightarrow 90 = 8s^2 + 16s
$$

Rearranging:

$$
4s^2 + 8s - 45 = 0 \tag{4}
$$

Solving this quadratic using the quadratic formula:

$$
s = \frac{-8 \pm \sqrt{8^2 - 4(4)(-45)}}{2(4)}
= \frac{-8 \pm \sqrt{64 + 720}}{8}
= \frac{-8 \pm \sqrt{784}}{8}
= \frac{-8 \pm 28}{8}
$$

Two possible solutions:

$$
s = \frac{20}{8} = 2.5 \quad \text{or} \quad s = \frac{-36}{8} = -4.5
$$

Since speed cannot be negative, we have:

$$
s = 2.5 \text{ km/h}
$$

### **Step 4: Solve for $ t $**

Use equation (1):

$$
\frac{9}{2.5} = 4 - \frac{t}{60}
\Rightarrow 3.6 = 4 - \frac{t}{60}
$$

So:

$$
\frac{t}{60} = 4 - 3.6 = 0.4 \Rightarrow t = 0.4 \times 60 = 24 \text{ minutes}
$$

### **Step 5: Solve for the New Total Time**

Aya now walks at speed:

$$
s + \frac{1}{2} = 2.5 + 0.5 = 3 \text{ km/h}

$$
\frac{9}{3} = 3 \text{ hours}
