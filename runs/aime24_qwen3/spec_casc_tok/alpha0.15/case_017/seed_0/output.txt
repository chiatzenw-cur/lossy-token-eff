### Step 1: Use Euler's Formula and Geometry

$$
OI^2 = R(R - 2r)
$$

$$
$$
OI = \sqrt{13}
$$

$$
IA^2 = OA^2 - OI^2 = 13^2 - 13 = 169 - 13 = 156
$$

Now, using the known identity for the distance $ IA $ from the incenter to vertex $ A $:

$$
IA = \frac{r}{\sin(A/2)}

$$

Solving:

$$
\sin^2(A/2) = \frac{36}{156} = \frac{3}{13} \Rightarrow \sin(A/2) = \sqrt{\frac{3}{13}}

$$
