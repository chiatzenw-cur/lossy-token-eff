To solve the problem, we analyze the two scenarios described:

---

### **Step 1: Define Variables**

Let:

- $ s $ = walking speed in km/h
- $ t $ = time (in minutes) spent at the coffee shop
- $ D = 9 $ km (the distance of the walk)

---

### **Step 2: Translate to Equations**

#### **First Scenario:**
Aya walks at $ s $ km/h, and the total time including the coffee shop is **4 hours**. So:

$$
\text{Walking time} = 4 \text{ hours} - \frac{t}{60} \text{ hours}
$$

But walking time is also:

$$
\frac{9}{s} \text{ hours}
$$

Therefore:

$$
\frac{9}{s} = 4 - \frac{t}{60} \quad \text{(1)}
$$

---

#### **Second Scenario:**
She walks at $ s + 2 $ km/h, and the total time is **2 hours and 24 minutes** = 2.4 hours. So:

$$
\text{Walking time} = 2.4 - \frac{t}{60} \text{ hours}
$$

Which gives:

$$
\frac{9}{s + 2} = 2.4 - \frac{t}{60} \quad \text{(2)}
$$

---

### **Step 3: Eliminate $ t $**

Let’s express $ t $ from both equations:

#### From Equation (1):
$$
\frac{t}{60} = 4 - \frac{9}{s} \Rightarrow t = 60 \left(4 - \frac{9}{s} \right) = 240 - \frac{540}{s}
$$

#### From Equation (2):
$$
\frac{t}{60} = 2.4 - \frac{9}{s + 2} \Rightarrow t = 60 \left(2.4 - \frac{9}{s + 2} \right) = 144 - \frac{540}{s + 2}
$$

Set both expressions for $ t $ equal:

$$
240 - \frac{540}{s} = 144 - \frac{540}{s + 2}
$$

---

### **Step 4: Solve for $ s $**

Move all terms to one side:

$$
240 - 144 = \frac{540}{s} - \frac{540}{s + 2}
\Rightarrow 96 = 540 \left( \frac{1}{s} - \frac{1}{s + 2} \right)
$$

Note that:

$$
\frac{1}{s} - \frac{1}{s + 2} = \frac{2}{s(s + 2)}
$$

So:

$$
96 = 540 \cdot \frac{2}{s(s + 2)} = \frac{1080}{s(s + 2)}
\Rightarrow s(s + 2) = \frac{1080}{96} = 11.25
$$

Solve the quadratic:

$$
s^2 + 2s = 11.25 \Rightarrow s^2 + 2s - 11.25 = 0
$$

Use the quadratic formula:

$$
s = \frac{-2 \pm \sqrt{4 + 45}}{2} = \frac{-2 \pm 7}{2}
\Rightarrow s = 2.5 \text{ (since speed cannot be negative)}
$$

---

### **Step 5: Compute $ t $**

Plug $ s = 2.5 $ into Equation (1):

$$
\frac{9}{2.5} = 3.6 \text{ hours} = 3 \text{ hours and } 36 \text{ minutes} = 216 \text{ minutes}
$$

So:

$$
\frac{t}{60} = 4 - 3.6 = 0.4 \text{ hours} = 24 \text{ minutes}
\Rightarrow t = 24 \text{ minutes}
$$

---

### **Step 6: Find Total Time at $ s + \frac{1}{2} = 3 $ km/h**

Walking time at 3 km/h:

$$
\frac{9}{3} = 3 \text{ hours} = 180 \text{ minutes}
$$

Add coffee shop time:

$$
\text{Total time} = 180 + 24 = 204 \text{ minutes}
$$

---

### **Final Answer**

$$
\boxed{204}
