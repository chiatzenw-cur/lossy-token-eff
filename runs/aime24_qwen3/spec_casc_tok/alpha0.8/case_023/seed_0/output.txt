5^2 + 7^2 + 9^2 + 9^2 = 25 + 49 + 81 + 81 = 236
$$

---

### Step 4: Verify There Are No Other Valid Configurations

We check other numbers of items $ n > 4 $ (e.g., 6, 8, ...) and find that constructing such a list that satisfies all conditions becomes impossible. Any attempt to satisfy the sum and the unique mode results in either:

- **mode not unique**, or
- **median in the list**, or
- **sum not equal to 30**

Similarly, for $ n = 2 $, we can't get a valid mode and sum simultaneously. So the only possible valid list is:

$$
\boxed{[5, 7, 9, 9]}
$$

---

### Final Answer

$$
\boxed{236}
$$
