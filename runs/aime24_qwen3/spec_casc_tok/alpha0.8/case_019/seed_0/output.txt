### **Step 2: Find the Equation of Segment $ AB $**

From the coordinates of $ A $ and $ B $, we find the slope:

$$
m = \frac{\frac{\sqrt{3}}{2} - 0}{0 - \frac{1}{2}} = -\sqrt{3}
$$

So, the line $ AB $ has equation:

$$
y = -\sqrt{3}x + \frac{\sqrt{3}}{2}
$$

This line segment also has length:

$$
\sqrt{ \left( \frac{1}{2} - 0 \right)^2 + \left( 0 - \frac{\sqrt{3}}{2} \right)^2 } = \sqrt{ \frac{1}{4} + \frac{3}{4} } = 1
$$

So, $ AB \in \mathcal{F} $, and hence is a member of the family of unit-length segments in the first quadrant with endpoints on the axes.

### **Step 3: The Envelope of the Family $ \mathcal{F} $**

The set of all such segments $ \overline{PQ} $, with $ P = (p, 0) $, $ Q = (0, q) $, and $ p^2 + q^2 = 1 $, form a family of chords of the unit circle in the first quadrant. The union of all these segments is the region covered by the family $ \mathcal{F} $, and the **envelope** of this family is the **astroid** given by:

$$
x^{2/3} + y^{2/3} = 1
$$

This envelope is the **boundary** of the region spanned by the segments, meaning that **points on the astroid** are the limiting points (i.e., they lie on **only one segment** in the family $ \mathcal{F} $, the one that is tangent to the astroid at that point).
