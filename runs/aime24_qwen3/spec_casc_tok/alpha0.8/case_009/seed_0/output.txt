### **Game Strategy and Winning/Losing Positions**

In such a turn-based game, positions can be classified as either **winning positions (N-positions)** or **losing positions (P-positions)**:

- **Winning Position (N):** The current player has a strategy to force a win regardless of the opponent's moves.
- **Losing Position (P):** No matter what the current player does, the opponent will be able to force a win.

We define the game as:
- On each turn, a player removes **1 or 4 tokens**.
- The player who **takes the last token** wins.

Our objective is to identify **losing positions (P-positions)**, because if the starting number of tokens is a P-position, **Alice** cannot guarantee a win, and thus **Bob** has a winning strategy.
