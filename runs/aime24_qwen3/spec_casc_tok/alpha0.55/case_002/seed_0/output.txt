### Step 1: Use the Power of a Point Theorem

Point $ D $ lies outside the circle $ \omega $, and since it’s the intersection of two tangents to the circle, the **Power of a Point** theorem tells us:

$$

$$

$$
DA = AP + PD = x + PD
$$
$$
