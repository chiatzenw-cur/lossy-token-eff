To solve the problem, we start by analyzing the given information:

- Aya walks **9 kilometers** every morning.
- When she walks at a speed of $ s $ km/h, the total time (including $ t $ minutes at the coffee shop) is **4 hours**.
- When she walks at a speed of $ s + 2 $ km/h, the total time including $ t $ minutes is **2 hours and 24 minutes**.
- We are to find the total time when she walks at a speed of $ s + \frac{1}{2} $ km/h, again including $ t $ minutes.

---

### Step 1: Convert Time to Hours (for Consistent Units)

We'll work in **hours** for the walking time, and convert the final answer to **minutes** at the end.

Let:
- $ t $ be the time in **minutes** spent at the coffee shop.
- $ u = \frac{t}{60} $, so we can express time in hours.

Using the formula:

$$
\text{Distance} = \text{Speed} \times \text{Walking Time}
$$

We can write two equations based on the given situations:

1. At speed $ s $ km/h:
   $$
   9 = s \left(4 - \frac{t}{60} \right)
   $$

2. At speed $ s + 2 $ km/h:
   $$
   9 = (s + 2)\left(2.4 - \frac{t}{60} \right)
   $$

Substituting $ u = \frac{t}{60} $, the equations become:

$$
9 = s(4 - u) \quad \text{(1)}
$$
$$
9 = (s + 2)(2.4 - u) \quad \text{(2)}
$$

---

### Step 2: Solve the System of Equations

From equation (1), solve for $ s $:

$$
s = \frac{9}{4 - u}
$$

Substitute into equation (2):

$$
\frac{9}{4 - u} + 2 = \frac{9}{2.4 - u}
$$

Multiply both sides by $ (4 - u)(2.4 - u) $ to eliminate denominators:

$$
9(2.4 - u) + 2(4 - u)(2.4 - u) = 9(4 - u)
$$

First, expand both sides:

- Left side:
  $$
  9(2.4 - u) = 21.6 - 9u
  $$
  $$
  2(4 - u)(2.4 - u) = 2(9.6 - 4u - 2.4u + u^2) = 2(9.6 - 6.4u + u^2) = 19.2 - 12.8u + 2u^2
  $$

  Combine:
  $$
  (21.6 - 9u) + (19.2 - 12.8u + 2u^2) = 40.8 - 21.8u + 2u^2
  $$

- Right side:
  $$
  9(4 - u) = 36 - 9u
  $$

Equating both sides:

$$
40.8 - 21.8u + 2u^2 = 36 - 9u
$$

Bring all terms to one side:

$$
2u^2 - 12.8u + 4.8 = 0
$$

To simplify, multiply by 10:

$$
20u^2 - 128u + 48 = 0
$$

Divide by 4:

$$
5u^2 - 32u + 12 = 0
$$

Use the quadratic formula:

$$
u = \frac{32 \pm \sqrt{(-32)^2 - 4(5)(12)}}{2(5)} = \frac{32 \pm \sqrt{1024 - 240}}{10} = \frac{32 \pm \sqrt{784}}{10}
$$

$$
u = \frac{32 \pm 28}{10} \Rightarrow u = 6 \text{ or } u = 0.4
$$

Now recall:
- $ u = \frac{t}{60} $
- $ u = 6 \Rightarrow t = 360 $ minutes → Impossible (walking time would be negative)
- $ u = 0.4 \Rightarrow t = 24 $ minutes (valid)

So, $ t = 24 $ minutes. Substituting back into the first equation to find $ s $:

$$
s = \frac{9}{4 - 0.4} = \frac{9}{3.6} = 2.5 \text{ km/h}
$$

### Step 3: Find Total Time at $ s + \frac{1}{2} $ km/h

New speed:

$$
s + \frac{1}{2} = 2.5 + 0.5 = 3 \text{ km/h}
$$

Walking time at 3 km/h:

$$
\frac{9}{3} = 3 \text{ hours}
$$

Total time including the $ t = 24 $ minutes in the coffee shop:
