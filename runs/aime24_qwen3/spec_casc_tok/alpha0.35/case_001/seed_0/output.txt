### **Step 1: Define Total Time and Walking Time**

Let:
- $ s $ be Aya's walking speed (in km/h),
- $ t $ be the time she spends at the coffee shop (in minutes),
- The total distance of her walk is **9 kilometers**.

1. At speed $ s $, the total time for the walk is **4 hours**, which includes $ t $ minutes at the coffee shop.

2. At speed $ s + 2 $, the total time for the walk is **2 hours and 24 minutes**, which is $ 2.4 $ hours, and again includes $ t $ minutes at the coffee shop.  
   - So, the **walking time** is $ 2.4 - \frac{t}{60} $ hours.

---

### **Step 2: Set Up Equations Using Distance = Speed × Time**

The distance is always 9 km, so:

$$
\frac{9}{s} = 4 - \frac{t}{60} \quad \text{(1)}
$$
$$
\frac{9}{s + 2} = 2.4 - \frac{t}{60} \quad \text{(2)}
$$

$$
\frac{9}{s} - \frac{9}{s + 2} = 4 - 2.4 = 1.6
$$

Simplify the left-hand side:

$$
\frac{9(s + 2) - 9s}{s(s + 2)} = \frac{18}{s(s + 2)} = 1.6
$$

This gives:

$$
\frac{18}{s(s + 2)} = 1.6
$$

We now solve this equation for $ s $.

---

### **Step 3: Solve for $ s $**

Convert $ 1.6 $ to a fraction:

$$
\frac{18}{s(s + 2)} = \frac{8}{5}
$$

Cross-multiplying:

$$
18 \cdot 5 = 8 \cdot s(s + 2)
$$
$$
90 = 8s^2 + 16s
$$

Rearranging:

$$
8s^2 + 16s - 90 = 0
$$

Divide through by 2:

$$
4s^2 + 8s - 45 = 0
$$

$$

$$
s = \frac{-8 \pm 28}{8}
$$

$$
s = \frac{20}{8} = \frac{5}{2} = 2.5 \quad \text{(km/h)}
$$

Plug $ s = 2.5 $ back into equation (1):

$$
\frac{9}{2.5} = 3.6 = 4 - \frac{t}{60}
