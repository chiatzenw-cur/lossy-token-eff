To solve the problem, we first identify the key elements of the situation:

- Aya walks **9 kilometers** every morning.
- She stops at a **coffee shop for t minutes**.
- The total time for the walk includes both the **walking time and t minutes**.

---

### Step 1: Convert Given Times to Hours

We are given:

- At speed $ s $ km/h, the total time is **4 hours**, including $ t $ minutes in the coffee shop.
- At speed $ s + 2 $ km/h, the total time is **2 hours 24 minutes**, which is **2.4 hours**, including $ t $ minutes.

---

### Step 2: Express Walking Time in Terms of $ s $ and $ t $

From the problem:

- **Walking time at $ s $ km/h** is $ \frac{9}{s} $ hours.
- **Total time including coffee shop** is 4 hours = $ \frac{9}{s} + \frac{t}{60} $.
- **Walking time at $ s + 2 $ km/h** is $ \frac{9}{s + 2} $ hours.
- **Total time including coffee shop** is 2.4 hours = $ \frac{9}{s + 2} + \frac{t}{60} $.

---

### Step 3: Set Up and Solve the System

From the first equation:

$$
\frac{9}{s} + \frac{t}{60} = 4
$$

So:

$$
\frac{9}{s} = 4 - \frac{t}{60} \quad \text{(1)}
$$

From the second equation:

$$
\frac{9}{s + 2} + \frac{t}{60} = 2.4
$$

So:

$$
\frac{9}{s + 2} = 2.4 - \frac{t}{60} \quad \text{(2)}
$$

Subtract equation (2) from equation (1):

$$
\frac{9}{s} - \frac{9}{s + 2} = 4 - 2.4 = 1.6
$$

Now compute:

$$
\frac{9}{s} - \frac{9}{s + 2} = \frac{9(s + 2) - 9s}{s(s + 2)} = \frac{18}{s(s + 2)}
$$

Set equal:

$$
\frac{18}{s(s + 2)} = 1.6 = \frac{8}{5} \Rightarrow s(s + 2) = \frac{18}{8/5} = \frac{18 \times 5}{8} = \frac{90}{8} = 11.25
$$

So:

$$
s(s + 2) = 11.25
$$

Let’s solve this:

$$
4s^2 + 8s = 45 \Rightarrow 4s^2 + 8s - 45 = 0
$$

Use the quadratic formula:

$$
s = \frac{-8 \pm \sqrt{8^2 - 4(4)(-45)}}{2(4)} = \frac{-8 \pm \sqrt{64 + 720}}{8} = \frac{-8 \pm \sqrt{784}}{8}
$$

$$
s = \frac{-8 \pm 28}{8} \Rightarrow s = 2.5
$$

---

### Step 4: Find $ t $

Now plug $ s = 2.5 $ back into equation (1):

$$
\frac{9}{2.5} + \frac{t}{60} = 4 \Rightarrow \frac{9}{2.5} = 3.6
$$

$$
3.6 + \frac{t}{60} = 4 \Rightarrow \frac{t}{60} = 0.4 \Rightarrow t = 24 \text{ minutes}
$$

### Step 5: Solve for the New Scenario
