Since we are limited to moving only **right (R)** or **up (U)**, and each path has exactly 8 R and 8 U steps, the total number of steps is 16. Therefore, we are considering all binary sequences of 16 steps consisting of 8 R's and 8 U's, with no restrictions initially.

However, the problem is not asking for **all** such paths, but only those **changing direction exactly four times**.

---

### Understanding Direction Changes

A **change of direction** occurs when the sequence of steps transitions from R to U or from U to R at a particular point. For example:
- The path `R U R` changes direction **twice**: from R to U and U to R.

Each **direction change** corresponds to a transition from one type of move to the other. So, the **number of direction changes** is equal to the number of times the direction changes, **which is one less than the number of segments**.

Given that the number of **direction changes is exactly 4**, we infer that the total number of **segments** in the path is **5** (since 4 + 1 = 5). These 5 segments must alternate between R and U to ensure only 4 changes. This is because, if we have 5 segments:
- If it **starts with R**, it alternates as R, U, R, U, R → 3 R segments and 2 U segments.
- If it **starts with U**, it alternates as U, R, U, R, U → 3 U segments and 2 R segments.

Thus, there are exactly **two distinct cases**:
- Case 1: 3 R segments and 2 U segments, starting with R and alternating.
- Case 2: 3 U segments and 2 R segments, starting with U and alternating.

---

### Computing the Number of Paths in Each Case

We now compute the number of such paths in case 1. The number of ways to split 8 R steps into 3 **positive** segments (each segment having at least 1 R) is:
$$
\binom{8 - 1}{3 - 1} = \binom{7}{2} = 21
$$
Similarly, the number of ways to split 8 U steps into 2 **positive** segments (each $\geq 1$) is:
$$
\binom{8 - 1}{2 - 1} = \binom{7}{1} = 7
$$
For each such partition, there is a unique way to interleave the segments, since the order is fixed.

So, the number of paths in **Case 1 (starting with R)** is:
$$
21 \times 7 = 147
$$

By symmetry, the **Case 2 (starting with U)** also yields 147 paths.

---

### Total Number of Valid Paths

Adding the two cases:
$$
147 + 147 = 294
$$
