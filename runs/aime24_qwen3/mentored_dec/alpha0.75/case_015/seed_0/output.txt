To determine the **greatest real number less than $ BD^2 $** for all such rhombi $ ABCD $ inscribed in the hyperbola

$$
\frac{x^2}{20} - \frac{y^2}{24} = 1
$$

with diagonals intersecting at the origin, we follow a structured approach to analyze and minimize $ BD^2 $ under the given constraints.

---

### Step 1: Understand the Geometry

We are told that $ ABCD $ is a **rhombus** with diagonals intersecting at the origin. For a rhombus:

- **Diagonals are perpendicular and bisect each other.**
- Since the diagonals intersect at the origin, they are **symmetrical about the origin**. So, if $ A = (a, b) $, then $ C = (-a, -b) $, and if $ B = (c, d) $, then $ D = (-c, -d) $.

From the geometry:
$$
BD = \text{distance from } B \text{ to } D = \sqrt{(c - (-c))^2 + (d - (-d))^2} = \sqrt{(2c)^2 + (2d)^2} = 2\sqrt{c^2 + d^2}
$$

Then:
$$
BD^2 = 4(c^2 + d^2)
$$

Hence, the task reduces to: **Minimize $ c^2 + d^2 $**, subject to:
1. $ A(a, b) $ and $ B(c, d) $ lie on the hyperbola $ \frac{x^2}{20} - \frac{y^2}{24} = 1 $
2. Diagonals $ AC, BD $ are **perpendicular**, which implies that vectors $ \vec{OA} $ and $ \vec{OB} $ are perpendicular, i.e.,

$$
a c + b d = 0
$$

---

### Step 2: Use Hyperbolic Constraint

Recall the **hyperbola equation** for point $ B(c, d) $:
$$
\frac{c^2}{20} - \frac{d^2}{24} = 1
$$

Multiply through by 120 (LCM of 20 and 24) to eliminate denominators:

$$
6c^2 - 5d^2 = 120 \quad \text{(Equation 1)}
$$

Solving for $ d^2 $:

$$
d^2 = \frac{6c^2 - 120}{5}
$$

---

### Step 3: Use Perpendicularity Condition

We also have:

$$
a c + b d = 0 \quad \text{(Equation 2)}
$$

From the hyperbola equation for $ A(a, b) $:

$$
\frac{a^2}{20} - \frac{b^2}{24} = 1
$$

Substituting $ b = -\frac{a c}{d} $ (from Equation 2), we plug into the equation for $ A $:

$$
\frac{a^2}{20} - \frac{a^2 c^2}{24 d^2} = 1
$$

Let’s define $ D = \frac{1}{20} - \frac{c^2}{24 d^2} $. Then,

$$
a^2 = \frac{1}{D} \Rightarrow \frac{a^2}{20} = \frac{1}{20 D}
$$

Now this should be valid **as long as $ D > 0 $**, which gives a condition:

$$
\frac{1}{20} - \frac{c^2}{24 d^2} > 0 \Rightarrow 24d^2 - 20c^2 > 0
$$

Using Equation 1, substitute $ d^2 = \frac{6c^2 - 120}{5} $ into the above:

$$
24d^2 - 20c^2 = 24\left(\frac{6c^2 - 120}{5}\right) - 20c^2
= \frac{144c^2 - 2880}{5} - 20c^2 = \frac{144c^2 - 2880 - 100c^2}{5}
= \frac{44c^2 - 2880}{5} > 0
\Rightarrow c^2 > \frac{2880}{44} = \frac{720}{11}
$$

This is a **lower bound** for $ c^2 $.

$$
BD^2 = 4(c^2 + d^2)
$$

$$
BD^2 = 4\left( \frac{11}{5}c^2 - 24 \right) = \frac{44}{5}c^2 - 96

To **minimize** $ BD^2 $, we minimize $ c^2 $, subject to $ c^2 > \frac{720}{11} $
