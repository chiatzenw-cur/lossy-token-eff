To determine the total time Aya takes for her walk, including the time spent at the coffee shop, we analyze the problem using the given time-speed relationships.

---

### **Step 1: Define Variables**

Let:
- $ s $: Aya's walking speed in km/h
- $ t $: the time spent at the coffee shop in *minutes*
- Total time in the first case = 4 hours = 240 minutes
- Total time in the second case = 2 hours 24 minutes = 2.4 hours = 144 minutes

The path is 9 km long, and Aya's **walking time** is given by the formula:

$$
\text{Walking time} = \frac{\text{Distance}}{\text{Speed}} = \frac{9}{s} \quad \text{(in hours)}
$$

Since the total time includes the fixed time $ t $ (in minutes), we must express all times in the same unit. Let's convert walking time to minutes:

$$
\text{Walking time in minutes} = \frac{9}{s} \times 60
$$

Thus, for the **first case**:

$$
\frac{9}{s} \times 60 = 240 - t \tag{1}
$$

Similarly, for the **second case**:

$$
\frac{9}{s + 2} \times 60 = 144 - t \tag{2}
$$

---

### **Step 2: Solve the System of Equations**

Starting with Equation (1):

$$
\frac{540}{s} = 240 - t \Rightarrow t = 240 - \frac{540}{s} \tag{3}
$$

From Equation (2):

$$
\frac{540}{s + 2} = 144 - t \Rightarrow t = 144 - \frac{540}{s + 2} \tag{4}
$$

Set Equations (3) and (4) equal:

$$
240 - \frac{540}{s} = 144 - \frac{540}{s + 2}
$$

Bring all terms to one side:

$$
240 - 144 = \frac{540}{s} - \frac{540}{s + 2}
\Rightarrow 96 = 540\left( \frac{1}{s} - \frac{1}{s + 2} \right)
$$

Simplify the difference of reciprocals:

$$
\frac{1}{s} - \frac{1}{s + 2} = \frac{(s + 2) - s}{s(s + 2)} = \frac{2}{s(s + 2)}
$$

So:

$$
96 = 540 \cdot \frac{2}{s(s + 2)} = \frac{1080}{s(s + 2)}
$$

Now solve for $ s(s + 2) $:

$$
s(s + 2) = \frac{1080}{96} = 11.25
$$

This is a quadratic in disguise:

$$
s^2 + 2s - 11.25 = 0
$$

Use the quadratic formula:

$$
s = \frac{-2 \pm \sqrt{4 + 45}}{2} = \frac{-2 \pm \sqrt{49}}{2} = \frac{-2 \pm 7}{2}
$$

We discard the negative solution, so:

$$
s = \frac{5}{2} = 2.5 \text{ km/h}
$$

---

### **Step 3: Find Coffee Shop Time $ t $**

Using Equation (3):

$$
t = 240 - \frac{540}{2.5} = 240 - 216 = 24 \text{ minutes}
$$

---

### **Step 4: Compute Total Walk Time at $ s + \frac{1}{2} $ km/h**

Now, Aya walks at:

$$
s + \frac{1}{2} = 2.5 + 0.5 = 3.0 \text{ km/h}
$$

Walking time for 9 km is:

$$
\frac{9}{3} = 3 \text{ hours}
$$

Convert to minutes:

$$
3 \text{ hours} = 180 \text{ minutes}
$$

Add the coffee shop time $ t = 24 $ minutes:

$$
\text{Total walk time} = 180 + 24 = \boxed{204} \text{ minutes}
