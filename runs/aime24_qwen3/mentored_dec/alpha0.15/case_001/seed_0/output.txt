### Step 1: Understand and Translate Given Information

- **Distance of the walk**: 9 kilometers
- **Case 1**: Walking speed = $ s $ km/h, total time = 4 hours (including $ t $ minutes at coffee shop)
- **Case 2**: Walking speed = $ s + 2 $ km/h, total time = 2 hours and 24 minutes = 2.4 hours (including $ t $ minutes at coffee shop)
- **Target**: Find total time (in minutes) when walking speed = $ s + \frac{1}{2} $ km/h, including the same $ t $ minutes at the coffee shop.

---

### Step 2: Convert Total Time to Hours and Set Up Equations

Let’s denote the time spent walking in hours as $ \text{walking time} = \frac{9}{s} $ and similarly, $ \frac{9}{s + 2} $ for the faster speed.

Since total time includes both walking time and $ t $ minutes (converted into hours), we can write:

- **Case 1**:  
  $$
  \frac{9}{s} + \frac{t}{60} = 4 \tag{1}
  $$

- **Case 2**:  
  $$
  \frac{9}{s + 2} + \frac{t}{60} = 2.4 \tag{2}
  $$

---

### Step 3: Eliminate $ t $ and Solve for $ s $

Subtract Equation (2) from Equation (1):

$$
\frac{9}{s} - \frac{9}{s+2} = 4 - 2.4 = 1.6
$$

Simplify the left side:

$$
\frac{9(s + 2) - 9s}{s(s + 2)} = 1.6 \Rightarrow \frac{18}{s(s + 2)} = 1.6
$$

Now solve:

$$
s(s + 2) = \frac{18}{1.6} = 11.25
$$

$$
s^2 + 2s - 11.25 = 0
$$

Using the quadratic formula:

$$
s = \frac{-2 \pm \sqrt{2^2 + 4 \cdot 1 \cdot 11.25}}{2} = \frac{-2 \pm \sqrt{4 + 45}}{2} = \frac{-2 \pm 7}{2}
$$

Take the positive root:

$$
s = \frac{5}{2} = 2.5
$$

### Step 4: Compute $ t $

$$
\frac{9}{2.5} + \frac{t}{60} = 4 \Rightarrow 3.6 + \frac{t}{60} = 4 \Rightarrow \frac{t}{60} = 0.4

$$
t = 0.4 \cdot 60 = 24 \text{ minutes}
$$

We now know:

- New speed = $ s + \frac{1}{2} = 2.5 + 0.5 = 3 $ km/h
