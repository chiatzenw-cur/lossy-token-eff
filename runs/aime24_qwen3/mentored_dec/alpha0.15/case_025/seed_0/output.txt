### Step 1: Convert the Logarithmic Equations to Exponent Form

The given equations are:

$$
\begin{aligned}
1.\quad \log_2\left(\frac{x}{yz}\right) &= \frac{1}{2} \\
2.\quad \log_2\left(\frac{y}{xz}\right) &= \frac{1}{3} \\
3.\quad \log_2\left(\frac{z}{xy}\right) &= \frac{1}{4}
\end{aligned}
$$

Converting these to exponent form:

$$
\begin{aligned}
\frac{x}{yz} &= 2^{1/2} \\
\frac{y}{xz} &= 2^{1/3} \\
\frac{z}{xy} &= 2^{1/4}
\end{aligned}
$$

---

### Step 2: Introduce Variables for Logarithms

Let us define:

- $ a = \log_2 x $
- $ b = \log_2 y $
- $ c = \log_2 z $

Then:

$$
\begin{aligned}
x &= 2^a,\quad y = 2^b,\quad z = 2^c \\
\log_2\left(\frac{x}{yz}\right) &= a - b - c = \frac{1}{2} \\
\log_2\left(\frac{y}{xz}\right) &= b - a - c = \frac{1}{3} \\
\log_2\left(\frac{z}{xy}\right) &= c - a - b = \frac{1}{4}
\end{aligned}
$$

This gives us the system of linear equations:

$$
\begin{cases}
a - b - c = \frac{1}{2} \\
-a + b - c = \frac{1}{3} \\
-a - b + c = \frac{1}{4}
\end{cases}
$$

### Step 3: Solve the System of Linear Equations

#### Add equations (1) and (2):

$$
(a - b - c) + (-a + b - c) = \frac{1}{2} + \frac{1}{3} = \frac{5}{6} \\
-2c = \frac{5}{6} \Rightarrow c = -\frac{5}{12}
$$

#### Add equations (1) and (3):

$$
(a - b - c) + (-a - b + c) = \frac{1}{2} + \frac{1}{4} = \frac{3}{4} \\

$$
