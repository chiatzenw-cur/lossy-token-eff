- $AB = CD = \sqrt{41}$
- $AC = BD = \sqrt{80}$
- $BC = AD = \sqrt{89}$

These symmetries indicate that the tetrahedron is a **disphenoid**, a type of tetrahedron where **opposite edges are equal** in length. This kind of tetrahedron can be inscribed in a rectangular box such that each pair of opposite edges corresponds to the face diagonals of the box.

### Step 1: Assign Dimensions to the Box

Let the dimensions of the box be $x$, $y$, and $z$. Then, the face diagonals of the box are:

- $x^2 + y^2 = 41$ (from $AB = \sqrt{41}$ and $CD = \sqrt{41}$)
- $x^2 + z^2 = 80$ (from $AC = \sqrt{80}$ and $BD = \sqrt{80}$)
- $y^2 + z^2 = 89$ (from $BC = \sqrt{89}$ and $AD = \sqrt{89}$)

Adding all three equations:

$$
(x^2 + y^2) + (x^2 + z^2) + (y^2 + z^2) = 41 + 80 + 89 = 210
$$

$$
2(x^2 + y^2 + z^2) = 210 \quad \Rightarrow \quad x^2 + y^2 + z^2 = 105
$$

Using this, compute individual values:

- $x^2 = 105 - (y^2 + z^2) = 105 - 89 = 16$, so $x = 4$
- $y^2 = 105 - (x^2 + z^2) = 105 - 80 = 25$, so $y = 5$
- $z^2 = 105 - (x^2 + y^2) = 105 - 41 = 64$, so $z = 8$

### Step 2: Compute the Volume of the Tetrahedron

The volume of a tetrahedron with vertices corresponding to alternate corners of a rectangular box of dimensions $x$, $y$, $z$ is given by:

$$
V = \frac{1}{3} \cdot x \cdot y \cdot z
$$

Substituting in values:

V = \frac{1}{3} \cdot 4 \cdot 5 \cdot 8 = \frac{160}{3}
