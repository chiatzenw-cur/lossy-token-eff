To solve the problem, we are asked to find the probability that Jen **wins the grand prize** given that she **wins a prize** in a lottery, and then express that probability as a reduced fraction $ \frac{m}{n} $, finally computing $ m + n $.

---

### Step 1: Understand the Scenario

- Jen picks **4 distinct numbers** from the set $ S = \{1, 2, \ldots, 10\} $.
- The lottery randomly selects **4 distinct numbers** from $ S $.
- She wins a **prize** if **at least 2** of her chosen numbers are among the 4 lottery numbers.
- She wins the **grand prize** if all **4** of her numbers match the 4 lottery numbers.

We are to compute the **conditional probability**:

$$
P(\text{Grand Prize} \mid \text{Prize}) = \frac{P(\text{Grand Prize} \cap \text{Prize})}{P(\text{Prize})}
$$

Since **winning the grand prize implies she has won the prize**, we can simplify:

$$
P(\text{Grand Prize} \mid \text{Prize}) = \frac{P(\text{Grand Prize})}{P(\text{Prize})}
$$

---

### Step 2: Compute the Total Number of Outcomes

The total number of ways to choose 4 numbers from 10 is:

$$
\binom{10}{4} = 210
$$

So, the total number of possible outcomes is 210.

---

### Step 3: Count the Favorable Outcomes for Winning a Prize

Let’s define:

- **Exactly 2 matches**: $ \binom{4}{2} \cdot \binom{6}{2} = 6 \cdot 15 = 90 $
- **Exactly 3 matches**: $ \binom{4}{3} \cdot \binom{6}{1} = 4 \cdot 6 = 24 $
- **Exactly 4 matches** (Grand Prize): $ \binom{4}{4} \cdot \binom{6}{0} = 1 \cdot 1 = 1 $

So, the total number of outcomes leading to a **prize** is:

$$
90 + 24 + 1 = 115
$$

---

### Step 4: Compute the Probability of Winning a Prize

$$
P(\text{Prize}) = \frac{115}{210}
$$

---

### Step 5: Compute the Probability of Winning the Grand Prize

Only 1 outcome results in **all 4 numbers matching**:

$$
P(\text{Grand Prize}) = \frac{1}{210}
$$

---

### Step 6: Apply the Conditional Probability Formula

Now compute:

$$
P(\text{Grand Prize} \mid \text{Prize}) = \frac{\frac{1}{210}}{\frac{115}{210}} = \frac{1}{115}
$$

This is already in lowest terms. Thus, $ m = 1 $, $ n = 115 $, and:

$$
m + n = 1 + 115 = \boxed{116}
