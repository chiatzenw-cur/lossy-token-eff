### 1.  Rewrite the displayed factor

\[
2-2\omega^k+\omega ^{2k}= \bigl(\omega^k-3\bigr)^2-2 .
\]

More usefully,
\[
2-2\omega^k+\omega^{2k}= \omega^{2k}-2\omega ^k+2,
\]
which is the evaluation of polynomial  

\[
g(x)=\;x^{2}-2x+2
\]
at *x* = \(\omega^k\).

--------------------------------------------------------------------

### 2.  Use the resultant of the two polynomials

The set \(\{\,\omega^k\mid k=0,1,\dots-12\,\}\) are the roots of the
polynomial

\[
f(x)=x^{13}-1 .
\]

Hence for any non‑zero polynomial \(h(x)\),

\[
f(x)=\prod_{x^{{13}}-1=0}\bigl( x-\omega^k\bigr)
                \quad\text{and}\quad f(\omega^k)=0 .
\]

The product we want is

\[
P=\prod_{x^{{13}}-1=0}\bigl(h(\omega^k)=g(\omega^k)\bigr).
   \tag{1}
\]

For two polynomials with coprime leading coefficients,
\( \text{Res}(f,g)=\prod_{g(\lambda)=0} f(\lambda)\).

Thus

\[
P=\text{Res}(f,g)=\prod_{g(\lambda)=0}\bigl(f(\lambda)\bigr)
      =\prod_{\lambda\,:\,\lambda^2-2\lambda+2}
        (\,\lambda^{13}-1\).
                 \tag{2}
\]

Thus equation (2) becomes
