### Express \(E(z)\) in terms of \(\cos\theta,\sin\theta\)

\[
\begin{aligned}
E(z)&=A(\cos\theta+i\sin\theta)+(B\cos\theta-Bi\sin\theta)\\
&=(A+B)\cos\theta+i(A-B)\sin\theta .
\end{aligned}
\]

Let  

\[
C=A+B=324+504e, \qquad D=A-B=276+432i.
\]

Hence

\[
E(z)=C\cos\theta+iD\sin\theta .
\]

The **real part** of \(E(z)\) is  

\[
\operatorname{Re}E(z)=\operatorname{Re}(C)\cos\theta-\operatorname{Im}(D)\sin\theta
                     =c_1\cos\theta-d_2\sin\theta,
\]
with   
\(c_1=\operatorname{Re}(C)=324,\)  \(d_2=\operatorname{Im}(D)=432\).

---

### Maximising \(\operatorname{Re}E(z)\)

For any real numbers \(p,q\),

\[
\max_{\theta}(p\,\cos\theta+q\,\sin\theta)=\sqrt{p^2+q^2}.
\]

Thus  

\[
\max_{\theta}\operatorname{Re}E(z)
  =\sqrt{c_1^2+d_2^2}
  =\sqrt{324^2+435^2}
  =\sqrt{104\,976+186\,624}
  =\sqrt{291\,600}=540 .
\]
