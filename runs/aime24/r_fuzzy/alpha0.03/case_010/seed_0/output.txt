- \(A\) be Jen’s chosen set of 4 distinct numbers from \(\{1,\dots ,10\}\).  
- \(B\) be the 4 numbers that are drawn at random (also distinct).

All \(4\)-element subsets of \(\{1,\dots ,10\}\) are equally likely, so for a fixed \(A\) the probability of any particular \(B\) is \(\frac1{\binom{10}{4}}=\frac1{210}\).

---

### 1.  Count the possible draws \(B\) according to the size of the intersection with \(A\)

|\(k=\lvert A\cap B\rvert\)|number of \(B\) with exactly \(k\) common numbers|
|---|---|
|4| \(\displaystyle \binom{4}{4}\binom{6}{0}=1\)|
|3| \(\displaystyle \binom{4}{3}\binom{6}{1}=4\cdot 6=24\)|
|2| \(\displaystyle \binom{4}{2}\binom{6}{2}=6\cdot 15=90\)|
|1| \(\displaystyle \binom{4}{1}\binom{6}{3}=4\cdot 20=80\)|
|0| \(\displaystyle \binom{4}{0}\binom{6}{4}=1\cdot15=15\)|

(The complement has \(6\) numbers because \(10-4=6\).)

---

### 2.  Probabilities

- **Grand prize (all four match):**  
  \[
  P(\text{Grand})=\frac{1}{210}.
  \]

- **Win a prize (at least two matches):**  
  For \(k=2,3,4\):
  \[
  P(\text{Prize}) = \frac{90+24+1}{210}=\frac{115}{210}=\frac{23}{42}.
  \]

---

### 3.  Conditional probability

\[
P(\text{Grand} \mid \text{Prize})=
\frac{P(\text{Grand})}{P(\text{Prize})}
=\frac{\,\frac1{210}\,}{\frac{23}{42}}
=\frac{1}{210}\cdot\frac{42}{23}
=\frac{42}{4830}
=\frac{1}{115}.
\]

Thus the probability is \(\dfrac{m}{n}=\dfrac{1}{115}\) with \(m=1,\;n=115\).
