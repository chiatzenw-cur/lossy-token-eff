\[
A=(x_1,y_1),\qquad B=(x_2,y_2)

are orthogonal.

From (2) we can realise

\[
\tanh\gamma\ge \kappa:=\frac{5}{6}, \qquad(0<\kappa<1).
\]

If \(\gamma>0\), then \(\sinh^2\gamma>0\) and BD grows with \(\gamma\).
Thus the minimal \(BD^2\) occurs when \(\gamma\) is the smallest
admissible value: \(\gamma=\arctanh(\kappa)\).

With \(\tanh\alpha\tanh\gamma=\kappa\)

\[
\tanh\alpha\sim \frac{\kappa}{\tanh\gamma} \le 1 .
\]

So \(\gamma=\arctanh(\kappa)\) suffices and the corresponding
\(\alpha\) is finite; in practice
\(\gamma\to\arctanh(\kappa)\) gives \(BD^2\) as close to the minimum as
desired.

Using \(\tanh\gamma=\kappa\) we obtain

\[
\sinh^2\gamma=\frac{\tanh\gamma^2}{\bigl(1-\tan h\gamma^2\bigr)}
      =\frac{\kappa^2}{1-\frac25}
      =\frac{25/36}{11/36}
      =\frac{25}{11}.
\]

Substituting this into (3):

\[
BD^2_{\min}=4\bigl(20\!+\!44\!\cdot\!\frac{25}{11})
          =80+176\!\cdot\!\frac{25}{11}
          =80+400=480 .
\]

--------------------------------------------------------------------
### 5.  Result

All admissible rhombi satisfy \(BD^2>480\), and values
arbitrarily close to \(480\) are attainable.  
Hence the greatest real number less than \(BD^2\) for every such
rhombus is

\[
\boxed{\,480\,}.
\]
