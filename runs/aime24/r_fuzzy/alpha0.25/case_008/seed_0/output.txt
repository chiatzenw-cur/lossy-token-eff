\[

\]

Multiply the two equations:

\[
\bigl[x\,\ln y\bigr]\cdot\bigl(4y\,\ln x\bigr)=
10\,\ln x\,\cdot\;10\,\ln y .
\]

The left side simplifies to \((4x y)\ln x\,\ln y\), and the right side to
\(100\,\ln x\,\ln y\). Thus

\[
4x y\,\ln x\,\ln y = 100\,\ln x\,\ln y .
\]

Because \(x,y>1\) we have \(\ln x\neq \ln y\neq 0\), which allows the
cancellation of the common factor \(\ln x\,\ln y\):

\[
4x y = 100 .
\]

Hence

\[
xy=\boxed{25}.
\]
