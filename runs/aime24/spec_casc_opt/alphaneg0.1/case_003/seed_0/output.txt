      =1+8+28+56+70=163 .
\]

There are \(2^8=256\) possible independent colourings of the octagon.

Thus the probability that a random colouring allows a rotation by
\(k\) such that every blue vertex lies at a formerly red vertex is

\[
\boxed{\frac{163}{256}}.
\]

m+n=163+256=419.
