### 1. Expressing the objective function

\[
S=a^{2}+b^{2}+c^{2}
= (a+b+c)^{2}-2(ab+bc+ca)
= (a+b+c)^{2}-54 .
\]

Hence maximising \(S\) is equivalent to maximising the sum \(p=a+b+c\).

\[

r_{\max}^{2}=\frac{S_{\max}}{4}
              =\frac{41.0625}{4}
              =\frac{657}{64}.
\]

The fraction \(\dfrac{657}{64}\) is already in lowest terms
(\(\gcd(657,64)=1\)).
