\]

### Compute the probabilities

* \(k=2\):
  \[
  P(2)=\frac{\binom{4}{2}\binom{6}{2}}{210}= \frac{6\times15}{210}= \frac{90}{210}= \frac{3}{7}
  \]

* \(k=3\):
  \[
  P(3)=\frac{\binom{4}{3}\binom{6}{1}}{210}= \frac{4\times6}{210}= \frac{24}{210}= \frac{4}{35}
  \]

* \(k=4\):
  \[
  P(4)=\frac{\binom{4}{4}\binom{6}{0}}{210}= \frac{1\times1}{210}= \frac{1}{210}
