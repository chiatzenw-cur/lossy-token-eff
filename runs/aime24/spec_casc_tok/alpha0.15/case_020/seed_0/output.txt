Then the product equals the resultant of \(p(z)=z^{13}-1\) and \(f(z)\):

\[
\prod_{r^{13}=1} f(r)=\operatorname{Res}(p,f)

For a monic \(p\), \(\operatorname{Res}(p,f)=\prod_{\beta:\,f(\beta)=0} p(\beta)\).

The roots of \(f(z)=0\) are \( \beta_1=1+i,\;\beta_2=1-i\).

Hence

\[
\prod_{k=0}^{12}\bigl(2-2\omega^k+\omega^{2k}\bigr)
=(\beta_1^{13}-1)(\beta_2^{13}-1).
\]

Write \(\beta_1=\sqrt2\,e^{i\pi/4}\).  
Then \(\beta_1^{13}=2^{13/2}e^{i13\pi/4}=2^{13/2}(-e^{i\pi/4})=-64\,\beta_1\).

So \(\beta_1^{13}-1=-64\beta_1-1\) and similarly \(\beta_2^{13}-1=-64\beta_2-1\).

Thus

\[
\begin{aligned}
(\beta_1^{13}-1)(\beta_2^{13}-1)
&=(64\beta_1+1)(64\beta_2+1)\\
&=64^2\beta_1\beta_2+64(\beta_1+\beta_2)+1\\
&=64^2\cdot2+64\cdot2+1\\
&=8192+128+1\\
