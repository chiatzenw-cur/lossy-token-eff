### 1.  Orthogonality of the diagonals

For a rhombus all sides are equal.  
The side lengths are

\[
AB=\|\vec u-\vec v\|,\qquad BC=\|\vec v+\vec u\|.
\]

Equality demands \( \|\vec u-\vec v\|=\|\vec u+\vec v\|\), which gives  

\[
\vec u\!\cdot\!\vec v=0 .
\]

Thus the diagonals are perpendicular.

---

### 2.  Parametrisation of the hyperbola

Each point on  

\[
\frac{x^2}{20}-\frac{y^2}{24}=1
\]

can be written as  

\[
\vec u_{1}=
\bigl(\pm \sqrt{20}\,\cosh a,\;\sqrt{24}\,\sinh a\bigr),\qquad
\vec u_{2}=
\bigl(\pm \sqrt{20}\,\cosh b,\;\sqrt{24}\,\sinh b\bigr)
\]

with \(a,b\in\mathbb R\).  
Let \(s=\pm1\) denote the product of the two \(x\)-signs.  
The orthogonality condition becomes  

\[
20\,s\cosh a\cosh b+24\sinh a\sinh b=0
\quad\Longrightarrow\quad
\tanh a=-\,\frac{5s\cosh b}{6\sinh b}.
\]

Because \(|\tanh a|\le1\), we must have  

\[
\frac{5\cosh b}{6|\sinh b|}\le 1
\quad\Longrightarrow\quad
\frac{\cosh b}{|\sinh b|}\le\frac{6}{5}\quad\Longrightarrow\quad
\coth|b|\le\frac{6}{5}.
\]

Hence  

\[
|b|\ge\operatorname{arccoth}\!\left(\frac{6}{5}\right)
=\frac12\ln\!\frac{1+\frac65}{1-\frac65}
=\frac12\ln 11 .
\]

### 3.  Minimising \(BD^2\)

The length of diagonal \(BD\) is  

\[
BD=2\|\vec v\|,\qquad
\|\vec v\|^2=20\cosh^2 b+24\sinh^2 b
=20+44\sinh^2 b .
\]

Since \(\sinh^2 b\) is increasing with \(|b|\), the minimum occurs at
\(|b|=\tfrac12\ln 11\).  
At this value

\[
\sinh b=\frac{5}{\sqrt{11}},\qquad
\sinh^2 b=\frac{25}{11}.
\]

Therefore  

\[
\|\vec v\|^2=20+44\cdot\frac{25}{11}=20+100=120,
\qquad
BD^2=4\|\vec v\|^2=4\cdot120=480 .
\]
