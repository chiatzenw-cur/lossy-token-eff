### 1.  Express the logarithms in natural form

\[
\log_x(y^x)=\frac{\ln(y^x)}{\ln x}= \frac{x\ln y}{\ln x}=10
\]
\[
\log_y(x^{4y})=\frac{\ln(x^{4y})}{\ln y}= \frac{4y\ln x}{\ln y}=10
\]

Thus

\[
\frac{x\ln y}{\ln x}=10 \quad\Longrightarrow\quad \ln y=\frac{10\ln x}{x}\tag{3}
\]
\[
\frac{4y\ln x}{\ln y}=10 \quad\Longrightarrow\quad \ln y=\frac{4y\ln x}{10}\tag{4}
\]

---

### 2.  Equate the two expressions for \(\ln y\)

From (3) and (4):

\[
\frac{10\ln x}{x}=\frac{4y\ln x}{10}
\]

Since \(x>1\) we have \(\ln x>0\), so we can cancel \(\ln x\):

\[
\frac{10}{x}=\frac{4y}{10}
\]

Multiply both sides by \(x\):

\[
10=\frac{4y\,x}{10}
\]

Multiply by \(10\):

\[
100=4xy
\]
