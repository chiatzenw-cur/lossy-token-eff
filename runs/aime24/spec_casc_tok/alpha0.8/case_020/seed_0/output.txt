Let \(f(t)=t^2-2t+2\) and \(g(t)=t^{13}-1\).  
The product over all \(13^{\text{th}}\) roots of unity is the resultant

\[
\operatorname{Res}(g,f)=\prod_{k=0}^{12}f(\omega^k).
\]

By the property of resultants,

\[
\operatorname{Res}(g,f)=\prod_{j=1}^{2}\bigl(g(s_j)\bigr)
\]

where \(s_j\) are the roots of \(f(t)\).
The roots of \(f(t)=0\) are \(s_1=1+i\) and \(s_2=1-i\).

\[
g(s_1)= (1+i)^{13}-1 ,\qquad
g(s_2)= (1-i)^{13}-1 .
\]

\[
1+i=\sqrt{2}\,e^{i\pi/4}\;\Longrightarrow\;
(1+i)^{13}=2^{13/2}e^{i13\pi/4}

\]

\[
g(s_1)=-64(1+i)-1=-65-64i,\qquad
g(s_2)=-65+64i.
