* a starting vertex \(\theta\) (\(12\) possibilities), and
* a step \(\alpha\) equal to one of the angles \(30^\circ, 60^\circ, 90^\circ, 120^\circ, 150^\circ\)
  (i.e. \( \alpha = 1,2,3,4,5\) steps of \(30^\circ\)).

So there are \(12\times 5 = 60\) ordered pairs \((\theta,\alpha)\).

Each rectangle is counted exactly four times among these pairs:
choosing the starting vertex as any of its four vertices gives the same rectangle.
Hence

\[
\text{Number of distinct rectangles}= \frac{60}{4}=15 .
\]
