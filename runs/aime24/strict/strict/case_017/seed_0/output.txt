### 1.  Using the given perpendicularity

For any triangle the distance between circumcenter \(O\) and incenter \(I\) is  

\[
OI^{2}=R(R-2r).
\]

Thus  

\[
OI^{2}=13(13-12)=13,\qquad OI=\sqrt{13}.
\]

Because \(\overline{IA}\perp\overline{OI}\) (right angle at \(I\)) and \(AO=R=13\),

\[
AO^{2}=AI^{2}+OI^{2}\quad\Longrightarrow\quad
AI^{2}=13^{2}-13=156.
\]

The distance from the incenter to a vertex has the well‑known form  

\[
AI^{2}=r^{2}+(s-a)^{2}, \qquad s=\frac{a+b+c}{2}.
\]

Hence  

\[
156=6^{2}+(s-a)^{2}\quad\Longrightarrow\quad
(s-a)^{2}=120\quad\Longrightarrow\quad s-a=2\sqrt{30}.
\]

--------------------------------------------------------------------
### 2.  Expressing the sides in terms of \(a\)

Let  

\[
x=b+c,\qquad y=bc.
\]

From \(s-a=2\sqrt{30}\) we get

\[
x-a=4\sqrt{30}\ \Longrightarrow\ x=a+4\sqrt{30},\qquad
s=a+2\sqrt{30}.
\]

The area of \( \triangle ABC\) satisfies  

\[
\Delta =rs=6s=\frac{abc}{4R}=\frac{abc}{52},
\]

so \(abc=312\,s\).  
With \(a\,y=abc\),

\[
y=\frac{312\,s}{a}=\frac{312\,(a+2\sqrt{30})}{a}.
\]

--------------------------------------------------------------------
### 3.  Using the law of cosines for \(A\)

\[
\cos A=\frac{b^{2}+c^{2}-a^{2}}{2bc}
      =\frac{x^{2}-2y-a^{2}}{2y}
      =\frac{4\sqrt{30}\,a+8\cdot 30}{y}-1
      =\frac{a\sqrt{30}}{78}-1 .
\]

Because \(R=\dfrac{a}{2\sin A}=13\),  
\(\sin A=\dfrac{a}{26}\) and \(\sin ^2A=\dfrac{a^{2}}{676}\).

From \(\sin^2A=1-\cos^2A\) and the expression for \(\cos A\):

\[
\frac{a^{2}}{676}=1-\left(\frac{a\sqrt{30}}{78}-1\right)^{2}
                 =\frac{2a\sqrt{30}}{78}-\frac{a^{2}\,30}{78^{2}}.
\]

Multiplying by \(676\cdot 78^{2}\) and simplifying gives

\[
6084a^{2}+676\cdot30\,a^{2}-105456\sqrt{30}\,a=0
      \quad\Longrightarrow\quad
a=4\sqrt{30}.
\]

Thus  

\[
x=b+c= a+4\sqrt{30}=8\sqrt{30},
\qquad
s=a+2\sqrt{30}=6\sqrt{30},
\qquad
y=bc=\frac{312\,s}{a}
      =\frac{312\,(6\sqrt{30})}{4\sqrt{30}}
      =468.
\]

\[
AB\cdot AC= c\cdot b = bc = \boxed{468}.
