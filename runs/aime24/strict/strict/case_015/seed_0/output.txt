Since the diagonals of a rhombus bisect one another,

### 1.  Condition for a rhombus

For consecutive vertices \(A, B, C, D\) the side–lengths are

\[
|AB|=|\mathbf u-\mathbf v|,\qquad |BC|=|\mathbf v+\mathbf u|,
\]

hence the rhombus condition \(|AB|=|BC|\) gives

\[
|\mathbf u-\mathbf v|^{2}=|\mathbf u+\mathbf v|^{2}\Longrightarrow
\mathbf u\!\cdot \!\mathbf v =0 .
\]

Thus the vectors \(\mathbf u\) and \(\mathbf v\) are perpendicular.

--------------------------------------------------------------------

### 2.  Hyperbola parametrisation

The hyperbola is  

\[
\frac{x^{2}}{20}-\frac{y^{2}}{24}=1 .
\]

Let  

\[
a^{2}=20,\qquad b^{2}=24 .
\]

A convenient parametrisation of the right–hand branch is  

\[
\mathbf u=(a\cosh t,\;b\sinh t),\qquad 
\mathbf v=(a\cosh s,\;b\sinh s),
\]

with \(t,s\in\mathbb R\).  
(The signs of the coordinates may be chosen independently; they
do not affect minimal lengths.)

The orthogonality condition \(\mathbf u\!\cdot\!\mathbf v=0\) becomes

\[
a^{2}\cosh t\cosh s\sigma_{x}+b^{2}\sinh t\sinh s\sigma_{y}=0,
\tag{1}
\]

where \(\sigma_{x},\sigma_{y}= \pm1\) represent the possible sign
choices.  Set \(\sigma=\sigma_{x}\sigma_{y}\) (also \(\pm1\)).
Then

\[
a^{2}\cosh s\,\cosh t +\sigma\,b^{2}\sinh s\,\sinh t =0 .
\tag{2}
\]

--------------------------------------------------------------------

### 3.  Finding the minimal \(|\mathbf v|\)

Write \(X=\cosh s,\;Y=\sinh s\) (\(X^{2}-Y^{2}=1\)).
Equation (2) gives

\[
\cosh t\, =-\sigma\,\frac{b^{2}Y}{a^{2}X}\;\sinh t .
\]

Hence \(\cosh t=k\sinh t\) with
\(k=-\sigma\,\frac{b^{2}Y}{a^{2}X}\).
A real \(t\) exists only if \(|k|>1\), i.e.

\[
\frac{b^{2}Y}{a^{2}X}>1\quad\Longrightarrow\quad 
24Y>20X \quad\Longrightarrow\quad
6Y>5X .
\tag{3}
\]

Let \(X,\;Y\) satisfy (3) and \(X^{2}-Y^{2}=1\).  
We want to minimise

\[
|\mathbf v|^{2}=a^{2}X^{2}+b^{2}Y^{2}
          =20X^{2}+24Y^{2}.
\tag{4}
\]

\[
|\mathbf v|^{2}=44Y^{2}+20 .
\]

\[
6Y=5X,\qquad X^{2}-Y^{2}=1
