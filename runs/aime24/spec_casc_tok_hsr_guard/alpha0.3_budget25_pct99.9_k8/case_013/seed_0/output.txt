### Real part

Write \(A=300+468i,\; B=24+36i\).
For \(e^{i\theta}=\cos\theta+i\sin\theta\),

\[
\begin{aligned}
A e^{i\theta}&=(300+468i)(\cos\theta+i\sin\theta)\\
&=300\cos\theta-468\sin\theta
  +i\bigl(468\cos\theta+300\sin\theta\bigr),\\[2mm]
B e^{-i\theta}&=(24+36i)(\cos\theta-i\sin\theta)\\
&=24\cos\theta+36\sin\theta
  +i\bigl(36\cos\theta-24\sin\theta\bigr).
\end{aligned}
\]

Adding the real parts gives  

\[
\operatorname{Re}\Big[(75+117i)z+\frac{96+144i}{z}\Big]
= (300+24)\cos\theta +(-468+36)\sin\theta
= 324\cos\theta-432\sin\theta .
\]

### Maximising \(324\cos\theta-432\sin\theta\)

This is a linear combination of \(\cos\theta\) and \(\sin\theta\):

\[
324\cos\theta-432\sin\theta
= \sqrt{324^2+(-432)^2}\;
   \cos(\theta-\phi),
\]

where \(\phi=\arg(324-432i)\).  
Thus the maximum value is simply the magnitude of the vector
