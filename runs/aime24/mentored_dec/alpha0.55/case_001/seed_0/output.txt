### 2.  Solve for \(x\)

\[
\frac{9}{4-x}+2=\frac{9}{2.4-x}

Let \(A=4-x,\;B=2.4-x\). Then

\[
\frac{9}{A}+2=\frac{9}{B}

\[
9B+2AB=9A

9(-1.6)+2AB=0\quad\Longrightarrow\quad AB=7.2
