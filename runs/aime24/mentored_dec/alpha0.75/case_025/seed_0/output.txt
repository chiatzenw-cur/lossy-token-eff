\begin{aligned}
\text{Let } a&=\log_2x,\; b=\log_2y,\; c=\log_2z. \\
\Rightarrow\; a-b-c&=\tfrac12, \\
b-a-c&=\tfrac13, \\
c-a-b&=\tfrac14.
\end{aligned}
\]

Add all three equations:

\[
(a-b-c)+(b-a-c)+(c-a-b)=\tfrac12+\tfrac13+\tfrac14\Longrightarrow -a-b-c=\frac{13}{12}.
\]

Hence \(a+b+c=-\frac13\!\cdot\!6=-\frac{13}{12}\).

Now solve each variable:

1. \(a-b-c=\tfrac12\) gives  
   \(a-\bigl((a+b+c)-a\bigr)=\tfrac12\Rightarrow 2a-\!\left(-\frac{13}{12}\right)=\tfrac12\)  

   \[
   2a=-\frac{7}{12}\;\Longrightarrow\; a=-\frac{7}{24}.
   \]

2. \(b-a-c=\tfrac13\) gives  
   \(b-\bigl((a+b+c)-b\bigr)=\tfrac13\Rightarrow 2b-\!\left(-\frac{13}{12}\right)=\tfrac13\)  

   \[
   2b=-\frac{9}{12}=-\frac34\;\Longrightarrow\; b=-\frac{3}{8}.
   \]

3. \(c-a-b=\tfrac14\) gives  
   \(c-\bigl((a+b+c)-c\bigr)=\tfrac14\Rightarrow 2c-\!\left(-\frac{13}{12}\right)=\tfrac14\)  

   \[
   2c=-\frac{10}{12}=-\frac56\;\Longrightarrow\; c=-\frac{5}{12}.
   \]

Now find \(\left|\log_2(x^4y^3z^2)\right|\):

\[
\log_2(x^4y^3z^2)=4a+3b+2c
        =4(-\tfrac{7}{24})+3(-\tfrac{3}{8})+2(-\tfrac{5}{12})
        =-\tfrac{7}{6}-\tfrac{9}{8}-\tfrac{5}{6}
        =-\tfrac{25}{8}.
\]

Taking absolute value gives  

\[
\left|\log_2(x^4y^3z^2)\right|=\frac{25}{8}.
\]

Thus \(\displaystyle \frac{m}{n}=\frac{25}{8}\) with \(m,n\) coprime, so  

\[
m+n=25+8=33.
