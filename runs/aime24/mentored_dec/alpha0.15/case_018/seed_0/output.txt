1\,000\,000+100(xy+yz+zx)+xyz
   =100\bigl[30\,000+(xy+yz+zx)\bigr]-2\,000\,000
   =1\,000\,000+100(xy+yz+zx).
\]

Thus

\[
xyz=0 .
\]

--------------------------------------------------------------------
### 3.  Consequence

Since \(x+y+z=0\) and at least one of \(x,y,z\) is zero,
at least one of \(a,b,c\) equals \(100\).

Conversely, if one of \(a,b,c\) equals \(100\), say \(a=100\),
then \(b+c=200\) and the original expression simplifies to

\[
100(b+c)+bc= 20\,000+bc ,
\]
and
\[
a^2b+a^2c+b^2a+b^2c+c^2a+c^2b
=300(bc+200)-3(100bc)=6\,000\,000 .
\]

Hence **every** triple satisfying the conditions is obtained by

* choosing one of the three positions for the value \(100\);
* choosing the other two numbers arbitrarily so that their sum is \(200\).

--------------------------------------------------------------------
### 4.  Counting the ordered triples

For a fixed position of the \(100\) the other two integers
\((u,v)\) satisfy \(u+v=200\) with \(u,v\ge 0\).
The number of ordered pairs \((u,v)\) is \(201\)
(\(u=0,1,\dots ,200\)).

There are three possible positions for \(100\).  
Thus we initially get \(3\times 201=603\) triples.

The triple \((100,100,100)\) is counted three times,
hence we subtract the duplicates \(\;2\).

\[
\text{Total ordered triples}= 603-2=601 .
\]
