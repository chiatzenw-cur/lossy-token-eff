### 1.  Condition for a rhombus

The side lengths are

\[
AB^2=(x_2-x_1)^2+(y_2-y_1)^2 ,\qquad
BC^2=(x_1+x_2)^2+(y_1+y_2)^2 .
\]

Setting \(AB^2=BC^2\) gives  

\[
x_1x_2+y_1y_2=0\;\; \Longleftrightarrow\;\; A\cdot B=0 .
\]

Thus two vertices are orthogonal vectors of the hyperbola.  
The equalities of the other two sides are automatically satisfied.

---

### 2.  Express \(BD^2\)

\[
BD^2=((x_2)-(-x_2))^2+((y_2)-(-y_2))^2
     =4(x_2^2+y_2^2).
\]

Hence \(BD^2\) depends only on the chosen vertex \(B\).

---

### 3.  Feasibility of an orthogonal vertex \(A\)

Let  

\[
u=(-y_2,\;x_2)
\]

be the vector orthogonal to \(B\).  
A point \(A\) must be a scalar multiple of \(u\): \(A=t\,u\).
From \(A\) lying on the hyperbola we require

\[
t^2\Bigl(\frac{(-y_2)^2}{20}-\frac{x_2^2}{24}\Bigr)=1 .
\]

Thus a real solution exists iff  

\[
\frac{y_2^{\,2}}{20}-\frac{x_2^{\,2}}{24}>0 .
\tag{1}
\]

### 4.  Use the hyperbola relation for \(B\)

For \(B\) we have

\[
\frac{x_2^{\,2}}{20}-\frac{y_2^{\,2}}{24}=1

\[
BD^2=4(x_2^{\,2}+y_2^{\,2})
