\[
A = 4(75+117i)=300+468i, \qquad

\[

### 2. Extract the real part

Write \(e^{i\theta}=\cos\theta+i\sin\theta\).  
Then

\[
\begin{aligned}
A\,e^{i\theta} &= (300+468i)(\cos\theta+i\sin\theta)
 = 300\cos\theta-468\sin\theta
   +i(300\sin\theta+468\cos\theta),\\[3pt]
B\,e^{-i\theta} &= (24+36i)(\cos\theta-i\sin\theta)
 = 24\cos\theta+36\sin\theta
   +i(-24\sin\theta+36\cos\theta).
\end{aligned}
\]

Therefore

\[
\operatorname{Re}E(\theta)=
(300+24)\cos\theta+(-468+36)\sin\theta
