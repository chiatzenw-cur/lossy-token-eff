The smallest possible sum of \(n-2\) distinct positive integers is
\(\displaystyle 1+2+\cdots+(n-2) = \frac{(n-2)(n-1)}{2}\).
For \(n=6\) this minimum is \(1+2+3+4=10 < 12\), but for \(n=8\) it is
