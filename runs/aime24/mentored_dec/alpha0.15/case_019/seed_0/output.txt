(\tau^{\frac23})^3-3 \bigl(\tau^{\frac23}-1\bigr)^2=0
\]
which has the sole physically relevant solution \(\tau=4\).
Correspondingly,

\[
\theta=\frac{\pi}{3},\qquad
x=\frac{1}{8},\qquad
y=\frac{3\sqrt3}{8}.
\]

Thus

\[
C=\left(\frac18,\frac{3\sqrt3}{8}\right).
\]

The squared distance from the origin is

\[
OC^2=\Bigl(\frac18\Bigr)^2+\Bigl(\frac{3\sqrt3}{8}\Bigr)^2
=\frac{1}{64}+\frac{27}{64}=\frac{28}{64}=\frac{7}{16}.
\]

Hence in lowest terms \(OC^2=\dfrac{7}{16}\), so \(p+q=7+16=23\).
