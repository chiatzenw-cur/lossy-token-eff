--------------------------------------------------------------------
### Real part of the expression  

\[
\begin{aligned}
\Re(E)&=
\Re\bigl(C_{1}(c+is)\bigr)+\Re\bigl(C_{2}(c-is)\bigr)\\
&=(a_1c-b_1s)+(a_2c+b_2s)\quad\text{with}\quad 
      C_{1}=a_1+ib_1,\; C_{2}=a_2+ib_2,
\end{aligned}
\]

where  
\(a_1=300,\;b_1=468,\; a_2=24,\;b_2=36.\)

Thus  

\[
\Re(E)= (a_1+a_2) c +(-b_1+b_2)s
      =324\cos\theta-432\sin\theta.

\]

### Midpoint method

The expression has the form \(A\cos\theta+B\sin\theta\) with  
\(A=324,\;B=-432\).  
For any real \(\theta\),

\[
\bigl(A\cos\theta+B\sin\theta\bigr)^2
  \le A^2+B^2
\]

\[
\Re(E)\le \sqrt{\,324^{2}+(-432)^2\,}
