--------------------------------------------------------------------
### 1.  Relating the columns

From (2)

\[
10(a+b+c)+(d+e+f)=99.
\]

Let  

\[
s=a+b+c,\qquad t=d+e+f .
\]

Then

\[
10s+t=99 \tag{3}
\]

--------------------------------------------------------------------
### 2.  Considering the addition of the two 3‑digit numbers

From (1) add the column‑by‑column

* Units: \(c+f\) must give a unit digit \(9\).  
  Since \(c+f \le 18\), only \(c+f=9\) is possible (no carry).
* Tens: \(b+e\) must give a tens digit \(9\) with no carry, so \(b+e=9\).
* Hundreds: \(a+d\) must give a hundreds digit \(9\) with no carry, hence \(a+d=9\).

Subtracting gives

\[
a+d=9,\qquad b+e=9,\qquad c+f=9. \tag{4}
\]

Thus  

\[
d=9-a,\quad e=9-b,\quad f=9-c .
\]

Consequently  

\[
t=d+e+f=(9-a)+(9-b)+(9-c)=27-(a+b+c)=27-s. \tag{5}
\]

--------------------------------------------------------------------
### 3.  Integrating the two sets of conditions

From (3) and (5):

\[
27-s=t=99-10s \Longrightarrow 27-s=99-10s
\]
\[
\Rightarrow 9s=72 \Longrightarrow s=a+b+c = 8.
\]

Then \(t = 27-8 = 19\).

### 4.  Counting solutions

We know  

\[
a+b+c=8,\qquad a,b,c\in\{0,1,\dots ,9\}.
\]

\[
\binom{8+3-1}{3-1}=\binom{10}{2}=45 .
\]
