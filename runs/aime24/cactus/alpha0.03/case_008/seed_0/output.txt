### Convert to natural logarithms

Let \(L_x=\ln x\) and \(L_y=\ln y\).

1. \(y^x=x^{10}\Rightarrow x\ln y=10\ln x\)  
   \[
   xL_y=10L_x \tag{1}
   \]
2. \(x^{4y}=y^{10}\Rightarrow 4y\ln x=10\ln y\)  
   \[
   4yL_x=10L_y \tag{2}
   \]

---

### Relate the two equations

From (1):
\[
L_y=\frac{10L_x}{x}.
\]

From (2):
\[
L_y=\frac{4yL_x}{10}=\frac{2yL_x}{5}.
\]

Set the two expressions for \(L_y\) equal and cancel \(L_x>0\) (since \(x>1\)):

\[
\frac{10}{x}=\frac{2y}{5}\quad\Longrightarrow\quad 50=2xy.
\]

---

### Find \(xy\)

\[
xy=\frac{50}{2}=25.
\]
