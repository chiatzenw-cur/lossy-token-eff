### Condition for an orthogonal partner

For a given \(Q=(x_2,y_2)\) that lies on the hyperbola, the equation of points
\(P=(x_1,y_1)\) satisfying \(P\cdot Q=0\) is

\[
x_1x_2+y_1y_2=0\quad\Longrightarrow\quad y_1=-\frac{x_2}{y_2}x_1 .
\]

Substituting this into the hyperbola equation gives

\[
\frac{x_{1}^{2}}{20}-\frac{(x_2/x_2\,x_1)^2}{24}=1
   \Longrightarrow x_{1}^{2}
   =\frac{1}{\frac{1}{20}-\frac{x_2^{2}}{24y_2^{2}}}\; .
\]

Thus \(x_1^{2}>0\) requires

\[
\frac{1}{20} > \frac{x_2^{2}}{24y_2^{2}}
\Longleftrightarrow
24y_2^{2} > 20x_2^{2}
\Longleftrightarrow
\frac{y_2^{2}}{x_2^{2}}>\frac{5}{6}
\Longleftrightarrow
\byint .
\]

Using standard hyperbola parametrisation,
\[
\frac{y_2^{2}}{x_2^{2}} = \frac{24\sinh ^{2}t}{20\cosh ^{2}t}
       =  \frac{6\,\sinh t}{5\,\cosh t}
       =\frac{6}{5}\tanh t .
\]
Thus the inequality becomes  
\[
\frac{6}{5}\tanh t > \frac{5}{6}\quad\Longrightarrow\quad
\tanh t > \frac{5}{6}.
\]

So an orthogonal partner \(P\) exists **iff** the hyperbola parameter
\(t\) of \(Q\) satisfies \(\tanh t>5/6\).
