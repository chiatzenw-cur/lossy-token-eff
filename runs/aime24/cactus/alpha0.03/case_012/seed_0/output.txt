<|end|><|start|>assistant<|channel|>final<|message|>To go from the lower–left corner to the upper–right corner of an \(8\times 8\) grid we must make

\[
8 \text{ steps right (R)} and\; 8 \text{ steps up (U)}
\]

so the path consists of \(16\) unit moves, each move being either \(R\) or \(U\).

--------------------------------------------------------------------
### Exactly four “turns”

A change of direction occurs when an \(R\) is followed by a \(U\) or a \(U\) is followed by an \(R\).
If there are exactly four changes, the path is divided into

\[
\text{number of runs}=4+1=5
\]

alternating horizontal and vertical segments.  
There are two possible alternating patterns:

* \(R\!-\!U\!-\!R\!-\!U\!-\!R\)  (start horizontal)  
* \(U\!-\!R\!-\!U\!-\!R\!-\!U\)  (start vertical)

We count each arrangement separately.

--------------------------------------------------------------------
### 1.  Pattern \(R\!-\!U\!-\!R\!-\!U\!-\!R\)

Let the horizontal run lengths be \(h_1,r_3,h_5\) and the vertical run lengths be \(u_1,u_2,u_3\)

\[
\begin{cases}
h_1+h_3+h_5 = 8, &h_i\ge 1,\\[2pt]
u_1+u_2+u_3 = 8, &u_i\ge 1.
\end{cases}
\]

The number of ways to write \(8\) as a sum of \(3\) positive integers is

\[
\binom{8-1}{3-1}=\binom{7}{2}=21 .
\]

Thus horizontal choices \(=21\) and vertical choices \(=21\).

\[
\text{Pattern R–U–R–U–R} : 21 \times 21 = 441 \text{ paths.}
\]

--------------------------------------------------------------------
### 2.  Pattern \(U\!-\!R\!-\!U\!-\!R\!-\!U\)

Now the vertical runs are \(u_1,u_3,u_5\) (three of them) and the horizontal runs are \(h_2,h_4\) (two of them).

\[
\begin{cases}
u_1+u_3+u_5 = 8, &u_i\ge 1,\\[2pt]
h_2+h_4 = 8, &h_i\ge 1.
\end{cases}
\]

Number of ways:

\[
\binom{7}{2}=21 \quad\text{(vertical)},\qquad
\binom{7}{1}=7 \quad\text{(horizontal)}.
\]

Hence

\[
\text{Pattern U–R–U–R–U} : 21 \times 7 = 147 \text{ paths.}
\]

--------------------------------------------------------------------
### Total

\[
\boxed{\, 441 + 147 = 588 \,}
\]

Therefore, there are \(588\) different paths of length \(16\) that change direction exactly four times.
