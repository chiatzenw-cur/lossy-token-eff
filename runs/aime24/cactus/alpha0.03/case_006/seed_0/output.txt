\]

### 2.  The volume

From the coordinates we also get the cross product  
\(\vec{AB}\times \vec{AC}=(0,0,\sqrt{3024}),\) hence

\[
V=\frac{1}{6}\bigl|(\vec{AB}\times\vec{AC})\!\cdot\!\vec{AD}\bigr|
      =\frac{1}{6}\sqrt{3024}\;z = 2\sqrt{21}\;z,
\]

where \(z\) is the \(z\)-coordinate of \(D\) (the perpendicular component of the
vector \(\vec{AD}\)).

--------------------------------------------------------------------
### 3.  Computing \(z\)

Let \(D=(x,y,z)\).  
With the remaining distances we have

\[
\begin{cases}
x^2+y^2+z^2=89,\\[2mm]
(x-\sqrt{41})^2+y^2+z^2=80,\\[2mm]
\Bigl(x-\dfrac{16}{\sqrt{41}}\Bigr)^2 +\bigl(y-\sqrt{\dfrac{3024}{41}}\bigr)^2+z^2=41,
\end{cases}
\]

which allow the elimination of \(x\) and \(y\) and give

\[
z^2= \frac{441\,094\,400}{13\,026\,069}.
\]

Because \(z>0\),

\[
z=\sqrt{\frac{441\,094\,400}{13\,026\,069}}=
\frac{80}{\sqrt{189}}\approx 5.817 .
\]

--------------------------------------------------------------------
### 4.  The inradius

The formula for the inradius gives

\[
\rho=\frac{3V}{S}=
\frac{3\bigl(2\sqrt{21}\,z\bigr)}{24\sqrt{21}}
   =\frac{z}{4}
   =\frac{\,80\,}{4\sqrt{189}}
   =\frac{20}{\sqrt{189}}.
\]

Now \(\sqrt{189}=3\sqrt{21}\) and

\[
\rho=\frac{20}{3\sqrt{21}}
     =\frac{20\sqrt{21}}{63}.
\]

This has the format \(\dfrac{m\sqrt{n}}{p}\) with  
\(m=20,\; n=21,\; p=63\) (\(m\) and \(p\) are coprime, \(n\) is
square‑free).

--------------------------------------------------------------------
### 5.  Result

\[
m+n+p = 20+21+63 = 104.
\]

\[
\boxed{104}
