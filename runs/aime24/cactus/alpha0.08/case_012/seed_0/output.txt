### 1.  What is a “change of direction”?

A direction change occurs when a horizontal step is followed by vertical step or vice‑versa.  
If a path changes direction exactly four times, the path is made of **five linear
segments** (either horizontal or vertical).  

Consequently the five segments must alternate in direction.  
Two possibilities exist:

* start with horizontal: \(H_1 V_1 H_2 V_2 H_3\) – 3 horizontal, 2 vertical segments  
* start with vertical:   \(V_1 H_1 V_2 H_2 V_3\) – 3 vertical, 2 horizontal segments  

--------------------------------------------------------------------

### 2.  Splitting the 8 horizontal and 8 vertical movements

Let the lengths of the horizontal and vertical segments be

\[
\begin{array}{l}
H_1+H_2+H_3 = 8 ,\qquad H_i\ge 1 \quad  (i=1,2,3)\\
V_1 + V_2     = 8 ,\qquad V_i\ge 1 \quad  (i=1,2)
\end{array}
\]

The number of positive integer solutions of \(H_1+H_2+H_3=8\) is

\[
\binom{8-1}{3-1}= \binom{7}{2}=21 .
\]

The number of positive integer solutions of \(V_1+V_2=8\) is

\[
\binom{8-1}{2-1}= \binom{7}{1}=7 .
\]

Thus, for a start with horizontal the number of possibilities is

\[
21 \times 7 = 147 .
\]

Analogously

* any start with vertical gives another 147.

### 3.  Total number of paths

\[
\text{Total count} = 147 + 147 = 294 .
\]
