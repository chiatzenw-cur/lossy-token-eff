### 1.  How many \(B\) have a given maximum?

For a fixed \(a\in A\), the set \(\{\,B\mid \max B=a,\;B\neq\varnothing\,\}\)

* must contain \(a\);
* may contain any subset of \(\{1,\dots ,a-1\}\);
* may contain no element larger than \(a\).

Thus \(\max B=a\) is obtained by taking the element  
\(\{a\}\) together with an arbitrary subset of the remaining \(a-1\) available
numbers.  
Hence the number of such subsets is

\[
2^{\,a-1}\qquad (a=1,2,3,\dots).
\]

--------------------------------------------------------------------
### 2.  The total number of sets Bob lists

From the statement, Bob’s list contains \(2024\) sets, so

\[
\sum_{a\in A}2^{\,a-1}=2024. \tag{1}
\]
