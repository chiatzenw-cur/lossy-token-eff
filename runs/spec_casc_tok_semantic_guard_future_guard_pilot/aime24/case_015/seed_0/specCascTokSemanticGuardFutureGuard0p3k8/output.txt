### 2.  Hyperbolic parametrisation

Write the hyperbola in the standard form  

\[
x^{2}a^{-2}-y^{2}b^{-2}=1,\qquad a^2=20,\;b^2=24.
\]

Hence we can parameterise

\[
u=a\cosh s,\;v=b\sinh s,\qquad
p=a\cosh t,\;q=b\sinh t,
\]

With this substitution (2) becomes  

\[
20\cosh s\cosh t+24\sinh s\sinh t=0.
\]

Using \(\cosh s\cosh t=\tfrac12(\cosh(s+t)+\cosh(s-t))\) and
\(\sinh s\sinh t=\tfrac12(\cosh(s+t)-\cosh(s-t))\) we obtain

\[
22\cosh(s+t)-2\cosh(s-t)=0

\cosh(s-t)=11\cosh(s+t). \tag{3}
\]

### 3.  Expressing the condition in terms of \(t\)

\[
\cosh(x-t)=11\cosh(x+t)

\[
5\cosh x\cosh t + 6\sinh x\sinh t =0 .
