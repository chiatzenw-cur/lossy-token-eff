### Step 1: Use the Inclusion-Exclusion Principle

We start by finding the probability that a student likes **either** blue or green (or both). Using the **Inclusion-Exclusion Principle**:

$$
P(\text{Blue} \cup \text{Green}) = P(\text{Blue}) + P(\text{Green}) - P(\text{Blue} \cap \text{Green})
$$

Substituting the given values:

$$
P(\text{Blue} \cup \text{Green}) = 58\% + 45\% - 22\% = 103\% - 22\% = 81\%
$$

This tells us that 81% of the students like **at least one** of the colors (blue or green).

---

### Step 2: Find the Complement

Now, the probability that a student likes **neither** blue nor green is the complement of the above probability:

$$
P(\text{Neither}) = 100\% - P(\text{Blue} \cup \text{Green}) = 1 - 0.81 = 0.19
$$
