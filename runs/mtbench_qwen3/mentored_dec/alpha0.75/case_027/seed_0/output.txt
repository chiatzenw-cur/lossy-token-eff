Photosynthesis is divided into two main stages: **the light-dependent reactions** and **the Calvin cycle (light-independent reactions)**. Here’s a breakdown of each stage, including their locations and primary inputs and outputs:

---

### **1. Light-Dependent Reactions**  
- **Location**: Thylakoid membranes of the chloroplast.  
- **Primary Inputs**:  
  - **Light energy** (sunlight)  
  - **Water (H₂O)**  
- **Primary Outputs**:  
  - **ATP** (adenosine triphosphate)  
  - **NADPH** (nicotinamide adenine dinucleotide phosphate)  
  - **Oxygen (O₂)** (as a byproduct)  

**Process**:  
- Light energy is absorbed by chlorophyll and other pigments in the thylakoid membrane.  
- Water is split into oxygen (released into the atmosphere), protons (H⁺), and electrons (a process called **photolysis**).  
- The energy from light is used to drive the synthesis of ATP and NADPH via **chemiosmosis** and the **electron transport chain**.

---

### **2. Calvin Cycle (Light-Independent Reactions)**  
- **Location**: Stroma of the chloroplast.  
- **Primary Inputs**:  
  - **ATP** (from light reactions)  
  - **NADPH** (from light reactions)  
  - **Carbon dioxide (CO₂)**  
- **Primary Outputs**:  
  - **Glucose (C₆H₁₂O₆)** (a sugar used for energy and biomass)  
  - **ADP** and **NADP⁺** (which are recycled back to the light reactions)  

**Process**:  
- CO₂ is fixed into a 5-carbon molecule (RuBP) through the enzyme **RuBisCO**, forming a 6-carbon intermediate that splits into two molecules of 3-phosphoglycerate (3-PGA).  
- ATP and NADPH are used to convert 3-PGA into **G3P** (glyceraldehyde-3-phosphate), a 3-carbon sugar.  
- Some G3P molecules are used to synthesize glucose and other carbohydrates, while others regenerate RuBP to sustain the cycle.

---

### **Summary Table**  
| Stage                | Location       | Inputs                          | Outputs                          |  
|----------------------|----------------|----------------------------------|-----------------------------------|  
| Light-Dependent Reactions | Thylakoid membranes | Light, Water (H₂O)              | ATP, NADPH, O₂ (byproduct)        |  
| Calvin Cycle         | Stroma         | ATP, NADPH, CO₂                  | Glucose (C₆H₁₂O₆), ADP, NADP⁺     |  

This division of labor ensures efficient use of light energy to produce energy-rich molecules (ATP/NADPH) and the synthesis of organic molecules (glucose) from CO₂.
