### Step 1: Analyze the Given Statements

- **Statement 1**: *Oranges cost more than apples.*

  $$
  O > A
  $$

- **Statement 2**: *Oranges cost less than bananas.*  
  That becomes:  
  $$
  O < B
  $$  
  where $ B $ is the price of bananas.

---

### Step 2: Use the Information to Derive Relationships

From these two inequalities, we can deduce:

$$
O > A \quad \text{and} \quad O < B
$$

Combining these two using the **transitive property**:

$$
\text{If } O < B \text{ and } O > A, \text{ then } B > A \text{ (since both are related to } O \text{).}
$$

This implies that **bananas cost more than apples**.
