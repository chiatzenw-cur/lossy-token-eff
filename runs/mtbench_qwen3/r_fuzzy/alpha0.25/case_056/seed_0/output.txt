### **5. Common Misconceptions**

- **"Previous events don’t affect the next"**  
  - Example: If you flip a fair coin and **get ** heads **3 times in a row**, the chance is still 50% for the next flip to be heads.

- **"Probability is about predicting exact outcomes"**  
  - Probability tells you the likelihood, not the outcome. It helps you understand **patterns** over time, not a single chance.
