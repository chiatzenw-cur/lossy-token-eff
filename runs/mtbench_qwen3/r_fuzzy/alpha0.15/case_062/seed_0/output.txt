Using the **inclusion-exclusion principle**, the formula for the probability that a student likes either blue or green is:

$$
P(\text{Blue} \cup \text{Green}) = P(\text{Blue}) + P(\text{Green}) - P(\text{Blue} \cap \text{Green})
$$

Substituting the values:

$$
P(\text{Blue} \cup \text{Green}) = 0.58 + 0.45 - 0.22 = 0.81
$$
