**Understanding Probability: A Simple Guide**

Probability is a way to measure how likely an event (like flipping a coin or rolling a die) is to happen, based on the number of possible outcomes. Think of it as a math tool to predict the chance of something occurring. Here's how to break it down:

---

### **1. Basic Concept: What Is Probability?**
Probability tells you the chance of an event happening, expressed as a number between **0 and 1** (or 0% to 100%).  
- **0** means the event is **impossible** (e.g., "It will rain on the moon tomorrow").  **1** means **certain** (e.g., "The moon is round").

---

### **2. Step-by-Step to Calculate Probability**
Follow the steps to calculate the probability of an event:

#### **Step 1: Identify the Event**
Example:
- "What’s the probability of rolling a 4 if I roll a fair die?"

#### **Step 2: Determine Total Possible Outcomes**
A standard die has **6 sides** (1, 2, 3, 4, 5, 6). So, **6 possible outcomes**.

#### **Step 3: Count Favorable Outcomes**
How many outcomes match the event?  
- Rolling a 4 Read **1 favorable outcome** (only the number "4").

#### **Step 4: Divide Favorable by Total**
Probability = (Favorable Outcomes) / (Total Outcomes)  
**Probability of rolling a 4 = **1/6 ≈ 0.0167 (or 167%)**.
