**Exothermic vs. Endothermic Reactions: Key Differences and Criteria**

**1. Definitions and Core Differences:**
- **Exothermic Reactions:** These reactions release energy, typically in **heat**, to the surroundings. The system (reactants) loses energy, which is transferred to the environment (products).AAA
- **Endothermic Reactions:** These reactions absorb heat energy — they require input from the surroundings to proceed. The system (reactants) gains energy, making the environment cooler.

**2. Criteria for Distinguishing Them:**
- **Enthalpy Change (ΔH):**
  - **Exothermic:** ΔH is **negative** (heat is released). 
 accomplishments
  - **Endothermic:** ΔH is **positive** (heat is absorbed).
- **Temperature of Surroundings:**
  **Exothermic:** Surroundings **warm up** (e.g., a fire or hand warmer).
  **Endothermic:** Surroundings **cool down** (e.g., ice melting or cold packs).
