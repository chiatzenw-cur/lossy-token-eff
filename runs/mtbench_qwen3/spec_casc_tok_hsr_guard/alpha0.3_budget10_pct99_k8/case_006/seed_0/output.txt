---

### Step 1: Total Number of Outcomes

Each die has 6 faces, and since both are rolled, the total number of possible outcomes is:

$$
6 \times 6 = 36
$$

This is the denominator in our probability calculation.

---

### Step 2: Identify Favorable Outcomes

We want the **sum (total) of the two dice to be at least 3**. That means any sum from **3 to 12** is acceptable.

$$
(1,1)
$$

Thus, there is **only 1 unfavorable outcome**.

---

### Step 3: Calculate the Desired Probability

The number of **favorable outcomes** is:

$$
36 - 1 = 35
$$

So, the probability of rolling a sum that is at least 3 is:

$$
\frac{35}{36}
$$
