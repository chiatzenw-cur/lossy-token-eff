---

### Step 1: Understand the Given Information

Let:
- $ P(\text{Blue}) = 58\% = 0.58 $
- $ P(\text{Green}) = 45\% = 0.45 $
- $ P(\text{Blue and Green}) = 22\% = 0.22 $

We are asked to find:

$$
P(\text{Neither Blue nor Green}) = 1 - P(\text{Blue or Green})
$$

---

### Step 2: Apply the Inclusion-Exclusion Principle

The probability that a student likes **either** blue or green (or both) is:

$$
P(\text{Blue or Green}) = P(\text{Blue}) + P(\text{Green}) - P(\text{Blue and Green})
$$

$$
= 0.58 + 0.45 - 0.22 = 0.81
$$

---

### Step 3: Subtract from Total Probability

The probability that a student likes **neither** is:

$$
P(\text{Neither}) = 1 - P(\text{Blue or Green}) = 1 - 0.81 = 0.19
$$

---

### Final Result

Thus, the probability that a randomly picked student would like **neither** blue **nor** green is:

$$
\boxed{0.19}
