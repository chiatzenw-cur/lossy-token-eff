### 📘 **What is Probability?**
Probability is a number between **0 and 1** (or 0% to 100%) that tells you how likely an event is to occur.  
- **0** means the event **will never happen** (e.g., "if it’s raining outside, you won’t fly a rocket ship").  
- **1** means the event **will definitely happen** (e.g., "if you drop a cup, it will fall").  
- **1/2 or 50%** means it’s **just as likely** to happen or not happen (e.g., flipping a coin).

### 🧮 **How to Calculate Probability (Step-by-Step)**  
**Formula:**  
$$
\text{Probability} = \frac{\text{Number of favorable outcomes}}{\text{Total number of possible outcomes}}
$$
