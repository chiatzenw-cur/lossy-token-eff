### Step 1: Use Inclusion-Exclusion Principle

We apply the inclusion-exclusion principle to find the probability that a student likes **either** blue **or** green:

$$
P(\text{Blue} \cup \text{Green}) = P(\text{Blue}) + P(\text{Green}) - P(\text{Blue} \cap \text{Green})
$$

Substituting the values:

$$
P(\text{Blue} \cup \text{Green}) = 0.58 + 0.45 - 0.22 = 0.81
$$

So, **81%** of students like either blue or green (or both).

### Step 2: Find the Complement

The probability that a student **likes neither** blue nor green is the complement of the probability that they like **either**:

$$
P(\text{Neither}) = 1 - P(\text{Blue} \cup \text{Green}) = 1 - 0.81 = 0.19
$$
