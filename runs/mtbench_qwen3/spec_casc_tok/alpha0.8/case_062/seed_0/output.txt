We are given the following:
- $ P(B) = 58\% = 0.58 $: Probability of liking blue
- $ P(G) = 45\% = 0.45 $: Probability of liking green
- $ P(B \cap G) = 22\% = 0.22 $: Probability of liking both blue and green

---

### **Step 2: Use the Inclusion-Exclusion Principle**
The formula to find the probability of a student liking **either blue or green** (or both) is:

$$
P(B \cup G) = P(B) + P(G) - P(B \cap G)
$$

Substituting the values:

$$
P(B \cup G) = 0.58 + 0.45 - 0.22 = 0.81
$$

So, the probability that a student likes **blue or green** (including both) is **81%**.

---

### **Step 3: Find the Complement**
The students who like **neither** blue **nor** green are the complement of those who like either color:

$$
P(\text{neither}) = 1 - P(B \cup G) = 1 - 0.81 = 0.19
$$
