To solve the inequality:

$$
|x + 5| < 10
$$

We use the standard approach for absolute value inequalities of the form $ |A| < B $, which means:

$$
-B < A < B
$$

---

### Step 1: Translate the Inequality

Applying this to the given inequality:

$$
-10 < x + 5 < 10
$$

Now, isolate $ x $ by subtracting 5 from all three parts:

$$
-10 - 5 < x + 5 - 5 < 10 - 5
$$

$$
-15 < x < 5
$$

---

### Step 2: Identify Integer Solutions

We are looking for all integers $ x $ that satisfy the condition:

$$
-15 < x < 5
$$

Note: The endpoints, $-15$ and $5$, are **not included** because the inequality is strict.

So the integers that satisfy the inequality are those strictly greater than $-15$ and strictly less than $5$. That is:

$$
x \in \{-14, -13, -12, \dots, 4\}
$$

---

### Step 3: Count the Integers

We count the total number of integers from $-14$ to $4$ inclusive.

This can be done using the formula for counting integers in an inclusive range:

$$
\text{Number of integers} = \text{Upper bound} - \text{Lower bound} + 1
$$

Here:

$$
\text{Upper bound} = 4, \quad \text{Lower bound} = -14
$$

$$
\text{Number of integers} = 4 - (-14) + 1 = 4 + 14 + 1 = 19
$$

---

### Final Answer

$$
\boxed{19}
$$
