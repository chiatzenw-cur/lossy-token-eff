The reaction between solid calcium carbonate (CaCO₃) and hydrochloric acid (HCl) is a **neutralization reaction** (specifically an **acid-carbonate reaction**), where the acid reacts with the carbonate to produce a salt (calcium chloride), water, and carbon dioxide gas. Here's the detailed breakdown:

---

### **Balanced Chemical Equation**  
$$
\text{CaCO}_3(s) + 2\text{HCl}(aq) \rightarrow \text{CaCl}_2(aq) + \text{H}_2\text{O}(l) + \text{CO}_2(g)
$$

- **Reactants**:  
  - **CaCO₃ (solid)**: Calcium carbonate, a base-like compound.  
  - **HCl (aqueous)**: Hydrochloric acid, a strong acid.  
- **Products**:  
  - **CaCl₂ (aqueous)**: Calcium chloride, a salt.  
  - **H₂O (liquid)**: Water.  
  - **CO₂ (gas)**: Carbon dioxide, released as bubbles.  

---

### **Type of Reaction**  
This is an **acid-base neutralization reaction**, where the carbonate (CO₃²⁻) in calcium carbonate reacts with hydrogen ions (H⁺) from hydrochloric acid. Alternatively, it can be classified as a **double displacement reaction** because the ions exchange partners (Ca²⁺ combines with Cl⁻, and H⁺ combines with CO₃²⁻ to form CO₂ and H₂O). However, the **primary classification** is **neutralization** due to the interaction between acid and carbonate.

---

### **Observations**  
1. **Effervescence (Bubbling)**:  
   - Carbon dioxide gas (CO₂) is released as bubbles, indicating the formation of gas.  
   - This is a key visual clue that the reaction is occurring.  

2. **Dissolution of Solid**:  
   - The solid calcium carbonate gradually dissolves in the acidic solution as the reaction proceeds.  

3. **Temperature Change**:  
   - The reaction is exothermic (releases heat), so the solution may warm up slightly.  

4. **Formation of a Colorless Solution**:  
   - Calcium chloride (CaCl₂) and water (H₂O) are both colorless, so the solution remains clear.  

---

### **Summary**  
- **Reaction Type**: Acid-base neutralization (or acid-carbonate reaction).  
- **Key Indicators**: Bubbling (CO₂ gas), dissolution of the solid, and possibly heat release.  
- **Balanced Equation**:  
  $$
  \text{CaCO}_3(s) + 2\text{HCl}(aq) \rightarrow \text{CaCl}_2(aq) + \text{H}_2\text{O}(l) + \text{CO}_2(g)
  $$
