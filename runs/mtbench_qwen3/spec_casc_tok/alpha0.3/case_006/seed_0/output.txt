$$
6 \times 6 = 36 \text{ total outcomes}
$$

---

### Understanding the Problem

We are asked to find the probability that the **sum of the two dice is at least 3**. That is, we want the probability that the total sum of the two dice is **3 or higher**.

Let’s verify this by considering the **complementary event**—i.e., the event where the sum is **less than 3**.

- The **minimum possible sum** of two dice is **2** (when both dice show 1).
- The **only** outcome that results in a sum less than 3 is **(1,1)**, which gives a sum of **2**.
- Thus, there is **just 1** outcome that is **not favorable**.

---

### Calculating the Desired Probability

Now, subtract the number of unfavorable outcomes from the total number of outcomes:

$$
36 - 1 = 35 \text{ favorable outcomes}
$$

Therefore, the probability of rolling a total of **at least 3** is:

$$
\frac{35}{36}
$$

$$
\boxed{\dfrac{35}{36}}
