To solve the inequality:

$$
|x + 5| < 10
$$

we begin by recalling that the absolute value inequality $ |A| < B $ (where $ B > 0 $) is equivalent to:

$$
-B < A < B
$$

Applying this to the given expression:

$$
-10 < x + 5 < 10
$$

---

**Step 1: Solve the compound inequality**

Subtract 5 from all parts:

$$
-10 - 5 < x + 5 - 5 < 10 - 5
$$
$$
-15 < x < 5
$$

This tells us that only values of $ x $ strictly between -15 and 5 (i.e., **not including -15 and 5**) are solutions.

---

**Step 2: Count the integers in this interval**

Since the solution is between -15 and 5 (exclusive), we are interested in the integers:

$$
x = -14, -13, -12, \ldots, -1, 0, 1, 2, 3, 4
$$

  $$
  That’s **19** integers.

- **Formula:** The number of integers between $ a $ and $ b $ (inclusive) is:
  $$
  \text{Count} = b - a + 1
  $$

  $$
  \text{Count} = 4 - (-14) + 1 = 4 + 14 + 1 = 19
