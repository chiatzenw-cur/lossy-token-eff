### 📌 **What is Probability?**  
Probability is a way to **estimate the chance of something happening**. It answers questions like:  
- *What’s the chance it will rain tomorrow?*  
- *What’s my chance of winning a game?*  
- *How likely is it to roll a 6 on a die?*  

### 📚 **Basic Formula**  
The core idea is:  
- **Probability = (Number of favorable outcomes) ÷ (Total number of possible outcomes)**  

This gives a number between **0 and 1**, where:  
- **0** means the event is **impossible** (e.g., probability of living forever is 0).  
- **1** means the event is **certain** (e.g., probability of the sun rising tomorrow is 1).  
- **Numbers between 0 and 1** show how likely the event is.  

### 🧩 **Step-by-Step Examples**  
#### Example 1: Coin Flip  
- **Event**: Flipping a fair coin and getting heads.  
- **Favorable outcomes**: 1 (heads).  
- **Total outcomes**: 2 (heads or tails).  
- **Probability**: 1avers/2 = **0.5** (or 50%).  

### Example 2: Drawing a Marble  
- **Bag contents**: 3 red marbles, 2 blue marbles, 1 green marble.  
- **Event**: Drawing a red marble.  
- **Favorable outcomes**: 3 (red marbles).  
- **Total outcomes**: 6 (3:2:1).  
- **Probability**: 3/6 = **0.5** (or 50%).  

### Example 3: Rolling a Die  
- **Event**: Rolling a 4 on a **six-sided die**.  
- **Favorable outcomes**: 1 (only four isvascular).  
- **Total outcomes**: 6 (numbers 1 through 6).  
- **Probability**: 1/6 ≈ **0.17** (or 17%).

### �vc **How to Apply It in Real Life**  
1. **Weather Forecast**:  
   - If the forecast says “50% chance of rain,” it means **half the time** it rains in similar conditions.  

2. **Games**:  
   - When you roll a die, the probability of rolling a 6 is **1/6**, so over many rolls, you’d expect it to happen about 1 out of 6 times.  

3. **Decision Making**:  
   - If you need to choose between two options, say, a trip, probability can help you compare your chances of success (e.g., choosing a Route A vs. Route B) if the outcome depends on something uncertain.  

---

### 📌 **Theoretical vs. Empirical Probability**  
- **Theoretical**: Based on reasoning (e.g., a fair coin has 50% chance of heads).  
- **Empirical**: Based on real-world data (e.g., if you flip a coin 100 times and get 55 heads, it’s **55%** heads).  

---

### 🚨 **Common Pitfalls**  
- **Assuming all outcomes are equally likely** (e.g., thinking a 7 is more likely to roll on two dice than a 2, but in reality, both are rare).  
- **Confusing "probability" with "certainty"**:  
  - If the probability of rain is 50%, that doesn’t mean it’s guaranteed to rain, just as likely as not to rain.
