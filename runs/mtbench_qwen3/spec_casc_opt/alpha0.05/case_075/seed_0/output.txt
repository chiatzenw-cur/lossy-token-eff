**Machine Learning Malk**  
Machine learning (ML) is a branch of artificial intelligence that enables systems to learn patterns from data, improving their performance on a task over time without being explicitly programmed. The core idea is to develop algorithms that can identify patterns, make predictions, or take decisions by analyzing historical data and adjusting their parameters to minimize errors or optimize outcomes.
