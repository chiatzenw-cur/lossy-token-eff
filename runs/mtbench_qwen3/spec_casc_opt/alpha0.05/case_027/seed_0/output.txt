Photosynthesis is a **two-stage process** that converts light energy into chemical energy, essential for life on Earth. Here's a structured outline of each stage, including their locations in the chloroplast and where they occur:

---

### **1. Light-Dependent Reactions (Light Reactions)**  
- **Location**: **Thylakoid Membranes** of the chlorophyll.  
- **Inputs**:  
  - **Sunlight** (light energy).  
  - **Water (H2O)**.  
  - **Chlorophyll** (to absorb light energy).  
- **Outputs**:  
  - **Adenosine triphosphate (ATP)**.  
  - **Nicotinamide adenine dinucleotide phosphate (NADPH)**.  
  - **Oxygen (O2)** (a byproduct of water splitting).  

**Process**:  
- Light energy is absorbed by chlorophyll, exciting electrons.  
- Water (H2O) is split into oxygen (O2), protons (H+), and electrons (a process called **photolysis**).  
- The energy from excited electrons is used to create ATP (via ATP synthase) and NADPH (via the electron transport chain).  
- **O2 is released into the atmosphere**.

---

### **2. Calvin Cycle (Light-Independent Reactions or Dark Reactions)**  
- **Location**: **Stroma** of the chlorophyll.  
- **Inputs**:  
  - **Carbon dioxide (CO2)**.  
  - **ATP** (from light reactions).  
  - **NADPH** (from light reactions).  
- **Outputs**:  
  - **Glucose (C6H12O6)** (or other organic molecules) and **regeneration of ATP and NADPH** for reuse.  
  - **No ATP or NADPH** is regenerated in this stage (they are consumed).  

**Process**:  
  - **Carbon fixation**: CO2 is converted into organic molecules (e.g., ribulose bisphosphate, RuBP) using ATP and NADPH.  
  - **Reduction**: The fixed carbon is reduced to form **glyceraldehyde-3-phosphate (G3P)**, a sugar-like molecule.  
  - **Regeneration**: Some G3P is used to replenish RuBP, allowing the cycle to continue.  
  - **Final product**: carbohydate (C6H12O6) is used to synthesize glucose, which is stored or used for energy.

---

### **Summary**:  
- **Light Reactions**: Convert light energy into chemical energy (ATP and NADPH) and release oxygen.  
- **Calvin Cycle**: Uses ATP and NADPH to fix CO2 into glucose, producing the sugars needed for plant growth and energy storage.  

### **Key Equations**:  
- Light reactions:  
  $$ 2H_2O + 2NADP + 3ADP + 3Pi \xrightarrow{\text{light}} O_2 + 2NADPH + 3ATP + 2H^+ $$  
- Calvin cycle:  
  $$ 4CO2 + 12ATP + 12NADPH + 5H2O \rightarrow C6H12O6 + 12ADP + 12Pi + 12NADPH + 6H2O $$  

These stages work together to sustain plant life and energy flow in ecosystems.
