## 5. Culinary Highlights

- **Poke** – Fresh ahi or tuna, bright with yuzu sauce, served on a crispy tortilla.  
- **Lomi Salmon** – Salted salmon with diced tomatoes, onions, and a splash of Kona coffee.  
- **Shaved Ice** – Flavors of mango, guava, and local *kōlea* (coconut)*.  
- **Malasadas** – French‑style donuts, dusted with molasses and chia seeds.  

A quick culinary tip: visit a *hawaiian farmer’s market* (e.g., *Kailua Farmers Market*) for the freshest sea‑salted ingredients and unique local preserves.

## 6. Practical Tips for Your Hawaiian Adventure

- **Best Time to Visit**: Mid‑April to early June, and September to mid‑November, for comfortable weather and fewer crowds.  
- **Transportation**: Renting a car gives you freedom, but consider public transit for the *Island Express* or the *ANA Express* for visitors on a budget.  
- **Respect the Land**: Stick to marked trails, avoid touching *tahoe* (turtle) nests, and always follow the *Mālama ʻĀina* principles.  
- **Cash & Mobile**: Small stalls often accept only cash; bring a **NANO** (U.S. dollar) for transactions.  
- **Packing**: Layers are key—mornings can be cool, and the island’s humidity can be heavy. Bring a reusable water bottle—stay hydrated, and keep the ocean clean.
