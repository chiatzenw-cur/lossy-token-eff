#### How to Apply These Norms

1. **Prepare Ahead** – Know the company’s hierarchy, the meeting agenda, and any cultural nuances.  
2. **Observe and Adapt** – Watch how senior members behave; mirror their style.  
3. **Practice Your Greeting** – Say “Hajimemashite” (Nice to meet you) and bow correctly.  
4. **Never Leave a Card on the Table** – Place it immediately in a card holder or palm.  
5. **Keep Your Phone Quiet** – Ensure it’s off or on silent during meetings.  
6. **Respect Silence** – A brief pause is normal; it’s not a sign of disinterest.  

---

#### Quick Cultural Reminder

- **Faces**: “Saving face” is a core value. Avoid any direct confrontation or public criticism.  
- **Consensus**: Decisions often require “kaizen” (continuous improvement); they’re rarely made by a single individual.  
- **Time**: Punctuality is a non‑negotiable etiquette.  

---

**Putting It All Together**  
Go into a meeting with a bow, a neat business card, a respectful tone, and a patient mindset. Honesty and humility are highly valued, and a few thoughtful gestures can go a long way toward building trust and creating a solid partnership. Good luck!
