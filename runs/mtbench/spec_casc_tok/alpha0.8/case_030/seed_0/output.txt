#### How to Use These Principles Together

1. **Read the Argument Once** – Get the gist.  
2. **Apply the Five Principles** – Take a systematic approach:  
   - First, ensure every claim is **clear**.  
   - Second, test the **logic**.  
   - Third, check **relevance**.  
   - Fourth, scrutinize **evidence**.  
   - Fifth, evaluate **soundness** and consider **counter‑arguments**.  
3. **Write Your Evaluation** –  
   - State whether the argument is valid, sound, and compelling.  
   - Highlight strengths and weaknesses.  
   - Offer concrete suggestions for improvement (e.g., “clarify the definition of X” or “replace the anecdote with a peer‑reviewed study”).  

Applying these five principles systematically will help you produce a rigorous, persuasive, and intellectually honest analytical critique.
