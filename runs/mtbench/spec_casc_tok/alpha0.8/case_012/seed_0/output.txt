## 3. DAY‑BY‑DAY PLAN  

### **Day 1 – 45 min: “Setting the Stage”**  
| **Segment** | **Time** | **Activities** | **Key Drama Techniques** |
|-------------|----------|----------------|--------------------------|
| 1. Warm‑up: “Five‑Second History” | 5 min | Teacher reads a headline (e.g., “British Cargos…”). Students improvise a 5‑second response scene. | Improvisation, movement |
| 2. Mini‑lecture: “Three‑Act Breakdown” | 10 min | Teacher outlines 3 phases: (a) Background & Trade, (b) Conflict, (c) Aftermath. Shows 4‑min documentary clip. | Visual storytelling |
| 3. Role‑assignment & Research | 10 min | Students pair up; each randomly draws a role (e.g., “Qianlong Emperor”, “British Consul”, “Local Trader”, “Younger Opium Smuggler”). Quick research using 3‑minute “fact‑card” sheets. | Character mind‑mapping |
| 4. “Character Monologue” | 10 min | Each student writes & practices a 30‑second monologue (no dialogue). Focus on posture, gestures. | Mime, solo performance |
| 5. Closing & Reflection | 10 min | Students share their monologue aloud, peers note what they felt & what events they think are happening. Teacher writes a 1‑page “First‑Day Reflection” prompt. | Reflection, peer‑feedback |

**Homework**: Homework sheet: “Write the completed 5‑line script for a scene involving your character and another character (use stage directions).”

---

### **Day 2 – 45 min: “Rehearsal & Refinement”**  
| **Segment** | **Time** | **Activities** | **Key Drama Techniques** |
|-------------|----------|----------------|--------------------------|
| 1. Quick Review & “Brainstorm” | 5 min | Quick recap of the 3‑phase outline. Students write 3 “must‑include” points for their scene. | Brainstorming |
| 2. Group Formation | 5 min | Students regroup into 5‑person teams representing a short sub‑story (e.g., “The Smuggler’s Decision”). | Collaborative planning |
| 3. Script & Mime Workshop | 15 min | Teams script the 5‑line scene, add stage directions. Peer‑swaps receive constructive feedback. | Writing, mime |
| 4. Rehearsal & Stage Direction | 10 min | Teams rehearse in the action space. Teacher notes body language, blocking. | Blocking, timing |
| 5. “Mini‑Performance” | 5 min | Each group performs 30‑second scene to the class. Teacher collects quick live‑feedback. | Performance, peer‑assessment |
| 6. Reflection & Reflection Assignment | 5 min | Students write 1‑paragraph reflection on “What did we learn about the perspective of each character?” | Reflection writing |

## 5. DIFFERENTIATION & INCLUSIVE PRACTICES  

| **Challenge** | **Strategy** |
|---------------|--------------|
| **Shyness / Anxious Students** | Provide “script‑prompt” cards, allow them to rehearse in small groups before performing. |
| **Non‑English Speakers** | Pair with strong English peers; provide visual cue cards; encourage gestures. |
| **Students with Physical Disabilities** | Offer seated “storytelling” roles; provide sign‑language support. |
| **Students with Advanced Knowledge** | Assign research‑heavy roles (e.g., “Treaty writer”); provide primary source excerpts. |
| **Students with Creative Strengths** | Let them design a “mood board” or create a prop. |
