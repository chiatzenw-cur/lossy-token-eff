## 2. The 5‑Step Conflict‑Resolution Playbook

### Step 1: Clarify the Problem
- **Identify the core issue** (e.g., “I feel you’re not listening when I talk about my day.”).
- **Ask clarifying questions**: “What specifically made you feel that way?”  
- **Write it down**: A quick note (you can share it later) helps keep focus.

### Step 2: Express Your Feelings & Needs
- **“I” statement**: “I feel ignored when…”
- **State your need**: “I need 10 minutes of focused conversation.”

### Step 3: Let Your Partner Respond
- **No interrupting**: Let them finish.
- **Reflect back**: “So you’re saying you feel overwhelmed by that conversation? …”

### Step 4: Brainstorm Solutions Together
- **Use “What if?” prompts**: “What if we set a timer for 10 minutes? Or maybe we have a short “check‑in” after dinner?”
- **Generate at least 3 options**—good ideas come from collaboration.

### Step 5: Agree on a Concrete Plan
- **Pick one solution** that feels doable for both.
- **Set a start date** and a quick check‑in after a week.

## 3. Communication Techniques That Build Empathy

| Technique | How to Use It | Example |
|-----------|---------------|---------|
| **Reflective Listening** | Repeat back the other’s key point. | “So you’re saying you feel rushed when I talk about my work?” |
| **Paraphrasing** | Show you understand without making it a judgment. | “You’re saying you want a calm evening, not a discussion about why you’re disappointed.” |
| **Validation** | Acknowledge the other’s feelings—no matter how you see it. | “I hear that you’re frustrated; that’s totally understandable.” |
| **Non‑verbal cues** | Eye contact, nodding, leaning forward. | Keep your posture open; avoid crossing arms. |

## 4. When to Take a Break (Disengage & Re‑engage)

1. **Notice your cues**: heart racing, throat tight, hyper‑focus on the next argument.  
2. **Use a “cool‑down” phrase**: “Can we pause for 10 minutes and reconvene?”  
3. **Re‑engage**: In the same chair, same tone, and re‑introduce the frustration as a “topic,” not a personal attack.
