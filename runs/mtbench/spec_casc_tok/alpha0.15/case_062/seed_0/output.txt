- \(B\) = student likes **blue**  
- \(G\) = student likes **green**

We’re given  

\[
P(B)=0.58,\qquad P(G)=0.45,\qquad P(B\cap G)=0.22 .
\]

The probability a student likes **at least one** of the colors is the union of the two events:

\[
P(B\cup G)=P(B)+P(G)-P(B\cap G)=0.58+0.45-0.22=0.81 .
\]

The probability that a student likes **neither** blue nor green is the complement of this union:

\[
P(\text{neither}) = 1 - P(B\cup G)=1-0.81=0.19 .
\]

\[
\boxed{0.19\text{ or }19\%}
