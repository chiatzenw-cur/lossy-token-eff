| Solution | How It Works | Implementation |
|----------|--------------|----------------|
| **Base Isolation Bearings** | Reduce seismic force transmitted to the superstructure. | • Install at pier bases and main‑span deck. Provide a clear buffering distance (≈ 20 cm). |
