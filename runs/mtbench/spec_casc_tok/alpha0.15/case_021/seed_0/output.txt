\[
|x+5|<10

\[
-10 < x+5 < 10.

Subtract 5 from all parts:

\[
-15 < x < 5.
\]

So \(x\) must be an integer strictly between \(-15\) and \(5\).

The integers in that open interval are  

\[
-14,\,-13,\,-12,\dotsc,\,0,\,1,\,2,\,3,\,4.

- The smallest integer is \(-14\).
- The largest integer is \(4\).

Number of integers = \(4 - (-14) + 1 = 19\).
