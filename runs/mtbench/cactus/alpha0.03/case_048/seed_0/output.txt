1. **“Waterway of Europe”** – From Switzerland to the North Sea, the Rhine is one of the continent’s most important inland waterways.  
   *Result:* Dominant transport corridor for goods, trade and import of building materials → supports a strong industrial network that makes lands along the river banks productive and densely populated.
