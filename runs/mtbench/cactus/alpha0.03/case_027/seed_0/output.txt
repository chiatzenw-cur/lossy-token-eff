### Quick mental diagram

```
Thylakoid membranes:
   Light photons → Photo‑electrons → PSI – PSII --> ATP (via chemiosmosis) + NADPH + O₂
Stroma (outside the phospholipid bilayer):
   O₂ produced in thylakoids travels freely; Calvin cycle self‑conditions
   CO₂ + ATP + NADPH → H₂O (return to water) + sugars
```

---

**Key take‑aways**

- **Light‑dependent stage** (in the thylakoid membrane) converts solar energy into **ATP** and **NADPH**, while **splitting water** returns electrons and releases **oxygen**.
- **Calvin cycle** (in the stroma) consumes **CO₂**, **ATP** and **NADPH** to build **permanent carbon compounds** (e.g., sugars, starch), providing the energy sources that support all other life on Earth.
