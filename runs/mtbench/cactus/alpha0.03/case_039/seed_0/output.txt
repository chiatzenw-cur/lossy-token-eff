- \(x\) = number of people who got on the bus at the terminal.  
- After any stop we’ll keep track of how many people are still on the bus.  

---

### 1.  First stop  
- **Half** of the \(x\) people get off.  
  \[
  \text{Left} = x - \frac{x}{2} = \frac{x}{2}
  \]
- **4** more people get on.  
  \[
  P_1 = \frac{x}{2} + 4
  \]

### 2.  Second stop  
- **6** people get off.  
  \[
  P_1 - 6 = \frac{x}{2} + 4 - 6 = \frac{x}{2} - 2
  \]
- **8** more people board.  
  \[
  P_2 = \left(\frac{x}{2} - 2\right) + 8 = \frac{x}{2} + 6
  \]

### 3.  Condition that today’s passengers are heading to the third stop  
After the second stop the bus is headed to the third stop.  
Hence the number of people on the bus at that point is given to be **25**:

\[
P_2 = \frac{x}{2} + 6 = 25 \quad\Longrightarrow\quad \frac{x}{2} = 19
\]

Multiplying by 2 gives

\[
x = 38
\]

---

**Answer:** **38 people** collected at the terminal.
