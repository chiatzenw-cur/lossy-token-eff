- \(B\) = set of students who like blue  
- \(G\) = set of students who like green  
- \(P(B\cap G)=0.22\) (both colors)

The probability that a randomly selected student likes either blue or green (i.e. the union) is given by

\[
P(B\cup G)=P(B)+P(G)-P(B\cap G).
\]

Plugging in the numbers:

\[
P(B\cup G)=0.58+0.45-0.22 = 0.81.
\]

Hence the probability that a student likes neither blue nor green is the complement of \(B\cup G\):

\[
P(\text{neither blue nor green}) = 1- P(B\cup G)=1-0.81 = 0.19.
\]
