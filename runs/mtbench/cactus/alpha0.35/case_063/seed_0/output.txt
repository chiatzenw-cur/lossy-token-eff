\[
S=x+y=4z,\qquad 
P=xy=4z^2 .
\]

The quadratic whose roots are \(x\) and \(y\) is  

\[
t^2-ST+P\,t=0\;\;\Longrightarrow\;\;t^2-tS+P=0 .
\]

Its discriminant is  

\[
\Delta = S^2-4P = (4z)^2-4(4z^2)=16z^2-16z^2=0 .
\]

Because \(\Delta=0\), the two roots coincide:

\[
x=y = \frac{S}{2}= \frac{4z}{2}=2z .
\]

Hence the two numbers are equal and consequently

\[
x-y = 0.
\]

So *the value of \(x-y\) is expressed in terms of \(z\) as \(0\).*
