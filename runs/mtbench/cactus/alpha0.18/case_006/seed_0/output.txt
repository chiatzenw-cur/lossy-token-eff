Let's produce final.<|end|><|start|>assistant<|channel|>final<|message|>For two standard six‑sided dice the outcomes are ordered pairs \((d_1,d_2)\) with \(d_1,d_2\in\{1,2,3,4,5,6\}\).  
There are \(6\times 6 = 36\) equally likely ordered pairs.

The only way to get a total **less** than 3 is to roll a total of **2**.  
A total of 2 is possible only in one way: \((1,1)\).

Hence the probability of rolling a total **at least 3** is

\[
P(\text{sum}\ge 3) = 1 - P(\text{sum}=2)
               = 1 - \frac{1}{36}
               = \frac{35}{36}
                \approx 0.9722.
\]

So,
\[
\boxed{\displaystyle \frac{35}{36}}
\]
