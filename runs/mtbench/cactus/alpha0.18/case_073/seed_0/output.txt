**Who named this?**

- **Francis Crick** (Professor of Biology at the University of Cambridge, later as a pioneer in molecular biology at MIT).  
- He wrote about the **central dogma** in his 1958 trans‑Atlantic paper “On protein synthesis, the virally heme and the chain of conjectures.”  
- The *dogma* was formally coined by Crick in his 1958 paper *“On protein synthesis versus the idea that genetic information can be transferred between the two strands”* and later used as a shorthand for the genetic informational flow in modern texts. It still forms the major conceptual framework of the discipline.
