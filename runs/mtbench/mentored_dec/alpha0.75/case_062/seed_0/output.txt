Using the rule for addition of two probabilities:

\[
P(\text{blue or green}) = P(\text{blue}) + P(\text{green})
                         - P(\text{both blue and green})
\]

Plugging in the percentages:

\[
P(\text{blue or green}) = 0.58 + 0.45 - 0.22 = 0.81
\]

So 81 % of the students like at least one of the two colors.

---

**Step 2 – Probability of liking neither color**

If 81 % like at least one color, the remainder (100 % – 81 %) must like neither:

\[
P(\text{neither}) = 1 - 0.81 = 0.19.
\]

---

**Answer**

The probability that a randomly chosen student likes **neither** blue nor green is **0.19**, i.e. **19 %**.
