## Design Considerations

1. **Solar Resource & Climate**  
   * Map average insolation, seasonal variation, peak sun hours.  
   * Account for hail‑shield or bird deterrent on high‑gain surfaces.

2. **Household Hot‑Water Demand**  
   * Calculate demand (L/day) based on occupants, showers, dish‑washing, laundry.  
   * Peak usage periods—identify the maximum continuous draw.

3. **Roof & Tilt**  
   * Confirm roof location, pitch, azimuth, and structural load capacity.  
   * Evaluate seasonal shading (trees, buildings, HVAC units).

4. **Codes & Regulations**  
   * Local building codes, local energy‐efficiency standards, electric/escalation permits.  
   * Insulation standards for tanks (e.g., EN 15555, WEL).  

5. **Financial & Incentive Analysis**  
   * Capital cost, operation & maintenance cost, payback period.  
   * Green incentives, tax credits, rebates, and feed‑in tariff (if applicable).  

6. **Maintenance & Longevity**  
   * Easy access to collecter, pump, and hot‐water tank.  
   * Inspection schedules for seals, hoses, flanges.  

7. **Safety & Redundancy**  
   * Burner protection, pressure‑relief, venting for gas or electric backup.  
   * Manual shut‑off to isolate the system during winter.
