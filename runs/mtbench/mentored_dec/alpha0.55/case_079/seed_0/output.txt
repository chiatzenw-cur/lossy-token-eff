### Quick‑Start Summary

1. **Arrive early** – being on time is a sign of respect.  
2. **Bowl first, shake second** – if a handshake is offered, use both hands.  
3. **Examine the card first** – look at the card before taking it away.  
4. **Use polite and hon‐den ōshō** – speak respectfully, avoid expletives.  
5. **Follow the senior’s lead** – prefer the senior’s decisions in meeting or “kaigi.”  
6. **Accept your first cup of tea** – it’s an acknowledgement of respect.  
7. **Iconic gift** – a small, thoughtful gift, not an extravagant, “bribery” gift.  
8. **Keep email formal** – begin with *oshiage* and close with *keigu*.  

These norms are not only rules but *cultural cues* that demonstrate you respect Japan’s strong work ethic, its deep care of hierarchy, and a culture that values polite, unitized, measurable behavior.  The more respectful you are, the faster trust builds – eventually translating into business success.
