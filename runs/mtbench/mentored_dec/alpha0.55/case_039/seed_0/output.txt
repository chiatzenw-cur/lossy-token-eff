Let initial number of people on bus, x. At first stop: half of people got down. So people left = x/2. Then 4 more people got in, so new count = x/2 + 4. At second stop: 6 people got down. So leave 6. We are told that there were a total of 25 people heading to the third stop. So x/2 + 6 = 25 => x/2 = 19 => x = 38.

- \(x\) = number of people who got on the bus at the terminal.  

After each stop we update the number on the bus.

**First stop**

- Half of the passengers get down: \(x/2\) leave.  
- \(4\) new passengers get on:  

\[
\text{People remaining after first stop}= \frac{x}{2} + 4.
\]

**Second stop**

- \(6\) people get down:  

\[
\frac{x}{2} + 4 - 6 = \frac{x}{2} - 2.
\]

- \(8\) more get on:  

\[
\text{People remaining after second stop}= \frac{x}{2} - 2 + 8 = \frac{x}{2} + 6.
\]

This number is the “total of 25 people heading to the third stop,” so

\[
\frac{x}{2} + 6 = 25 \quad\Longrightarrow\quad \frac{x}{2} = 19 \quad\Longrightarrow\quad x = 38.
\]
