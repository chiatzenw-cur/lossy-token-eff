**Explanation**

When two fair six‑sided dice are rolled, there are  
\(6 \times 6 = 36\) equally‑likely ordered outcomes.

- The only way to get a total **less than 3** is to sum to 2.
- The sum of 2 occurs only when both dice show 1 (the \(1\!-\!1\) pair).

Thus, the number of favorable outcomes for “sum ≥ 3” is  

\[
36 - 1 = 35.
\]

So the probability is

\[
\frac{\text{favorable outcomes}}{\text{total outcomes}}
   = \frac{35}{36}
   \approx 0.9722 .
\]

In other words, there is a 97.2 % chance that two dice will total at least 3.
