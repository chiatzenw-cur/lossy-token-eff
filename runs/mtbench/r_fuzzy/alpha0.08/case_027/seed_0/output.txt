| Feature | Details |
|---|---|
| **Where in the chloroplast** | **Thylakoid membranes** (the network of stacked discs inside the chloroplast). Light‑absorbing pigments (chlorophyll a, b, carotenoids) are embedded within these membranes. |
| **Primary inputs** | • Solar photons<br>• Water (H₂O) as the electron donor<br>• Carbon‑less cofactors (ATP & NADP⁺ that are “used” but not permanently consumed) |
| **Key events** | • Photon energy excites electrons in PSII → electrons jump to the electron transport chain (ETC).<br>• Water is split (photolysis) to replace those electrons, producing O₂ and H⁺.<br>• Electrons move through the ETC, pumping protons into the thylakoid lumen, creating a proton motive force.<br>• Protons flow back through ATP synthase, forming ATP (photophosphorylation).<br>• Reduced electrons finally reduce NADP⁺ to NADPH via ferredoxin (FNR). |
| **Primary outputs** | • ATP (energy currency)<br>• NADPH (reduced carrier)<br>• O₂ (by‑product released into the atmosphere)<br>• Protons (H⁺) in the lumen (used for ATP synthesis). |

**Calvin‑Benson (Light‑Independent) Cycle – “Day‑reaction”**

| Feature | Details |
|---|---|
| **Where in the chloroplast** | **Stroma** (the fluid surrounding the thylakoid sacs) and the enzyme complexes that sit in the thylakoid interior. |
| **Primary inputs** | • CO₂ from the atmosphere (taken in through stomata)<br>• ATP produced in the light reactions<br>• NADPH produced in the light reactions |
| **Key events** | • CO₂ is fixed by Rubisco into 3‑phosphoglycerate (3‑PGA).<br>• ATP and NADPH provide the energy and reducing power to convert 3‑PGA into glyceraldehyde‑3‑phosphate (G3P).<br>• Some G3P leaves (or leafs) for sucrose, starch, and other carbohydrates; the rest rewrites the cycle. |
| **Primary outputs** | • G3P (3‑carbon sugar) – the immediate product that can be polymerized into glucose, starch, sucrose, cellulose, lignin, etc.<br>• Regenerated ribulose‑bisphosphate (RuBP), so the cycle can continue. |

### Quick picture

```
            Sunlight
               |
          Thylakoid Membranes
          (PSII → Cyt b6f → PSI → ETC)
               |
          ATP  +  NADPH + O₂  (light reactions)
               ↓
          Stroma
          Calvin‑Benson Cycle
               |
          G3P → Glucose / Starch / Sucrose
```

In short:

* **Stage 1 – Light‑dependent reactions;** take place in the thylakoid membrane; use light, water, NADP⁺ to produce ATP, NADPH, and CH₂ O₂.  
* **Stage 2 – Calvin cycle;** takes place in the stroma; uses CO₂, CO₂‑converted substrates, ATP, NADPH to produce G3P and, ultimately, carbohydrates.
