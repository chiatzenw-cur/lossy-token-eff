### How to deduce the most likely cause

1. **Ask about a *home* or *hospital* environment** – If Thomas sees people or equipment that suggests a workplace or a patient‐care routine, that points toward a job or a chronic treatment.  
2. **Look at the frequency** – Daily visits is more common for dialysis, chemotherapy, or daily drug infusions than for routine disease monitoring.  
3. **Probe for “non‑health” reasons** – If you discover he works for a hospital as a medical technician, nurse, or paramedic, the visits are occupational.  
4. **Ask about implanted devices** – Implantable devices often need daily checks or daily battery replacements.
