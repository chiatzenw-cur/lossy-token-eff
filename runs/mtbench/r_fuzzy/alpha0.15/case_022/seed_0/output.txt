### Things to keep in mind

| Issue | Explanation |
|------|-------------|
| **Performance** | The naïve recursive algorithm has exponential growth (`O(2^n)`), so it becomes impractical for `n > ~40` on typical hardware. |

| **Stack depth** | The recursion depth is linear in `n`. For very large `n` (> 10,000) you can hit the call stack limit. |
