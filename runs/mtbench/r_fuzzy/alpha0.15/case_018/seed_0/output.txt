The usual trick with this kind of hinting question is that the “healthy” Thomas is healthy *in the way he’s been designed to be*, but he has a very specific, routine need that forces him to be a “patient” each day.  

The most common textbook answer (and one that keeps the mystery tight) is:  

> **He has an implanted device that he must have checked (or worked) on daily** – think pacemaker, implantable cardiac monitor, insulin pump, or even a vascular plug that needs daily imaging.  
> **OR**  
> **He is a person in a clinical trial because he is healthy but is being used to test something (e.g., a patient‑oriented study).**  

In short, he’s healthy but a device/clinical arrangement calls him in every day.
