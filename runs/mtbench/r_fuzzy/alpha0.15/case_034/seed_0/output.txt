## 4. Who Should Seek Immediate Referral

- Persistent fever > 38 °C or chills  
- Shift‑changing abdominal pain, rigidity, rebound tenderness  
- Blood or grossly bloody stool  
- Signs of dehydration or shock (tachycardia)  
- Failure to reduce pain with first‑line meds  
- Pregnancy in a young woman (check for ectopic)*
