> **“Genetic information flows in one direction: the sequence of DNA is copied into messenger RNA (mRNA), and the sequence of mRNA is translated into a polypeptide chain (protein).”**  
> (Crick, 1958 – the idea was first articulated by Crick as “the natural sciences with the exception of changing the way the data are stored” – a modification of a single phrase, which one of the first “scientific laws” used by Go.)

### 1. Core Processes that Illustrate the Dogma

| Step | Key players | What happens | Key enzymes / complexes |
|------|-------------|--------------|------------------------|
| **DNA replication** | DNA polymerase + helicase | DNA copies itself → two identical DNA strands | DNA polymerase II, III, etc. |
| **Transcription** | RNA polymerase, transcription factors (DNA‑binding proteins) | DNA → pre‑mRNA (or other RNA species) | RNA polymerase I, II, III, NTPs |
| **RNA processing** | Spliceosome, poly‑A polymerase, cationyl‑methyltransferases | Pre‑mRNA contains introns → mature mRNA (cleons, poly‑A tail) | – |
| **Translation** | Ribosomes, tRNA, aminoacyl‑tRNA synthetases | mRNA → polypeptide chain → folded protein | Ribosome, EF‑TuB, enzyme complex |
| **Post‑translational modifications** | Kinases, proteases, ubiquitin‑ligases | Protein + modifications | kinases, phosphatases, etc. |

> **Additional processes (non‑canonical) that prove the dogma is *a* conceptual basis, not a hard rule**  
> • **Reverse transcription** – enzymes like *reverse transcriptase* convert RNA → protein.  
> • **RNA editing** – de‑amination of adenine in certain RNAs (e.g., ADAR).  
> • **Protein‑to‑RNA** reverse chiral cycles (rare but non‑canonical).

### 2. Who Named It?

**Francis Crick** (1926‑2004) coined the phrase **“central dogma”** in 1958.  
- While the antecedent “artificial strategy” for the central dogma was proposed by others (e.g., Watson & Crick for DNA, and the notion that “the flow of information to the protein” was one‑way process), Crick formally introduced the concept.

> *Crick’s 1958 essay* (Crick, 1958) stated that **“information flows from genetic material to many proteins.”**  
> The phrase, though short, became a foundational concept in molecular biology.

### 3. How Does the Dogma Guide Experiments?

| Classic Experiment | Motor | Insight |

### 4. Bottom Line & Key Take‑Away

1. **Bird‑keep** – the dogma is not a chemical law; it’s a set of key, distinct processes that describe the *direction* of information flow from DNA to RNA to proteins.  
2. “DNA → RNA → Protein” is the product of **DNA replication**, **transcription**, and **translation** (plus processing).  
3. “Central Dogma” is **E. I. 1958** – coined by **Francis Crick**.
