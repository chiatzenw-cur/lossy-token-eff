**Two‑stage process overview**  
*(Simplified to remember that “light” is the first step, “dark” is the second.)*  

1. **Light‑dependent reactions**  
   * **Site**: Thylakoid membranes.  
   * **Key events**: Photosystems II and I absorb photons → electrons move along optical chain → ATP maker (ATP synthase) and NADPH are produced.  
   * **Inputs**: Light & water.  
   * **Outputs**: Water is cleaved, giving O₂; NADPH oxidized (`NADP⁺` ↔ `NADPH`) and ATP synthetized.  

2. **Calvin cycle/Light‑independent reactions**  
   * **Site**: Stroma (initially macromolecular enzyme pool).  
   * **Key events**: Rubisco enzyme carboxylates RuBP → C₃ sugars → regeneration.  
   * **Inputs**: CO₂ (from the atmosphere), CO₂-binding enzyme (RuBP) and the energy carriers ATP and NADPH produced in stage 1.  
   * **Outputs**: G3P → molecules that can be polymerised into glucose, starch, cellulose, etc. It may be considered “carbohydrate” output for the plant.  

*(Additionally, the “dark” portion of the cycle consumes the ATP and NADPH of the light‑dependent stage to drive a series of redox reactions, essentially “re‑using” the energy stored during the light steps.)*

### Short Summary

| Stage | Where | Inputs → Outputs |
|-------|-------|------------------|
| Light‑dependent | Thylakoid membranes | Light + Water → ATP & NADPH + O₂ |
| Light‑independent | Stroma | CO₂ + ATP + NADPH → ADP/ATP + G3P (→ glucose and other starches) |

The two stages together are often referred to as the **feather‑ed “two‑stage” system**, the first producing the necessary energy carriers, the second using those carriers to build the plant’s food stores.
