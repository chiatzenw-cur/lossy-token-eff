## 1. Key Components & Design Considerations

| Category | Component | Why it matters | Typical Specs |
|----------|-----------|----------------|---------------|
| **Solar Collector** | Flat‑plate (glass, aluminum) or Evacuated‑tube (high‑temp) | Converts solar flux to heat; choice of type determines efficiency, cost, and suitability for local climate. | 1 – 3 km² panels; 1 m² collector ≈ 0.2 kWth peak |
| **Storage** | Two‑tank (active‑cold or passive) or single‑tank with positive‑pressure | Holds heat for use during nights, cloudy days, or low‑solar demand. | 200–300 L (30–50 °C ΔT) + 350–500 L (hot | ~ 120–200 °C) |
| **Heat Exchange** | Direct‑flow or indirect‑water‑to‑water (CIP) | Transfer heat from collector to potable water; affects losses and stability. | 1‑m² surface area; ΔT ≈ 12–18 °C |
| **Pumping & Controls** | Solar‑powered pumps + PLC (or microcontroller) | Move water through collector and tank; controls start/stop based on temperature, temperature differential, etc. | 10‑50 L x min⁻², 12 V DC or 48 V DC |
| **Backup / Grid Integration** | Conventional electric motor‑heated tank; optional electric/thermal storage | Supplies unmet demand; safety cut‑over and heat‑up during cloudy days. | 10 kW electric heat |
| **Maintenance & Safety** | Shut‑off valves, pressure relief, monitoring probes | Protects occupants, meets local codes, ensures longevity. | 0.5 bar relief; 0.5 L/min bleed valve |
| **Instrumentation** | Temperature probes, flow meters, UV fast indicator, LCD display | Enables real‑time monitoring, data logging for performance. | 1 kW power consumption |

## 2. Five‑Step Workflow for System Design

Below is a concise, end‑to‑end workflow, with each phase spanned by key decisions and deliverables.

| Step | Objective | Key Tasks | Deliverables |

Do a simple “Sun & Shade” scan of roof, walls, or ground. | • Heat‑load curve (kW/d). <br>• Wind/temperature maps. <br>• Initial solar potential. |
| **Step 2 – System Concept & Collector Selection** | Choose collector type, orientation, and sizing. | 1. Compare flat‑plate vs. evacuated‑tube vs. thermosyphon. <br>2. | **Step 3 – Storage & Pumping Strategy** | Design tank(s) and circulation path. | 1. Decide between active + cold‑tank or passive / no‑tank. <br>2. Interface: direct‑flow vs indirect, UV black‑body maintain. | • 2‑tank schematics. <br>• Pump curve, electrical draw. <br>• Filter & valve layout. |
