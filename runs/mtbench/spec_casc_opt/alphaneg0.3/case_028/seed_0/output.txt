### Important Design Considerations

1. **Solar Resource Assessment**  
   * Solar insolation data (average kWh/m²/day).  
   * Seasonal variation (summer vs. winter).  
   * Local shading analysis (roof, trees, adjacent buildings).

2. **Orientation & Tilt**  
   * Optimal azimuth (~0° for true south in the northern hemisphere).  
   * Tilt equal to local latitude.

3. **Demand Profile of the Residential Building**  
   * Time‑of‑use patterns (morning, evening, weekends).  
   * Peak daily demand (cold showers, laundry).  
   * Historical or projected water‑use curves.

4. **Efficiency & Losses**  
   * Counter‑flow vs. parallel collector designs.  
   * Minimize thermal losses by insulation, proper piping routing.  
   * Use potential to preheat cold water with solar‑heated water or ambient air.

5. **Size & Storage Volume**  
   * Balance between energy capture and storage cost.  
   * Avoid over‑loading the collector array (too many panels with insufficient demand).

6. **Backup Compatibility**  
   * System must remain safe and compliant when backup backup is active (e.g., gas or electric).  
   * Avoid interference between solar and backup heaters.

7. **Compliance & Safety**  
   * Local building codes, heating/cooling regulations.  
   * ISO 9927 (solar thermal) or ISO 4837 compliance.  
   * Installation drawings, permits, required inspector approvals.

8. **Durability & Warranty**  
   * Expectated lifespan of collectors (20–30 yr).  
   * Warranty terms for pumps, tanks, controls.  
   * Maintenance schedule for filtration, panel cleaning, insulation.

9. **Economics & Payback**  
   * Cost per watt for collectors, pumping cost, backup heater cost.  
   * Payback period, net present value (NPV) analysis, incentives.  
   * Insurance or ownership interest prevention.

10. **Environmental & Sustainability Factors**  
    * Use of recycled or low‑impact materials (e.g., recycled steel).  
    * Life‑cycle assessment if needed.

### Additional Considerations

* **Software & Data** – Use a simple design tool (`SolarCalc`, `CSFD`, or a custom spreadsheet) for sizing; post‑install, an IoT‑enabled dashboard (Grafana, etc.) for real‑time performance.

* **Continuous Improvement** – After 6–12 months, analyze the data to refine setpoints or add a small boost in collection area if demand gaps remain.
