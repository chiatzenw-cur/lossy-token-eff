### Key Take‑away

| Aspect | What Happens | How It Shapes Time & Mortality |

### Key Take‑away

| **Influence** | Deconstruction of the *“safe”* hours of childhood into *financial stability* + *relationship building* + *spiritual / personal growth*. |

### Key Take‑away  
As adult responsibilities compound, *time becomes a quantifiable asset*; *mortality becomes a discounted factor*—a future event gently offset by the present.

| Aspect | What Happens | How It Shapes Time & Mortality |

| **Influence** | Cultural rituals (e.g., ancestral rites, memorials) become life’s final teacher of how we measure *chronos* and *kairos* (opportune moments). |

| Culture | Time View | Mortality View |
|---------|-----------|----------------|
| **East Asian (Japan, China)** | *Kairo* (time as a continuous pipeline). | *Kara Jōyō* (impermanence). |
| **Western (USA, Europe)** | *Chrono-centric* (timeline). | *Death as a separateness*; many rituals emphasize sanitizing. |
| **Indigenous (Native American, Aboriginal)** | *Tribal cycles* (feast, war, seasons). | *Living/Grieving relationship* with ancestors. |

**How this matters**  
Cultural context shapes the *archetype*—our default mental models for how we interpret time and mortality. So while development is universal, its *paradigmatic filters* differ.
