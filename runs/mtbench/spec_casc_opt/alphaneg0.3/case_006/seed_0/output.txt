The only way to get a total **less than 3** is to roll a total of **2**.

- Rolling a total of 2 can happen only when both dice show a 1.  
  There is exactly **1** such outcome out of the **36** possible outcomes.

Hence the chance of not getting a total of 2 (i.e., getting a total of at least 3) is

\[
P(\text{sum}\ge 3) = 1 - P(\text{sum}=2) = 1 - \frac{1}{36} = \frac{35}{36}\approx 0.9722.
\]

So the probability that two dice sum to at least 3 is \(\boxed{\frac{35}{36}}\).
