**Key Considerations**

1. **Weather & Solar Resource**  
   - Most beam‑directed sun: 5–10 kWh/m²/day in mid‑latitude.  
   - Use pyranometer or historical local data to size inlet/outlet.  

2. **Roof Selections & Orientation**  
   - South‑facing south‑facing with minimal inclination to get ~90 % optimal; tilt = latitude ± 10°.  
   - Sunny roof, shade, minimal debris.  

3. **Load Profile**  
   - Estimate domestic hot water demand (peak per day typical 120‑160 L).  
   - Calculate theoretical energy demand, but include 25 % reserve for winter or cloudy days.  

4. **Local Codes & Regulations**  
   - Plumbing, fire, electrical reaction standards.  
   - Certification of collectors (e.g., EPA's ENERGY STAR) vs. local exemptions.  

5. **Cost & ROI**  
   - Include consumer rebates, utility incentives.  
   - Estimate payback period 4–7 years.  

6. **Maintenance & Longevity**  
   - Keep an easy‑access inspection cross‑section.  
   - Select materials with low corrosion risk (Algal, silicon, etc.).  

7. **Safety Features**  
   - Pressure relief and temperature cut‑off at 75 °C in U.S. standards.  
   - Electric control heating; 24‑V for collector, 120 V for backup.  

8. **Integration & User Experience**  
   - Display panel showing consumption and hot‑water reservoir temperature.  
   - Keep the system in a homeowner‑friendly zone.
