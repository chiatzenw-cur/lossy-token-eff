**Why?**

Let  

- \(B\) = “student likes blue”  
- \(G\) = “student likes green”

Given:

- \(P(B)=58\% = 0.58\)
- \(P(G)=45\% = 0.45\)
- \(P(B\cap G)=0.22\)

**Step 1 – Find the probability of liking at least one of the colors.**  
Using the principle of inclusion–exclusion:

\[
P(B\cup G)=P(B)+P(G)-P(B\cap G)
            =0.58+0.45-0.22
            =0.81
\]

So 81 % of the students like at least one of the colors.

**Step 2 – Find the probability of liking neither.**  
The complement event gives the probability of wanting neither:

\[
P(\text{neither}) = 1 - P(B\cup G) = 1 - 0.81 = 0.19 .
\]

So, **19 %** of the students would like neither blue nor green.
