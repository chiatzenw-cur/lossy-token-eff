### Quick “cheat sheet” for easy reference

- **Light‑dependent reactions (light‑dependent)**
  - **Location:** Thylakoid membranes
  - **Key components:** PSII, PSI, cytochrome‑b₆f, ATP synthase, ATP synthase.
  - **Inputs:** Light, water, NADP⁺.
  - **Outputs:** ATP, NADPH, O₂.

- **Light‑independent reactions (Calvin cycle)**
  - **Location:** Stroma (the fluid inside the chloroplast)
  - **Key component:** Rubisco (cyanide‑act)
  - **Inputs:** CO₂, ATP, NADPH (generated by the light‑dependent reactions).
  - **Outputs:** G3P (which can be converted into glucose, sucrose, starch, etc.), CO₂ (regenerated) and ATP and NADPH (re‑used so the cycle is sustainable).

---

**Why the two steps matter**

1. **Light‑dependent reactions** convert light energy into chemical energy (ATP + NADPH).  
2. **Light‑independent reactions** take that energy to fix atmospheric CO₂ into sugars that can be stored and later carried out by organisms.

Together, they make a closed system that powers life while simultaneously releasing CO₂ in the atmosphere.
