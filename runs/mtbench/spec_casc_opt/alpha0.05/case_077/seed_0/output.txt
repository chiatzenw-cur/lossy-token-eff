### Example:  “Rule of Law” vs. “Market‑Yield”  
- **US**: The legal doctrine is “consumer protection” – the court's key question is whether the merger or conduct *injuriously lower price or *reduce competition*.  
- **China**: The logic is “market‑share” – a firm becomes a fraught monopoly if its product > 50 % and behaves “unfairly.” The Principled solutions are **indirect**: they may order a company to shift its sales, restructure, route or withdraw from the market.
