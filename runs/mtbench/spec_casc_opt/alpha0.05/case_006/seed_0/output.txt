The next possible total is \(3\).

So the only way a roll **fails** to be at least 3 is if the two dice sum to 2.  
A sum of 2 occurs only when both dice show 1, which is only one outcome out of the six‑sided die’s \(6\times6=36\) equally likely outcomes.

\[
P(\text{sum}\ge2)=\frac{36-1}{36}=\frac{35}{36}\approx 0.9722.
\]
