The probability of rolling a 2 is

\[
P(\text{sum}=2)=\frac{1}{6}\times\frac{1}{6}=\frac{1}{36}.
\]

Hence the probability of rolling a sum **at least 3** is the complement of that:

\[
P(\text{sum}\ge 3)=1-\frac{1}{36}=\frac{35}{36}\approx 0.9722.
\]
