- \(x\) = number of people who got on at the terminal.  

**First stop**

- Half of the people on the bus get off: \( \frac{x}{2}\) get off.  
- 4 more people get in.  

So after the first stop the bus has  

\[
x-\frac{x}{2}+4 = \frac{x}{2}+4
\]

**Second stop**

- 6 people get off: \(\frac{x}{2}+4-6= \frac{x}{2}-2\).  
- 8 more people get in: \(\frac{x}{2}-2+8= \frac{x}{2}+6\).

Thus, just before the third stop the bus carries  

\[
\frac{x}{2}+6 \text{ people}.
\]

We are told that there are 25 people heading to the third stop, i.e., the bus has 25 people just before the third stop:

\[
\frac{x}{2}+6 = 25.
\]

Solve for \(x\):

\[
\frac{x}{2} = 25-6 = 19 \quad\Longrightarrow\quad x = 2 \times 19 = 38.
\]

\[
\boxed{38}
\]
