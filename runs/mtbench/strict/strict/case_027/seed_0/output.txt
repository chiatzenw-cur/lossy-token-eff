**Why it matters**

* Light reactions convert solar energy into chemical energy (ATP + NADPH) and release O₂ that fuels aerobic respiration of nearly all life on Earth.
* The Calvin cycle uses that energy to fix CO₂ into carbohydrates—essential for plant growth, food for herbivores, and the source of most animal food chains.
