### Review of the Correct Context

The **long-term temperature goal** of the Paris Agreement is outlined in **Article 2, paragraph 1**, which states:

> "This Agreement, in enhancing the implementation of the Convention, including its objective, aims to strengthen the global response to the threat of climate change, in the context of sustainable development and efforts to eradicate poverty, including by:  
> (a) Holding the increase in the global average temperature to well below 2 °C above pre-industrial levels and pursuing efforts to limit the temperature increase to 1.5 °C above pre-industrial levels..."

Thus, the goal is explicitly **to limit global temperature rise to well below 2 °C**, and **to pursue actions to limit it to 1.5 °C**, while increasing **adaptation capacity** and promoting **low greenhouse gas emissions development**.
