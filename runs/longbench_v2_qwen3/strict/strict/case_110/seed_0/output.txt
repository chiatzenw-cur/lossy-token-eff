### Question Review and Key Focus

The question asks which description **is not accurate** regarding the **efforts parties plan to make** in order to **achieve the long-term temperature goal** of the Paris Agreement.

To determine this, we must closely examine each option against the **official provisions** and **references** in the **Paris Agreement**, such as **Article 2**, **Article 4**, and **Decision 1/CP.21**. The long-term temperature goal is specified in **Article 2**, which calls for "holding the increase in global average temperature to well below 2°C above pre-industrial levels" and "pursuing efforts to limit the temperature increase to 1.5°C" as a key twin objective.

### Analysis of the Options

#### **Option A**  
> *"Each Party shall prepare and communicate nationally determined contribution every five years and account for anthropogenic emissions and removals corresponding to their nationally determined contributions."*

- This is **accurately described** in **Article 4**, which mandates that all parties **prepare**, **communicate**, and **maintain** NDCs every five years and **account** for emissions.  
- ✅ **Correct** and **accurate** description.

---

#### **Option B**  
> *"All Parties shall provide the information necessary for clarity, transparency and understanding in accordance with decision 1/CP.21 and any relevant decisions of the Conference of the Parties to the United Nations Framework Convention on Climate Change."*

- While **decision 1/CP.21** does indeed concern the **adoption of the Paris Agreement**, the actual **Article 4, paragraph 8** of the Agreement requires parties to provide information in accordance with **decision 1/CP.21** and any **relevant decisions of the Conference of the Parties serving as the meeting of the Parties to this Agreement** (i.e., the COP under the Paris Agreement).
- The key issue is that **Option B refers to "the Conference of the Parties to the United Nations Framework Convention on Climate Change"** — this is **not precise** and **does not align with the text** of the Agreement.
- The COP **serving as the meeting of the Parties to the Paris Agreement** is **distinct** and **specific**, not the original COP under the Convention.
- ❌ **Inaccurate** because it **misidentifies** the reference to the Conference of the Parties. The COP serving as meeting of the Parties to the Paris Agreement is not the same as the COP under the Convention, even though they are technically the same body, the **phrasing is not accurate**.

---

#### **Option C**  
> *"Developed countries should pursue domestic mitigation measures, take the lead by undertaking economy-wide absolute emission reduction targets and provide transparent and consistent information on support for developing country Parties."*

- This is in line with **Article 4**, which places **developed countries in a leadership role** (economy-wide absolute reduction targets) and mandates **transparent support** for developing countries (e.g., **Article 9** and **Article 4, paragraph 9**).  
- ✅ **Correct** and **accurate** description.

---

#### **Option D**  
> *"Parties should strive to hold the increase in the global average temperature to well below 2 °C above pre-industrial levels and increase the ability to adapt to the adverse impacts of climate change."*

- This is an **exact recapitulation** of **Article 2, paragraph 1**, which clearly sets out the **long-term goal** of the Paris Agreement.  
- ✅ **Correct** and **accurate** description.
