This determination was based on a detailed analysis of the interpretation of the term "commercial scale," which the Panel found to be **relatively** and **contextually** defined. - Specifies that **"wilful trademark counterfeiting or copyright piracy on a commercial scale"** must be subject to **criminal procedures and penalties**.
- Leaves of **flexibility** for members to define and apply thresholds, so long as they align with the **minimum standards** of the the Agreement. 

The Panel also highlighted that the **burden of proof** on the US was not met, and that **China’s criminal thresholds** did not conclusively demonstrate the **inconsistency** required to trigger a finding of a violation under Article 61.
