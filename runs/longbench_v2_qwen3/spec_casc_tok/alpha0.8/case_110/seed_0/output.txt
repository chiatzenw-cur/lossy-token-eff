From **Article 4** of the Agreement:

- **Paragraph 2** says that each Party must **prepare, communicate, and maintain successive NDCs**.
- **Paragraph 8** adds that these NDCs should be **communicated every five years in accordance with decision 1/CP.21** and **any relevant decisions of the Conference of the Parties serving as the meeting of the Parties to this Agreement**.
- **Paragraph 13** outlines the **transparency framework** and firm requirements for **accurate reporting** and **accounting for emissions** in line with the **modality and guidelines** developed by the COP.
